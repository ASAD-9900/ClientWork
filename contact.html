Clear & Transparent Process

What Happens After You Enquire?

A straightforward, professional path from initial enquiry to project delivery.

01

Enquiry & Review

Tell us about your property, scope of work, approximate budget, and timeline. Our studio team reviews your brief within 24 hours.

02

Initial Discussion

We arrange a phone, video, or studio conversation with Rachana Gupta and our senior designers to discuss your aspirations and lifestyle goals, or browse our portfolio to see our recent projects.

03

Scope & Services

We evaluate spatial layout requirements, regulatory/planning considerations (including Listed Buildings), and advise on the appropriate design service.

04

Transparent Proposal

You receive a detailed proposal covering phased design fees, estimated schedules, and turnkey construction options. For room-by-room design, you can also review our structured interior design packages.
