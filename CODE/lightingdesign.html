What Is Layered Lighting?

One Room Always Needs More Than One Type of Light

Relying on a grid of ceiling downlights produces a sterile, flat environment. A sophisticated lighting scheme interweaves four distinct layers to create depth, warmth, and visual interest.

01 General

Ambient Lighting

Soft, indirect illumination bouncing from ceilings, coffer details, and wall washes to establish the overall room baseline.

02 Precision

Task Lighting

Focused, shadow-free illumination for kitchen prep, reading in bed, study desks, and dressing room vanity mirrors.

03 Focus

Accent Lighting

Narrow-beam spotlights and concealed LED strips highlighting art, niches, marble veining, and bespoke joinery shelving.

04 Sculptural

Decorative Lighting

Statement chandeliers, artisan glass pendants, and sculptural table lamps that serve as focal artworks within the room.

What Does an Architectural Lighting Plan Look Like?

Technical CAD Documentation Your Electrician Can Build From

We don't provide vague sketches. Our technical lighting packages provide exact dimensions, beam spreads, circuit assignments, and luminaire schedules for clear on-site execution.

1. Dimensioned Reflected Ceiling Plans (RCP)

Overlaying lighting points directly onto approved furniture plans, with exact centreline measurements from walls, islands, and joinery.

2. Circuitry, Switching & Dimming Schedules

Detailed coding connecting each light fixture to its specific switch gang, dimmer module, and automated control channel.

3. Luminaire Specification & Driver Schedules

Product codes, cut-out sizes, beam angles (e.g. 15° spot vs 40° flood), colour temperatures, wattage, and remote driver locations.

Frequently Asked

Lighting Design Questions

Key questions answered about architectural lighting design and electrical coordination in London.

London FAQ
[accordion] [accordion-item title="Why should I hire a lighting designer instead of letting my electrician decide?"]

Electricians focus on wiring safety and circuit loading; they typically install a symmetrical grid of ceiling lights that creates clinical, unflattering glare. An interior lighting designer coordinates fittings around furniture positions, joinery, artwork, and atmosphere, creating a layered, dimmable scheme.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="At what stage of a project should lighting design begin?"]

Lighting design must be developed after the furniture floor plan is approved, but BEFORE first-fix electrical cables are pulled. Once walls and ceilings are drylined and plastered, adding or moving lighting points becomes disruptive and costly.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="What drawings will my electrician receive?"]

Your contractor will receive dimensioned Reflected Ceiling Plans (RCP), switching and circuit schedules, wall sconce height diagrams, joinery LED routing details, and luminaire specification sheets with cut-out sizes and driver locations.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you help choose decorative chandeliers and pendants?"]

Yes. We curate and supply statement decorative pendants, chandeliers, wall sconces, and floor lamps from premier UK and European lighting houses, coordinating scale, drop heights, and trade discounts.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you provide lighting design for commercial spaces?"]

Yes. Oraanj designs lighting schemes for boutique offices, restaurants, bars, cafés, and retail stores across London, engineering high-impact lighting scenes that comply with commercial lux-level standards.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj handle the physical installation too?"]

Yes. If you appoint us for Design & Build or Turnkey refurbishment, our certified electricians and construction team deliver the full installation, testing, and scene programming under one project contract.
