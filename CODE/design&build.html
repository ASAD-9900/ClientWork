Design First. Build From an Agreed Plan.

What Does Design & Build Mean?

At Oraanj, Design & Build doesn't mean starting construction and making design decisions as the project progresses.

We develop the interior first. Layouts, lighting, materials, joinery and other important design decisions are considered before the main renovation begins.

The approved design can then be developed into the information required for implementation. This creates a clearer basis for costing, procurement and the works on site.

London Architecture & Regulations

London Properties Need More Than a Good CGI

The Property Itself Can Shape the Project

London renovation projects involve practical considerations that aren't visible in a finished render.

Apartments

Building-management rules, lift access, parking, delivery hours, and noisy-working restrictions that influence the site programme.

Leasehold Properties

Certain internal alterations require formal licence to alter approvals, structural reviews, and landlord or freeholder consents.

Period Properties

Preserving architectural character, uneven historic structures, and building fabric that affect how new joinery and services integrate.

Listed Properties

Navigating statutory listed building consents, conservation officers, and heritage restrictions before undertaking physical changes.

Cost Transparency

What Does a Design & Build Project Cost?

Design Fees and Build Costs Are Different

There isn't one meaningful fixed price for a Design & Build project without understanding the premises.

A cosmetic refurbishment has a very different cost from a complete property involving structural changes, new kitchens, luxury bathrooms, bespoke joinery, and full services overhaul.

The design stage defines what is actually being built. Once the design and scope are sufficiently developed, the works can be priced on a realistic, transparent basis.

Programme Management

How Long Does Design & Build Take?

Work Backwards From When You Need the Property

The programme depends on the property and scope.

A complete Design & Build project progresses through design, technical development, approvals, costing, procurement, renovation, bespoke manufacturing, installation, and handover.

Some items—particularly bespoke joinery, specialist furniture, stone, and imported finishes—have longer lead times and should be considered early. If you have a target completion or move-in date, tell us at the beginning so the wider programme can be structured around it.
