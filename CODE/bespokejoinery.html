Why Choose Bespoke?

When Standard Furniture Doesn't Solve the Problem

Off-the-shelf furniture is made to standard dimensions. Your property isn't.

An awkward alcove, sloping ceiling, period wall, narrow bedroom or unusually shaped room can leave valuable space unused. Bespoke joinery allows the furniture to respond directly to the property's architecture rather than forcing compromises.

Media Walls & TV Units

Plan the Technology Before Designing the Furniture

A good media wall considers what is happening behind the finished surface as rigorously as what is visible in front.

Before specifying veneers or stone hearths, we map out television dimensions, sightlines, soundbars, subwoofers, gaming consoles, set-top boxes, ventilation airflow, and invisible conduit paths for cabling.

The result can range from an understated floating media credenza to a dramatic full-height feature wall combining acoustic timber slats, marble-effect backdrops, concealed push-to-open storage and display niches.

How Much Does Bespoke Joinery Cost in London?

The Price Depends on What Is Being Made

There isn't one universal price for bespoke joinery because every commission is individually engineered.

A single painted alcove cabinet with simple shelving differs considerably from a walk-in dressing suite with internal micro-LED lighting, solid walnut drawer boxes, smoked glass doors and suede-lined jewellery trays.

Key cost drivers include overall dimensions, number of moving drawers and doors, timber veneer species, specialist lacquer finishes, hardware grade, integrated lighting, and site installation complexity.

Bespoke Joinery as Part of a Renovation

Plan It Before the Walls Are Finished

Joinery connects directly with electrical, lighting, plumbing and plastering work.

A media wall requires data cables and concealed sockets. A fitted wardrobe needs first-fix wiring for internal LED sensors. Built-in cabinetry must interface cleanly with skirting boards, floor finishes, architraves and ceiling cornices.

Designing joinery early in a renovation ensures all electrical and structural prep work is completed cleanly before decorating, avoiding costly alterations later.

First-fix wiring aligned with cabinetry

Expert Guidance
[accordion] [accordion-item title="What is bespoke joinery?"]

Bespoke joinery is furniture or cabinetry designed and manufactured specifically for a particular space and client requirements, rather than bought in standard modular dimensions. It allows full floor-to-ceiling utilisation and precise accommodation of architectural quirks.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="What types of bespoke joinery can Oraanj design?"]

Our projects encompass fitted wardrobes, walk-in dressing rooms, living room media walls, alcove units, bespoke home offices, library shelving, concealed storage, architectural wall panelling, and bespoke commercial counters and reception desks.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you design the inside of my wardrobe as well?"]

Yes. Internal storage planning is an integral part of our wardrobe and dressing room design. We measure hanging requirements, folded items, accessories, shoe collections and luggage before determining the internal partition layout.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can lighting be integrated into bespoke joinery?"]

Yes. Concealed LED profile channels, sensor-activated shelf lighting and integrated warm ambient illumination are planned during the initial design so wiring can be installed cleanly before the joinery is fitted.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj design a media wall around my television?"]

Yes. We engineer the furniture around your specific television screen, soundbars, subwoofers, consoles and decoders, ensuring ventilation airflow and completely invisible cable conduits.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you design a fitted home office?"]

Yes. We design ergonomic desks, power and data connectivity, monitor mounting, printer drawers, filing systems and closed storage, including slide-away pocket doors for multi-purpose rooms.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj design the joinery if I already have my own carpenter?"]

Yes. We can provide full architectural joinery packages including elevations, section drawings, hardware schedules and material specifications for your own cabinet makers or main building contractor to build.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you arrange fabrication and installation?"]

Yes. Where clients prefer a complete turnkey service, Oraanj coordinates fabrication with trusted specialist joinery workshops and manages delivery and professional on-site installation.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="When should bespoke joinery be designed during a renovation?"]

Ideally during the spatial planning phase before first-fix electrical, plumbing and plastering work is completed. This ensures wiring, switches and structural supports are positioned in exact alignment with the joinery.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How do I start a bespoke joinery project?"]

Send us photographs of the space, approximate room dimensions or floor plans, and a brief description of what you would like to store or create. We will review the details and arrange an initial design consultation.
