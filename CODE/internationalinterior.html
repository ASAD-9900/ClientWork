Common Enquiries

International Client Questions

Clear Answers for Property Owners Overseas

Do I need to visit London in person during the project?

No. Many of our international clients complete their entire project without travelling to London. We present designs via video calls, courier physical material samples, provide regular video site progress walkthroughs, and prepare the property for your arrival.

How do you handle different time zones?

We schedule calls around your availability, whether you are based in the Middle East, Asia, the Americas, or Australasia. We use structured messaging channels and recorded video presentations so you can review work at a time that suits you.

Can you work with my London estate agent or building management?

Yes. We frequently liaise with managing agents, concierges, freeholders, and building management companies to arrange access, manage key collections, ensure compliance with house rules, and obtain Licences to Alter (LtA).

Can you manage the physical renovation works, or only the design?

We offer both options. We can provide full turnkey design & build where we manage contractors, joinery fabrication, and delivery directly, or we can produce the design and technical drawing pack for your own contractor to build from.

How are payments and procurement managed internationally?

Payments are structured in clear, agreed milestone stages. Procurement is tracked in detailed schedules with transparent trade discounts. We coordinate all logistics, UK import duties, and consolidated site deliveries.
