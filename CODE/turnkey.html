What Does Turnkey Interior Design Actually Mean?

Beyond Drawings. We Take Responsibility for the Finished Property.

Many interior design studios stop at the design stage. They provide mood boards, floor plans and a list of items for you to buy and manage.

Turnkey interior design is different. It means the studio takes responsibility not just for what the property looks like on paper, but for making it exist in the physical space.

At Oraanj, our turnkey service brings together everything needed to take a property from its starting condition to the day you walk through the door:

  • Space Planning & Layout: Determining how every room connects and functions
  • Interior Architecture & Technical Drawings: MEP, lighting, small power and joinery elevations
  • Procurement & Purchasing: Trade ordering, warehouse consolidation, and quality checks
  • Renovation & Construction: Managed by our integrated Design & Build delivery team
  • White-Glove Installation: Final placement, window treatments, and professional styling

Furniture & Spatial Planning Starts With the Floor Plan

Furniture Doesn't Come at the End — It Drives the Architecture

A common mistake in interior projects is leaving furniture until the renovation is finished. By then, socket positions, pendant drops, wall switches, and radiator locations are already fixed in the wrong places.

At Oraanj, furniture planning happens on day one alongside the floor plan:

  • Circulation & Proportions: Ensuring dining tables, sofas, and consoles fit comfortably without blocking walkways
  • Electrical Alignment: Floor sockets positioned precisely under sofas, bedside reading lights wired to exact headboard heights
  • Architectural Lighting: Ceiling downlights, chandeliers, and joinery LED strips coordinated with key seating zones
  • Procurement Timelines: Bespoke sofas and Italian cabinetry have 8–14 week lead times; ordering early prevents move-in delays

What Does Turnkey Interior Design Cost in London?

Transparent Pricing Built on a Defined Scope

Because a turnkey project covers multiple stages — design, construction, bespoke joinery, and furniture — costs vary depending on property size, condition, and specification level.

The investment divides logically into three components:

•
1. Interior Design & Technical Planning: Fixed design fee based on property scope, room count, and technical drawing complexity.
•
2. Renovation & Construction: Itemised quotation based on the approved design, engineering, and material specifications.
•
3. FF&E, Joinery & Procurement: Trade-discounted pricing for furniture, bespoke joinery, lighting, and finishes with complete cost transparency.

We provide itemised, transparent proposals before works commence so you maintain complete financial control.

London FAQs
[accordion] [accordion-item title="What is turnkey interior design?"]

Turnkey interior design is a comprehensive service where the studio manages the entire project from initial concept through space planning, technical design, procurement, renovation, joinery installation, furniture delivery, and final styling. You receive a fully finished, move-in-ready property.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="What is the difference between turnkey and Design & Build?"]

Design & Build covers the design and the physical construction work. Turnkey includes Design & Build, plus all furniture procurement, bespoke joinery, window dressings, decorative lighting, artwork, accessories, and white-glove setup.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can I live in the property during a turnkey project?"]

For whole-home turnkey projects involving renovation, the property is usually vacant during works. For cosmetic furnishing or single-room projects, occupancy can be accommodated depending on the scope.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you manage my property if I live abroad?"]

Yes. A substantial portion of our turnkey clients are overseas property owners. We provide complete remote project management, digital walkthroughs, sample deliveries, and weekly progress updates.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How long does a turnkey project take?"]

A full-home turnkey project typically takes 3 to 6 months depending on property size, extent of structural alterations, and joinery lead times. We establish a clear programme before starting.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can I keep some of my existing furniture?"]

Yes. We regularly integrate cherished art, antiques, and existing furniture pieces into the new layout and design narrative.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you provide turnkey services for commercial properties?"]

Yes. Oraanj undertakes turnkey interiors for offices, boutique hospitality, restaurants, and retail spaces across London.
