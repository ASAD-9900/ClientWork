Pre-Move Planning

Just Bought a Property? Start Before You Move In

A vacant property offers the ideal opportunity to complete major works before day-to-day life and furniture are introduced.

Critical decisions we establish before works start:

  • Optimal Space Planning: Internal layouts, circulation, and room usage.
  • Renovation Scope: Kitchen and bathroom alterations, wall removals, or re-plastering.
  • M&E Infrastructure: Architectural lighting, electrical sockets, and smart technology.
  • Bespoke Joinery: Fitted wardrobes, media units, and integrated storage.
  • Flooring & Finishes: Wood floors, carpets, stone, and paint schedules.
  • Long-Lead Procurement: Sourcing custom furniture and fabrics early to align with move-in.
  • Target Handover Date: Establishing a fixed date for key collection and arrival.

Planning these stages together helps the design, building work, and furnishing programmes align toward a single fixed handover milestone.

Spatial Harmony

Why Furniture Planning Starts With the Floor Plan

The Interior Needs to Work Before Anything Is Ordered

A sofa isn't simply a product selection. Its dimensions dictate how the room functions.

The size and orientation of key furniture directly affects circulation routes, side table placement, socket positions, floor-box locations, rug scale, lighting points, and sightlines to media joinery.

The same architectural logic applies to dining tables, beds, and desks. That is why we plan furniture alongside space planning and electrical grids rather than treating furnishing as an afterthought.
