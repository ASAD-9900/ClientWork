Spatial Planning

Furniture & Spatial Planning Starts With the Floor Plan

Furniture Doesn't Come at the End — It Drives the Architecture

A common mistake in interior projects is leaving furniture until the renovation is finished. By then, socket positions, pendant drops, wall switches, and radiator locations are already fixed in the wrong places.

01 Proportions

Circulation & Scale

Ensuring dining tables, seating groups, and consoles fit comfortably without blocking walkways or natural sightlines.

02 First-Fix

Electrical Alignment

Floor sockets positioned precisely under sofas; bedside switches and reading sconces wired to exact headboard dimensions.

03 Atmosphere

Architectural Lighting

Ceiling downlights, statement pendants, and joinery LED washes coordinated with key seating zones and artwork locations.

04 Sourcing

Lead-Time Protection

Bespoke European sofas and Italian cabinetry have 8–14 week lead times; ordering early prevents move-in delays.

What Does Turnkey Interior Design Cost in London?

Transparent Pricing Built on a Defined Scope

Because a turnkey project covers multiple stages — design, construction, bespoke joinery, and furniture — costs vary depending on property size, condition, and specification level.

The investment divides logically into three components:

1. Interior Design & Technical Planning

A transparent fixed design fee based on property footprint and room count, covering space planning, 3D CGI visuals, and complete technical drawing packages.

2. Renovation & Construction

A fully itemised, transparent quotation based on the approved architectural specifications, structural engineering, MEP plans, and material schedules.

3. FF&E, Joinery & Procurement

Trade-discounted pricing for bespoke fitted joinery, designer loose furniture, window treatments, and finishes with full line-item visibility and cost control.

We provide itemised, transparent proposals before works commence so you maintain complete financial control.
