Designed Around Your Business

A Commercial Interior Needs to Do More Than Look Good

It needs to support the way the business actually operates on a daily basis.

Before developing the design, we look at how customers enter and move through the space, how employees work, what needs to be stored, where different activities take place and what impression the environment should create.

For a restaurant, that might mean balancing customer seating with circulation and service. For an office, it could mean creating the right mix of focused work, meetings and collaboration. For a customer-facing business, the entrance, reception and overall experience may be particularly important.

Understanding these requirements first allows the design to respond to the business rather than simply decorating the premises.

•Staff workflow & circulation
•Client reception & first impression
•Ergonomic furniture & workstation layout
•Acoustics & meeting privacy

Budgets & Scope

How Much Does Commercial Interior Design Cost in London?

Commercial fees reflect the floor area, industry requirements, and depth of technical delivery required.

A compact office requiring space planning and interior styling involves a different scope from a full restaurant, clinic, or multi-floor workplace requiring detailed mechanical coordination, bespoke joinery production, and turnkey fit-out management.

The existing condition of the premises (shell unit vs. functioning second-generation space) also dictates whether structural, ventilation, or landlord approvals are needed.

We recommend discussing your premises early so we can provide a transparent proposal aligned with your commercial timeline and budget.

Programme Planning

How Long Does a Commercial Project Take?

The Programme Depends on the Size and Complexity of the Premises

Commercial moves and fit-outs often carry fixed deadlines driven by lease dates, break clauses, or target business openings.

The design stage typically encompasses site survey, space planning, concept iterations, approvals, technical documentation, and contractor procurement before any site works begin.

Where physical construction is required, the overall programme also coordinates landlord licences to alter (LTA), Building Control inspections, specialist joinery fabrication lead times, and furniture delivery.

If you have a fixed target opening, lease expiry, or relocation milestone, tell us at the beginning so we can establish a realistic critical path.
