Design & Delivery

Landscape Design vs. Landscaping & Our Process

Work With Your Own Landscaper or Coordinate Within an Oraanj Project

Landscape & garden design establishes what the outdoor space should look like and how it should work; landscaping is the physical groundwork, paving, construction, and planting.

1. Already Have a Landscaper?

Design and physical installation can be separate. We can develop the outdoor design for implementation by your chosen landscape contractor, or coordinate outdoor works within a wider Oraanj renovation scope.

2. Specialist Technical & Horticultural Input

Where structural loading (such as roof terraces), drainage, waterproofing, electrical installation, or detailed horticultural specifications are required, these are coordinated with the appropriate qualified specialists.

3. Clear Step-by-Step Design Process

From reviewing your existing garden plans, photos, and priorities to establishing the layout, developing materials and furniture, visualising in 3D, and refining the final design.
