Project Delivery

Refurbishing in Occupation vs. Full Turnkey

Minimising Room Downtime and Protecting Ongoing Revenue

Hotels cannot afford prolonged closures. We plan phased floor-by-floor refurbishments with strict acoustic barriers, dedicated service lift access, and zero guest disruption.

1. Phased Floor-by-Floor Delivery

Restricting noisy contractor activities to daytime checkout windows (10:00–16:00) with sealed dust partitions, keeping lower floors fully bookable.

2. Complete Turnkey Hospitality Delivery

A single Design & Build contract encompassing demolition, MEP alterations, acoustic ceiling systems, en-suite tiling, and joinery installation.

3. Dedicated Hospitality Site Supervision

On-site construction managers enforcing strict hotel security protocols, room-by-room quality checklists, and fast-track handover snags.
