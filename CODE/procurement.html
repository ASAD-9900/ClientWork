Clear Distinction

Sourcing vs. Procurement

Two Related Disciplines That Solve Different Problems

Clients often confuse sourcing with procurement. Understanding the distinction ensures your project is resourced effectively from design to installation.

1. Interior Sourcing: "What Should We Buy?"

The creative curation phase where designers identify suitable furniture pieces, fabrics, and finishes that fit your aesthetic, room proportions, and budget.

2. FF&E Procurement: "How Do We Get It Delivered?"

The commercial operations phase managing purchase orders, factory deposits, trade discounts, freight shipping, customs clearance, and delivery schedules.

3. Integrated Sourcing & Procurement Management

Combining both services guarantees that what is specified by the design team is procured accurately, on time, and within agreed budgets.
