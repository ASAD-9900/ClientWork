Knightsbridge Residence

A refined contemporary home shaped by quiet luxury and considered detail

This sophisticated Knightsbridge residence was designed to create a calm and elegant home for modern family living. A restrained palette of taupe, warm timber and muted olive tones brings softness throughout the interiors, while carefully selected furnishings and statement lighting add depth, character and a sense of understated luxury.

Bespoke joinery, layered lighting and richly textured finishes create continuity from the living and dining spaces through to the kitchen, bedrooms and bathroom. Sculptural furniture, marble surfaces and subtle brass accents complete the scheme, balancing refined detailing with a welcoming and comfortable atmosphere.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Layered Luxury Designed for Contemporary Family Living

Every space within the Knightsbridge residence was considered as part of a cohesive interior, combining refined detailing with the comfort and practicality required for everyday family life.

From the sculptural lighting and bespoke wall panelling to the carefully integrated joinery and generous marble surfaces, each element contributes to a calm and sophisticated atmosphere. Warm timber, tactile fabrics, brushed brass accents and muted olive tones bring depth to the neutral palette, while carefully framed artwork and decorative objects add personality throughout.

01 Principal Suite

Bespoke Joinery

02 Refined Living Spaces

Art Deco Heritage

03 Timeless Materiality

Natural Finishes
