Notting Hill Residence

an Art Deco family home reimagined with
timeless elegance

This beautifully renovated 1940s Art Deco family home in Notting Hill was designed for a young family, balancing timeless interiors with the practicalities of everyday living. Rich in colour, pattern and natural materials, the design celebrates craftsmanship and considered detailing, creating spaces that feel warm, inviting and made to stand the test of time.

A layered palette flows throughout the home, with bespoke joinery, thoughtful marble detailing and handcrafted finishes creating depth and character. The children’s bedrooms continue the home’s timeless aesthetic, allowing them to adapt naturally as the family grows.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Artisan Detailing Tailored to Modern Family Life

Beyond the architectural flow, every room in the Notting Hill home balances tactile refinement with the functional practicalities of busy family routines.

From the principal ensuite featuring custom twin arched mirrors and handcrafted zellige tiling to the bespoke kitchen cabinetry and integrated reading nooks, each element was selected to age gracefully. Layered architectural and accent lighting emphasizes the bespoke fluted joinery, soft natural stone surfaces, and rich tactile textiles throughout.

01 Principal Suite

Ensuite & Millwork

Bespoke twin arched mirrors, honed marble vanities, antique brass sanitaryware, and floor-to-ceiling joinery tailored for effortless storage.

02 Living Spaces

Art Deco Heritage

Subtle archways and restored period proportions paired with tailored seating, a focal fireplace, and display cabinetry.

03 Materiality

Natural Finishes

Tactile linen wallcoverings, hand-finished oak parquet, fluted glass, and warm organic textures crafted to stand the test of time.
