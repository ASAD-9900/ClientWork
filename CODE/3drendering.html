What Is a 3D Interior Render?

A Photorealistic Digital Model of Your Future Home

A 2D plan indicates dimensions; a 3D render allows you to experience the atmosphere, light, and materiality of the finished room before spending on trades.

Spatial Proportions

Our Technical Pipeline

From CAD Geometry to Photorealism

A Disciplined CGI Pipeline Based on Real Architecture

Our renders are built upon verified architectural plans, accurate window daylight orientations, and physically based rendering (PBR) shaders.

01 Geometry

3D Architectural Modelling

Extruding precise room geometry from 2D CAD floorplans, including door reveals, skirting details, and ceiling coffers.

02 Lighting

Physically Based Lighting

Setting true geographical sun angles and calibrating Kelvin temperatures for downlights, sconces, and LED coves.

03 Textures

PBR Material Shaders

Applying high-resolution maps for wood grain bump, marble gloss, fabric weave, and brushed metal reflectivity.

04 Output

Ultra HD Rendering

Generating crisp 4K-resolution presentation images with post-production color balance and camera depth-of-field.

3D FAQ
[accordion] [accordion-item title="Can you create 3D renders for just a single room?"]

Yes. We frequently produce 3D visualisations for individual rooms—such as a kitchen, open-plan living room, master en-suite, or bespoke dressing suite—as well as complete residential and commercial properties.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How long does it take to produce 3D interior renders?"]

Standard visualisations for a single room typically take 5 to 10 working days from receipt of dimensions and design brief. Full multi-room properties or complex commercial spaces are scheduled around your project programme.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Are revisions included in the 3D rendering service?"]

Yes. We include formal review stages to allow you to request adjustments to finishes, paint colours, lighting accents, or furniture selections before final high-resolution images are generated.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you work from my architect's CAD drawings?"]

Yes. We can import DWG, DXF, or PDF drawings from your architect, structural engineer, or developer to build precise 3D geometry reflecting the exact building footprint.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you help me choose the furniture and materials in the render?"]

Yes. If you haven't finalised selections, our interior design team can curate furniture pieces, bespoke joinery concepts, colour schemes, and lighting fixtures directly for the 3D model.

Yes. Beyond 3D visualisation, Oraanj provides comprehensive interior design, trade procurement, bespoke joinery manufacture, and complete Design & Build renovation across London.
