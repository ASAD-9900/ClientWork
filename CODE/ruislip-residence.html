Turnkey Design & Build Architecture

A distinguished residential transformation uniting structural precision, bespoke joinery and refined contemporary elegance

Commissioned as a comprehensive design and build transformation in Ruislip, this substantial family home underwent a complete architectural reconfiguration, structural extension, and interior refurbishment. Delivered under Oraanj's single-point-of-accountability turnkey model, the project seamlessly merged architectural planning and structural alterations with high-end interior detailing.

Delivered by our dedicated team of interior designers in Ruislip, every stage from spatial planning and council compliance to final on-site installation was orchestrated with meticulous precision.

A cohesive material language spans the residence, combining honed Calacatta marble, bespoke American walnut and reeded oak joinery, fluted architectural wall panelling, brushed warm brass metalwork, and tailored illumination. Spaces flow effortlessly from the breathtaking double-height entrance foyer into expansive reception areas, a bespoke culinary kitchen with waterfall island, formal dining suite, and private parental sanctuaries.

Building upon our renowned work on the Northwood residence case study, this Ruislip home exemplifies the harmony between structural integrity and luxurious livability.

Turnkey Craftsmanship & Precision

Holistic Architectural Reconfiguration & Bespoke Interior Fit-Out

Every architectural element was designed and delivered to balance grand residential proportions with seamless modern family living.

From initial structural modifications and MEP infrastructure to custom joinery installation and artisanal finishes, our turnkey delivery removed all intermediary friction. Bespoke cabinetry throughout the reception rooms, study, and kitchen was precision-crafted to integrate hidden storage, concealed smart-home wiring, and architectural perimeter illumination.

The culinary zone features book-matched marble surfaces with a monolithic waterfall island, complemented by luxury appliance suites. The master bedroom and ensuite retreat showcase fluted wall panels, walk-in wet room showers with bespoke brassware, and a freestanding sculptural bathtub positioned under soft natural light.
