Whole-Home Interior Architecture

Whole-Home Interior Design

One Home, Rather Than a Collection of Separate Rooms

Designing a complete property requires a different approach from decorating individual rooms in isolation.

Flooring moves from one area into another. Lighting needs to work across the property. Joinery, doors and architectural details repeat. Colours need to connect without every room looking identical.

We develop the home as one overall design while considering how each individual room needs to function. This is particularly valuable when renovating a newly purchased property or making significant changes across an existing family home.

Designing London Homes

Understanding the Property Matters

London homes present distinct architectural opportunities and structural constraints:

Period Houses & Townhouses

Victorian, Georgian and Edwardian properties have generous proportions and period cornicing, but often require modernised flow, expanded kitchens, and improved natural light.

Leasehold Apartments & Penthouses

Navigating licences to alter, strict acoustic ratings, service ducting, and building-management delivery logistics without compromising design ambition.

Family Residences

Designing robust, elegant homes that balance shared family living and entertaining with dedicated home working and children's study zones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Oraanj on your London residential interior.

[accordion] [accordion-item title="What does a residential interior designer do?"]

A residential interior designer plans and develops the interior of a home around the people living there. This can include space planning, layouts, colours, materials, furniture, lighting, bespoke joinery and technical design depending on the project.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How much does residential interior design cost in London?"]

The cost depends on property size, number of rooms and level of service required. Individual rooms start with fixed packages from £600 to £1,500, while whole-home interior design is quoted based on exact property dimensions and technical requirements.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj design just one room?"]

Yes. Residential projects range from individual living rooms or master bedroom suites to complete lateral apartments and multi-storey townhouses.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you design flats and apartments?"]

Yes. We design London apartments as well as houses, period properties, family homes and larger residences, working closely with building management and licence to alter rules.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you work with furniture I already own?"]

Yes. Existing furniture, heirloom pieces or artwork can be incorporated into the new design where you would like to retain them.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you design bespoke wardrobes and storage?"]

Yes. Bespoke joinery forms a central part of our service, including fitted wardrobes, media walls, home offices, and concealed storage designed around your exact belongings.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you design kitchens and bathrooms?"]

Yes. Kitchens and bathrooms can be designed individually or as part of a wider residential interior project, with full technical drawings for plumbing and electrics.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do I need an interior designer before renovating?"]

For substantial renovations, appointing a designer before works begin ensures layouts, lighting, materials, and joinery are properly resolved before contractors tender, preventing costly on-site variations.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj manage the renovation as well?"]

Yes. Clients who want complete execution can take advantage of our integrated Design & Build and Turnkey delivery services.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Which areas of London do you work in?"]

We work across all prime London boroughs including Kensington & Chelsea, Westminster, Camden, Islington, Richmond, Wandsworth, and Greater London.
