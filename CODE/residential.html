Whole-Home Interior Architecture

Whole-Home Interior Design

One Home, Rather Than a Collection of Separate Rooms

Designing a complete property requires a different approach from decorating individual rooms in isolation.

Flooring moves from one area into another. Lighting needs to work across the property. Joinery, doors and architectural details repeat. Colours need to connect without every room looking identical.

We develop the home as one overall design while considering how each individual room needs to function. This is particularly valuable when renovating a newly purchased property or making significant changes across an existing family home.

Designing London Homes

Understanding the Property Matters

London homes present distinct architectural opportunities and structural constraints:

Period Houses & Townhouses

Victorian, Georgian and Edwardian properties have generous proportions and period cornicing, but often require modernised flow, expanded kitchens, and improved natural light.

Leasehold Apartments & Penthouses

Navigating licences to alter, strict acoustic ratings, service ducting, and building-management delivery logistics without compromising design ambition.

Family Residences

Designing robust, elegant homes that balance shared family living and entertaining with dedicated home working and children's study zones.
