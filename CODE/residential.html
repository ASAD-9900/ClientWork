Lifestyle-Led Interior Architecture

Homes Designed Around the Way You Live

A truly successful home interior is not simply about selecting attractive colours or stylish furniture — it is about how the space feels and performs in your day-to-day life.

We examine the practical connections of daily living before specifying a single piece of furniture. How you move from bedroom to dressing area in the morning, how kitchen and dining zones interact when entertaining, where family storage naturally accumulates, and how lighting changes your atmosphere as evening arrives.

Every element is deliberate: bespoke joinery that makes optimal use of alcoves, ergonomic spatial circulation, tactile materials that age gracefully, and lighting circuits designed for both practical tasks and warm evening relaxation.

  • Morning Routines: Effortless spatial flow between principal suites, dressing areas, and en-suite bathrooms.
  • Entertaining & Dining: Social kitchen islands and dining spaces configured for intimate dinners and lively gatherings.
  • Work & Retreat: Focused home study environments and comfortable, calm reading corners.
  • Architectural Lighting: Layered ambient, task, and accent lighting scenes controlled intuitively.
  • Concealed Storage: Made-to-measure cabinetry seamlessly integrated into the room architecture.

Holistic Architectural Flow

Whole-Home Interior Design

Designing a home is not about decorating isolated rooms in isolation. It is about creating a continuous, balanced architectural dialogue from the front door through every private space.

When rooms are designed independently, homes often end up feeling fragmented — with jarring floor finishes, inconsistent ironmongery, clashing skirting profiles, and discordant colour temperatures.

With Oraanj's whole-home interior design, your property benefits from a singular, master-planned creative direction. Timber flooring transitions smoothly from entrance hall to living quarters, bespoke joinery shares consistent edge details and hardware finishes, and the lighting temperature remains perfectly harmonious across day and night.

  • Unified materiality, consistent architectural ironmongery, and harmonious wood stains.
  • Balanced investment prioritisation across public reception rooms and private bedrooms.
  • Seamless multi-room procurement that reduces freight, staging, and installation costs.

London Heritage & Regulations

Designing London Homes

London properties present unique architectural and regulatory conditions that require seasoned local expertise.

Whether working on a Grade II listed Georgian townhouse in Bloomsbury, a Victorian terrace in Wandsworth, an Edwardian family home in Dulwich, or a contemporary apartment in Canary Wharf, we understand how to work alongside local authority conservation teams, estate management companies, and freeholder license-to-alter requirements.

We navigate structural party wall notices, listed building consents, acoustic regulations in apartment leases, and complex ceiling voids for MEP routing — ensuring your design is not only visually stunning but completely compliant.

  • Conservation Areas & Listed Buildings: Sensitive detailing that respects original heritage fabric while enhancing modern comfort.
  • License to Alter & Freeholder Approvals: Complete technical drawing packages prepared for managing agents and landlord surveyors.
  • Space & Light Optimisation: Clever architectural interventions that introduce daylight deep into narrow London terraced footprints.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Our Residential Interior Design Service

How does the residential interior design process begin?

We start with an initial consultation — either in person at your London property or via video call. We discuss how you live in the home, your aesthetic preferences, functional priorities, budget parameters, and any key architectural features. Following this, we issue a clear scope proposal with fixed design fees before commencing work.

Can you work on just one room, or only whole homes?

We offer both single-room design packages and whole-property commissions. Whether you need a living room redesign, a new kitchen and dining layout, a master bedroom retreat, or a four-storey townhouse transformation, our packages scale to meet your exact requirements.

What is the difference between One Design, Unlimited Design, and Design & Technical?

One Design (£600/room) provides one complete layout and concept scheme with shopping links, ideal for straightforward room refreshes. Unlimited Design (£900/room) includes photorealistic 3D renders and unlimited revisions until you are completely satisfied. Design & Technical (£1,500/room) adds contractor-ready technical drawings, joinery elevations, electrical plans, and plumbing specifications for building works.

Do you pass on trade discounts on furniture and materials?

Yes. As an SBID-accredited practice, Oraanj has established trade relationships with leading British and European furniture, fabric, stone, and lighting manufacturers. We pass on substantial trade discounts directly to our clients, which often saves them more than the cost of our design fees.

How realistic are your 3D visualisations?

Our 3D CGI visuals are photorealistic and built from exact room measurements, real window orientations, specified paint finishes, exact textile swatches, and real furniture dimensions. What you see in our renders is an accurate representation of how the finished room will look in daylight and evening lighting.

What if I already have my own builder or contractors?

That is entirely fine. With our Design & Technical package, we produce comprehensive, millimetre-accurate architectural drawing sets, joinery details, and finish schedules that any professional contractor can price and build from. We can also provide periodic design coordination site visits if required.

How long does a residential interior design project take?

The initial design concept and 3D visualisations typically take 3 to 6 weeks depending on the number of rooms. Technical drawings require an additional 2 to 4 weeks. Bespoke furniture and joinery manufacturing usually requires 6 to 10 weeks from sign-off. We provide a detailed project programme at the outset.

Can Oraanj handle bespoke joinery and custom cabinetry?

Bespoke joinery is one of our core specialties. We design custom walk-in dressing rooms, media walls, banquette seating, hidden study desks, and architectural bookcases. We produce detailed manufacturing drawings and work closely with master cabinet makers to ensure flawless fit and finish.

Do you work on listed properties and conservation areas in London?

Yes. We have extensive experience working on Grade II listed residences and period properties across London's conservation boroughs. We understand the heritage guidelines, license-to-alter requirements, and the technical sensitivities of integrating modern services into historic buildings.

How do payments and design fees work?

Design fees are transparent, fixed, and agreed upfront before any work starts. They are typically split into milestones (e.g., 50% upon project commencement and 50% upon completion of design deliverables). Furniture procurement and building works are itemised separately with zero hidden markups.
