More Than Choosing Colours and Furniture

Commercial interior design has a different starting point.

Interior Design or Interior Architecture?

They Work Closely Together, but They Are Not Quite the Same

Interior Design

Focuses on how the space looks, feels and functions through layout, furniture, materials, lighting, colours and finishes.

Interior Architecture

Goes further into spatial and technical planning, such as more detailed layouts, elevations, lighting and electrical positions, bespoke joinery and technical information.

Many renovation projects require elements of both.

If you are changing the way the property is laid out or preparing information before construction, interior architecture may form part of the project.
