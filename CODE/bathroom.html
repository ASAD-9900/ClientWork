Spatial Choice

Bath or Walk-In Shower?

Start With How You Actually Use the Bathroom

Not every bathroom needs both. Squeezing a cramped bath and a tiny shower enclosure into the same room frequently compromises both experiences.

A standalone bath is invaluable for young families or those who regularly soak. However, replacing an unused bath with an expansive, low-threshold walk-in shower with twin rainfall heads often creates vastly superior everyday luxury and room circulation.

Freestanding & Fitted Baths

Carefully scaled composite stone baths with floor-mounted brassware, planned with comfortable drying zones.

Walk-In Showers & Wet Areas

Full-height frameless glass screens, linear drain channels, integrated seating benches, and flush floor level access.
