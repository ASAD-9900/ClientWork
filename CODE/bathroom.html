Bath or Walk-In Shower?

Start With How You Actually Use the Bathroom

Not every bathroom needs both. Squeezing a cramped bath and a tiny shower enclosure into the same room frequently compromises both experiences.

A standalone bath is invaluable for young families or those who regularly soak. However, replacing an unused bath with an expansive, low-threshold walk-in shower with twin rainfall heads often creates vastly superior everyday luxury and room circulation.

Bathroom Renovation Questions

Clear answers to common questions about renovating bathrooms in London houses and apartments.

[accordion] [accordion-item title="How long does a bathroom renovation take?"]

A typical London bathroom renovation takes between 2 to 4 weeks on site, depending on whether plumbing positions are altered, the tile complexity, and whether bespoke joinery is being fitted.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can I move the toilet or shower to another wall?"]

Yes, subject to the location of the soil vent pipe and waste drainage falls. In apartments, waste pipe routing through concrete slabs may require raising shower trays or installing discreet false floors.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="What is the difference between a walk-in shower and a wet room?"]

A walk-in shower typically features a low-profile shower tray with frameless glass screens. A wet room has a continuous tiled floor with an integrated gradient former, requiring the entire room floor and walls to be fully tanked.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How do you prevent bathroom mould and condensation?"]

We combine high-performance, whisper-quiet inline extraction fans vented directly externally, warm underfloor heating, anti-mould epoxy grouts, and balanced room temperature control.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj manage the entire renovation?"]

Yes. Our integrated service covers spatial design, 3D visualisations, plumbing, electrical installations, tanking, precision tiling, and final sanitaryware fitting under single-point accountability.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do I need planning permission to renovate my bathroom?"]

Internal bathroom renovations do not usually require planning permission unless your home is a Listed building or you are altering external soil stacks and vents in a conservation area.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you design bespoke vanities and storage?"]

Yes. Bespoke vanity units, mirrored shaving cabinets with internal charging sockets, and recessed niches are custom-made to suit your exact room dimensions.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="What water pressure do I need for a rainfall shower?"]

Concealed rainfall showers work best with a minimum of 2.0 to 3.0 bar water pressure. For low-pressure gravity systems, we can specify mains unvented cylinders or booster pump systems.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can I use my own builder?"]

Yes. Oraanj can be appointed for design, 3D visualisations, and technical drawing packages while you work with your own building team.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="When should I contact Oraanj?"]

Ideally before purchasing tiles or sanitaryware. Planning the layout early ensures services are positioned in the exact locations required for your chosen fixtures.
