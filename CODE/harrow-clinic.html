Healthcare & Wellness Architecture

A North West London private clinic uniting clinical precision, patient reassurance and bespoke timber joinery

Private healthcare environments must reconcile two demanding criteria: rigorous clinical compliance and an unhurried, hospitality-led patient experience. For this private medical and wellness clinic in Harrow, we designed a holistic environment that eliminates the cold sterility of traditional surgeries whilst fully satisfying CQC infection control standards.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to interior designer in Harrow, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

Arrival is defined by a sculptural curved reception desk clad in olive-green fluted timber with a non-porous honed composite stone counter. Beyond, a boutique waiting lounge offers comfortable olive velvet armchairs, rust-toned cushions, and integrated oak display shelving. Private consultation rooms feature acoustic glass partitions, concealed medical diagnostic equipment housings, and layered dimmable lighting, creating a calm atmosphere for patient consultations.

Our team delivered tailored Westminster dental clinic project, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Clinical Architecture & Joinery

Infection Control, Acoustic Privacy & Biophilic Caliber

Balancing clinical hygiene regulations with hospitality-grade materiality to ease patient anxiety.

Medical fit-outs require seamless integration of services. We concealed medical air conditioning, HEPA filtration units, and data conduits within bespoke oak cabinetry and dropped ceiling rafts. High-grade acoustic plasterboard and double-glazed acoustic glass partitions achieve 48dB sound insulation between consulting suites, ensuring complete patient confidentiality.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated commercial interior design, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Acoustics

48dB Patient Privacy

Double-glazed acoustic partitions and solid timber acoustic door cores prevent sound transfer between clinical suites.

02 Compliance

CQC-Approved Finishes

Non-porous solid surface worktops, seamless clinical flooring, and wipeable antibacterial wall coverings tailored for clinical hygiene.

03 Biophilia

Hospitality-Grade Comfort

Tactile fluted timber reception fronts, curved architectural silhouettes, and warm 2700K indirect illumination that reduce patient stress.
