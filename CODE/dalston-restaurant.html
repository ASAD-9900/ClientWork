Hospitality Architecture

A characterful East London dining venue uniting industrial heritage, tactile banquettes and intimate bar culture

Situated near Kingsland Road in Dalston, this hospitality project converted a former commercial brick unit into a vibrant neighbourhood restaurant and evening bar. The design intent was to craft an authentic East London venue that feels rooted in its creative surroundings rather than an ostentatious luxury dining room.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to interior designer in Dalston, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

Continuous fluted terracotta leather banquettes run the perimeter of the dining floor, maximising covers whilst offering relaxed comfort. Dark-stained English oak dining tables, olive-green fluted bar cabinetry, exposed brick shelving gantries, and low-slung brass wall sconces create an intimate dining atmosphere. Acoustic plaster finishes dampen ambient noise, allowing easy conversation even during peak dinner services.

Our team delivered tailored restaurant interior design London, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Hospitality Joinery & Space Planning

Cover Economics, Fluted Bar Joinery & Acoustic Comfort

Balancing table density with generous guest comfort and efficient waiter circulation routes.

The floor layout was planned to accommodate 68 covers without compromising service aisles between the front-of-house bar and rear commercial pass. Deep terracotta leather banquettes eliminate dead corner zones, while solid oak tables on weighted cast-iron bases ensure rock-solid stability. Acoustic plasterboard ceilings absorb clatter from tableware, creating a comfortable soundscape.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated Greenwich restaurant project, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Planning

68-Cover Spatial Layout

Perimeter banquettes and central 2-top tables on flexible tracks allow swift reconfiguration for parties and small groups.

02 Bar Craft

Fluted Bar Joinery

03 Lighting

Intimate 2400K Atmosphere
