Do You Have Space for an Island?

Don't Force One Into the Room

Kitchen islands are desirable, but shoehorning one into an inadequate space creates frustrating bottlenecks.

A functioning island requires at least 1,000mm to 1,200mm of clear walkway on all sides so oven doors, dishwashers and deep drawers can open freely without blocking traffic.

Where space is tight, a peninsula, L-shaped return or mobile architectural butcher's block often provides superior worktop area, barstool seating and circulation.

How Much Does a Kitchen Renovation Cost in London?

Start With the Scope, Not a Generic Price Per Metre

Two kitchens of identical square footage can require very different budgets depending on cabinetry construction, stone surfaces, appliance tiers, and structural wall removals.

Rather than guessing generic estimates, we examine your exact brief, provide itemised transparency, and guide you on where to spend for maximum everyday return.

Smart Budget Allocation

Invest in layout optimization, heavy-use cabinetry runners, durable worktops and task lighting, while economising on lower-impact decorative trims.

Lead Time Coordination

Planning lead times for bespoke cabinetry (6-10 weeks) and templated stone worktops to ensure trade handovers run without site delays.
