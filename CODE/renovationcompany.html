Design Dependencies

The Decisions That Are Expensive to Change Later

Plan Them Before Work Begins

Renovations become harder when major decisions are left until they are urgently needed on site. Planning these relationships earlier reduces avoidable redesign and costly site variations.

01

Socket Positions

These need to relate directly to furniture layout, televisions, bedside tables, desks, and equipment.

02

Lighting Positions

Lighting works best when it relates to the final layout rather than being positioned before furniture is planned.

03

Joinery

Made-to-measure furniture may affect sockets, lighting, skirting, cornices and surrounding finishes.

04

Kitchen Layout

Cabinetry, appliances, plumbing, lighting and electrical requirements all need seamless coordination.

05

Bathroom Layout

Sanitaryware positions affect plumbing falls, tiling alignments, storage cavities and task lighting.

06

Floor Finishes

Transitions between different materials, thresholds, and underfloor heating zones need consideration before screeding.

07

Furniture Dimensions

Large pieces influence circulation paths, natural daylighting and even the position of architectural doorways.

Project Logistics

Can You Live in Your Home During a Renovation?

Sometimes — but it depends heavily on the scope of work.

A focused renovation affecting one part of a property can be very different from a complete refurbishment involving kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, electrical rewiring and multiple living rooms.

Things to consider include:

Will you have a working kitchen?
Will a usable bathroom remain available?
How much dust & noise will works create?
Will power or water be interrupted?
Are trades working throughout the home?
Will flooring or decor affect circulation?

For larger renovations, temporary accommodation may be more practical. The appropriate arrangement should be considered once the actual scope and programme are understood.

Budget Allocation

Where Should the Money Go?

Not Every Part of a Renovation Needs the Same Level of Spend

A good renovation budget isn't simply divided equally between rooms. The priorities depend on the property and client, but larger parts of the budget may need to be considered around:

01 • Kitchens
High daily use, appliances, work surfaces, and specialized joinery require appropriate investment.
02 • Bathrooms
Waterproofing, high-end sanitaryware, concealed valves, and precision tiling make up core value.
03 • Bespoke Joinery
Fitted wardrobes, cabinetry and media walls designed to maximise every millimetre of London space.
04 • Lighting
Architectural dimming, LED warmth, and statement luminaires deliver the emotional mood of the home.
05 • Flooring
Quality engineered timber or stone flooring that handles wear, acoustics, and underfloor heating.
06 • Building & Services
First-fix electrics, plumbing, plastering, and structural integrity that underpin the entire home.
07 • Furniture & FF&E
Curated signature pieces and bespoke upholstery that complete the tactile luxury experience.
Guiding Principle

"The important thing is to establish priorities early. If the budget is limited, it is usually better to understand that during design development than after everything has already been specified."
