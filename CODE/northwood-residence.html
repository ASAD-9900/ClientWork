Modern-Classic Residential Design

A distinguished detached family home uniting classic architectural grandeur, bespoke walnut joinery and understated luxury

Inspired by the generous scale and leafy seclusion of Northwood premier avenues, this substantial detached residence underwent a comprehensive interior architecture and decoration commission. The brief centred on crafting a timeless, modern-classic family home balancing formal entertaining with comfortable everyday life.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to interior designer in Northwood, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

A refined palette of muted sage, warm ivory, rich American walnut, natural stone, and understated satin brass extends across three expansive floors. The layout accommodates an impressive reception room, bespoke culinary kitchen, formal dining room, grand entrance hallway, and a tranquil principal bedroom suite.

Our team delivered tailored Barnet residence case study, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Artisanal Joinery & Materiality

Tailored Architectural Joinery & Period Proportion

Every architectural element was designed to balance the classical proportions of the residence with modern convenience.

Bespoke cabinetry throughout the ground and first floors was custom built in solid American walnut and hand-painted timber. In the reception room, fluted joinery conceals media systems and atmospheric perimeter LED illumination, while delicate plaster mouldings preserve architectural character.

The principal ensuite features book-matched natural stone slabs, brushed brass tapware, and a bespoke double vanity with reeded timber drawers, providing an indulgent spa retreat.
