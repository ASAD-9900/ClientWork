Zone Pricing Explained

How Do We Count Rooms & Zones?

Packages Are Priced Per Room or Functional Zone

Each room or functional area is treated as an individual design zone. Even if Kitchen, Dining, and Living sit within one open-plan room, they have different functions and are treated as 3 zones.

01 £600 / Zone

One Design (3 Zones: £1,800)

Example for an open-plan Kitchen (1 zone) + Dining (1 zone) + Living (1 zone) at £600 per zone = £1,800 total.

02 £900 / Zone

Unlimited Design (3 Zones: £2,700)

Example for an open-plan Kitchen + Dining + Living (3 zones) at £900 per zone = £2,700 total.

03 £1,500 / Zone

Design & Technical (3 Zones: £4,500)

Example for an open-plan Kitchen + Dining + Living (3 zones) at £1,500 per zone = £4,500 total.

04 Clarity

Confirmed Upfront

Zones can include Kitchen, Dining, Living, Home Office, Reading, Dressing, or Hallway—confirmed before your project begins.
