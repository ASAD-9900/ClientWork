Apartment Refurbishment

Renovating a London Apartment?

Check the Building Requirements Early

Many London apartment projects involve more than the interior design itself.

Depending on the freeholder and managing agent, refurbishments often require Licences to Alter, insurance certifications, contractor RAMS, strict working hours, waste chutes or timed clearances, water shut-off permits and acoustic test certificates.

Oraanj coordinates technical design drawings and specifications early to ensure building management approvals run smoothly without delaying your programme.

Licence to Alter coordination
Acoustic floor testing specs
Managing agent liaison
Contractor RAMS & insurance
Timed delivery & waste permits
Water isolation scheduling
Historic London Homes

Renovating a Period or Listed Property?

Understand the Property Before Changing It

London properties situated in conservation areas or carrying listed status require sensitive architectural handling.

While not every internal alteration requires formal consent, proposals involving structural layout changes, historical cornices, chimneypieces, or window replacements require careful review.

We develop our interior schemes with the building's historical integrity in mind, collaborating closely with conservation officers and heritage consultants where statutory permissions are involved.

Conservation area awareness
Preservation of period mouldings
Scribing joinery to historic walls
Sensitive integration of modern services
