London Retail FAQ
[accordion] [accordion-item title="What does a retail interior designer do?"]

A retail interior designer plans how a shop functions, sells, and feels. This includes spatial layouts, customer circulation paths, merchandise display systems, high-CRI lighting schemes, bespoke joinery, POS counters, materials, fitting rooms, and back-of-house storage.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you design a shop from an empty shell unit?"]

Yes. We design for brand-new commercial shell units as well as existing high street premises undergoing complete refurbishment, managing everything from basic partition layouts to luxury bespoke finishes.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you help us improve our existing shop layout?"]

Yes. Space planning can identify underperforming sales areas, optimize customer circulation routes, resolve till queues, and introduce more effective display fixtures to boost sales per square metre.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you design bespoke retail displays and counters?"]

Yes. We design bespoke wall display units, centre-floor display tables, till counters, secure glass vitrines, and fitting-room joinery engineered specifically around your product dimensions and storage needs.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj manage the retail fit-out and construction?"]

Yes. We provide interior design as an independent consulting service, or as an integrated Design & Build contract managing building contractors, specialist electricians, and joinery fitters through to opening day.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you work alongside our existing shopfitter or contractor?"]

Yes. If you already have an appointed contractor, we produce detailed technical design packages, joinery elevations, and lighting schedules, maintaining regular site coordination to ensure design integrity.
