Choosing the Right Delivery Route

Full-Service Design, Design & Build, or Turnkey?

Depending on whether your property requires pure furnishing, physical renovation, or full hands-off turnkey execution, we offer three clear project paths:

Full-Service Interior Design (Design + Sourcing + Procurement)

For properties with no structural work required, or where you already have a builder. We handle space planning, interior architecture, 3D CGI, furniture sourcing, procurement, and white-glove setup.

Design & Build (Design + Renovation Delivery)

For homes needing wall alterations, new kitchens, luxury bathrooms, and technical MEP. Oraanj coordinates both the design drawings and the physical building works under one contract.

Turnkey Interior Design (Complete Handover)

The ultimate hands-off route: complete design, architectural renovation, trade procurement, bespoke joinery, loose furniture, and dressing so you walk into a finished home.

London FAQ
[accordion] [accordion-item title="What is full-service interior design?"]

Full-Service Interior Design manages the entire creative and operational journey. Depending on the agreed scope, it includes space planning, 3D visualisations, furniture and lighting curation, bespoke joinery fabrication, trade procurement, delivery consolidation, and final white-glove styling.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="What is the difference between Full-Service, Design & Build, and Turnkey?"]

Full-Service focuses on the design, furniture sourcing, procurement, and styling. Design & Build delivers design plus the physical construction and renovation. Turnkey brings everything together: design, construction, bespoke joinery, all furniture, and final dressing so the property is move-in ready.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj work alongside my existing builder?"]

Yes. If you have an appointed contractor, Oraanj prepares the technical drawings, lighting plans, and joinery elevations for your contractor to implement on site while we coordinate the FF&E procurement.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you manage my London project if I live abroad?"]

Yes. A substantial portion of our full-service clients live overseas. We provide digital design walkthroughs, courier physical finish sample boxes, and manage all London supplier logistics remotely.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How long does full-service interior design take?"]

A multi-room or whole-home project typically takes 3 to 6 months depending on the extent of bespoke joinery and Italian furniture manufacturing lead times. We establish a clear timeline before beginning.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can I keep cherished pieces of existing furniture?"]

Yes. We regularly incorporate beloved heirlooms, antiques, and artworks into the new layout and design narrative.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you provide full-service design for commercial premises?"]

Yes. Oraanj undertakes full-service interior design for executive offices, boutique hospitality, restaurants, and retail spaces across London.
