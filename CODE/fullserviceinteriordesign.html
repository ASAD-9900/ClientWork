Service Routes

Choosing the Right Delivery Route

Full-Service Design, Design & Build, or Turnkey?

Depending on whether your property requires pure furnishing, physical renovation, or full hands-off turnkey execution, we offer three clear project paths:

Full-Service Interior Design (Design + Sourcing + Procurement)

For properties with no structural work required, or where you already have a builder. We handle space planning, interior architecture, 3D CGI, furniture sourcing, procurement, and white-glove setup.

Design & Build (Design + Renovation Delivery)

For homes needing wall alterations, new kitchens, luxury bathrooms, and technical MEP. Oraanj coordinates both the design drawings and the physical building works under one contract.

Turnkey Interior Design (Complete Handover)

The ultimate hands-off route: complete design, architectural renovation, trade procurement, bespoke joinery, loose furniture, and dressing so you walk into a finished home.
