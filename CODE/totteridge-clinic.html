Healthcare & Dental Architecture

A tranquil North London wellness clinic uniting clinical excellence, calming biophilic tones and bespoke terrazzo craftsmanship

Commissioned for a prestigious private medical and dental practice in Totteridge, this turnkey commercial interior balances stringent CQC clinical regulations with the soothing, hospitality-calibre ambiance essential for nervous patients. The spatial layout moves patients seamlessly from an open, light-filled reception foyer into whisper-quiet treatment suites.

The practice was delivered through our integrated methodology as an interior designer in Totteridge, aligning sterile healthcare requirements with luxury aesthetic detailing.

A restorative architectural palette of warm neutral fluted plaster, natural oak joinery, bespoke terrazzo vanities with satin brass tapware, and anti-microbial medical surfaces elevates the clinical standard. Acoustic-rated partitions, concealed MEP integration, and diffused cove illumination foster an unhurried, reassuring environment throughout the patient journey.

Building on our extensive portfolio of healthcare commissions, including the Harrow clinic project, this Totteridge facility sets a fresh benchmark for prime dental and wellness design.

Medical Detailing & Compliance

CQC Standards, Terrazzo Detailing & Acoustic Seclusion

Balancing clinical infection control, precision plumbing and suction routing, with sensory calm and bespoke interior architecture.

In modern dental and medical practices, technical compliance must integrate invisibly. We specified non-porous antimicrobial solid surfaces, flush-fitting cabinetry with touchless sensor hardware, and commercial hospital-grade air exchange systems. Fluted timber reception counters and textured terrazzo basins create an immediate sense of boutique luxury from the moment patients enter.

Consultation and surgery suites feature double-stud acoustic walls and sound-dampening acoustic plasterboard, guaranteeing complete doctor-patient confidentiality. Diffused, shadowless LED luminaires provide precise working illumination for clinicians while remaining gentle on patients' eyes.
