Why Plan the Electrical Layout With the Interior?

Because Furniture Changes Where Electrical Points Need to Go

An electrical plan should relate to the way the room will actually be used. When electrical decisions are made separately from the furniture plan, points can end up in inconvenient or visually awkward positions.

Scope & Responsibility

What Oraanj's Electrical Layout Is — and Isn't

Interior Electrical Coordination vs. Qualified Electrical & M&E Engineering

Oraanj's electrical layout drawings communicate interior design requirements and proposed positions within our agreed design scope. They are not a substitute for electrical engineering or the work of a qualified electrician.

1. What Oraanj Coordinates

Proposed socket positions, proposed switch positions, lighting locations, furniture and joinery relationships, and selected appliance locations around the interior design.

2. What Specialist Electrical Professionals Handle

Qualified electricians and engineers remain responsible for circuit design, cable sizing, electrical loads, distribution boards, protective devices, safety, testing, certification, and regulatory compliance.

3. Mechanical & M&E Engineering

Where specialist M&E building services (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, electrical distribution, and plant) are required, they should be produced or confirmed by a qualified M&E consultant.
