Furniture-First Planning

Why Plan the Electrical Layout With the Interior?

Because Furniture Changes Where Electrical Points Need to Go

An electrical plan should relate to the way the room will actually be used. When these decisions are made separately from the furniture plan, electrical points can end up in inconvenient or visually awkward positions.

01 Beds & Sofas

Seating & Sleeping Positions

Bed position affects bedside sockets and switches, while sofa position determines floor and table lamp power requirements.

02 Dining & Kitchen

Tables & Appliances

Dining-table placement dictates pendant centres, and kitchen layouts define worktop sockets and appliance positions.

03 Work & Media

TVs, Media Walls & Desks

Television and media-wall designs require concealed power and data locations, while desks need accessible charging points.

04 Fitted Joinery

Wardrobes & Cabinetry

Bespoke wardrobe and shelving designs affect internal LED lighting channels, transformer access, and switch positions.

Our electrical plans form part of comprehensive technical CAD drawings ensuring complete coordination on site.

Electrical Planning for Open-Plan Living & Dining

Kitchen, Dining & Living Need to Work Together

Scope, Process & Cost

What Oraanj's Electrical Layout Is — and How We Work

Interior Electrical Coordination vs. Specialist Engineering & Clear Pricing

Oraanj's electrical layout drawings communicate interior design requirements and proposed positions within our agreed design scope. They are not a substitute for electrical or M&E engineering.

1. Interior Coordination vs. Qualified Engineering

Oraanj coordinates proposed socket, switch, and lighting locations with furniture and joinery. Qualified electricians and M&E engineers remain responsible for circuit design, cable sizing, loads, distribution boards, HVAC/mechanical services, safety, testing, certification, and compliance.

2. Our 7-Step Drawing Process

Send project information → Establish furniture layout → Confirm requirements → Produce proposed drawing → Client review → Coordinate with wider team → Issue final drawing in agreed PDF or DWG format.

3. Tailored Drawing Fees

Fees depend on the number of floors and rooms, whether an accurate DWG/furniture layout already exists, lighting and bespoke joinery complexity, and required deliverables.
