What Does Luxury Interior Design Mean to Us?

Not More Decoration. Better Decisions.

In high-end interior design, luxury is frequently mistaken for ornament: more fabrics, heavier mouldings, higher price tags. We see it differently.

Genuine luxury in a London home is the result of architectural clarity: balanced proportions, natural daylight, intuitive circulation, exquisite materials that age gracefully, and acoustic serenity.

01 Architecture

Proportions & Volume

Celebrating generous ceiling heights, period details, and natural illumination throughout the day.

02 Flow

Spatial Symmetry

Aligning portals and sightlines so spaces feel generous, calm, and effortless to navigate.

03 Lifestyle

Tailored Living

Designing specifically for family routines, fine art collections, and effortless entertaining.

04 Materials

Enduring Quality

Honest natural stone, solid timber, and refined textiles that gain beauty and patina with age.

Luxury Interior Design Questions

Practical insights on commissioning high-end residential interior design and turnkey delivery in London.

Expert Guidance
[accordion] [accordion-item title="What does a luxury interior designer do?"]

A luxury interior designer coordinates the multifaceted decisions required for a high-end home: spatial planning, interior architecture, electrical and lighting design, bespoke joinery, stone and timber selection, furniture curation, procurement, and complete turnkey delivery.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="What makes an interior feel luxurious?"]

Luxury is defined by spatial balance, proportion, natural light, material authenticity, precise craftsmanship, and acoustic comfort rather than mere brand labels or superficial decoration.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Does Oraanj have a rigid signature style?"]

No. We develop each interior organically around the client's lifestyle, art collection, and the specific architecture of the property, creating unique, bespoke residences.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you design complete London homes?"]

Yes. We undertake complete multi-storey townhouses, lateral penthouses, substantial family homes, as well as focused single-room transformations.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you work on period and listed properties?"]

Yes. We balance the preservation and restoration of historic mouldings, fireplaces and cornices with contemporary kitchens, bathrooms and smart home services.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you design penthouses and apartments?"]

Yes. We design for prime Central London apartments and penthouses, managing building management regulations, licences to alter, and acoustic compliance.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you design bespoke joinery and custom furniture?"]

Yes. All fitted wardrobes, dressing suites, media walls, home offices, and custom credenzas are individually detailed and crafted by our master joinery partners.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you manage the renovation as well?"]

Yes. Our integrated Design & Build service provides complete single-source construction, trade management, site supervision, and turnkey handover.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you work alongside my existing architect or builder?"]

Yes. We regularly collaborate with external architects, structural engineers, and main contractors, providing detailed interior architectural packages and FF&E schedules.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you manage my London property while I am overseas?"]

Yes. We provide complete turnkey management for overseas clients, with virtual design presentations, couriered physical sample boxes, and weekly video progress updates.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How much does luxury interior design cost in London?"]

Cost depends on property size, bespoke joinery volume, material grades, and whether renovation delivery is included. We provide transparent, itemised project proposals following our initial property review.
