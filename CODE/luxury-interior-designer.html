What Does Luxury Interior Design Mean to Us?

Not More Decoration. Better Decisions.

A high-end interior is not created by filling a property with expensive products. It comes from getting hundreds of individual decisions to work together seamlessly.

We balance furniture proportions, natural circulation flows, where light falls, how bespoke joinery responds to the architecture, material tactile qualities, what is concealed versus displayed, and how the home transitions from bright morning routines to atmospheric evening entertaining.

The result should feel considered and deeply individual without ever feeling over-designed.

What Does Luxury Interior Design Cost in London?

High-End Projects Are Individually Scoped

There isn't a single universal price for high-end residential interior projects.

Total investment depends on property square footage, structural alterations, bespoke joinery volume, kitchen and bathroom specifications, FF&E selections, and project management scope. Substantial residences are individually scoped with transparent milestone deliverables.

For targeted single spaces, room-based design packages start from £600 per functional zone. For whole-home transformations, we prepare an itemised design and delivery proposal tailored to your property.
