Renovating Your London Home?

Design the Interior Before the Building Work Begins

When undertaking a substantial refurbishment in London, interior design decisions determine critical structural, technical, and trade sequences long before decorating starts.

Key early design decisions dictate:

  • Structural Layouts: Wall removals, door alignments, and spatial circulation
  • First-Fix Electrical & MEP: Light switches, 5-amp circuits, wall lights, and floor sockets
  • Plumbing Coordinates: Concealed shower valves, freestanding bath waste, and kitchen islands
  • Bespoke Joinery Details: Wall recesses, ceiling bulkheads, and lighting coves
  • Material Interfaces: Precise transitions between timber, marble, and tile

Resolving these specifications before your contractor breaks ground eliminates costly variation orders, site delays, and aesthetic compromises. Where appointed for Design & Build, Oraanj seamlessly delivers both the design and the build under one accountable team.

Can an Interior Designer Visit My Property?

Yes — Where an On-Site Consultation Is Appropriate

For projects requiring an in-person assessment, Oraanj arranges site consultations across London to evaluate your property firsthand.

How Much Does an Interior Designer Cost in London?

Transparent Pricing Based on Project Requirements

Interior design costs vary considerably because styling a single bedroom differs fundamentally from designing and managing a multi-storey townhouse refurbishment.

Key factors influencing your overall project investment include:

  • Overall Scale: Number of rooms, floor area, and ceiling heights
  • Design Depth: Mood boards vs. detailed technical and MEP CAD drawing packages
  • 3D Visualisation: Volume of photorealistic CGI renders required
  • Custom Joinery: Extent of bespoke architectural cabinetry and dressing rooms
  • FF&E Specification: High-street selections vs. bespoke artisan upholstery and luxury stone
  • Delivery Method: Design-only consultancy vs. turnkey renovation and site supervision

While room-based design packages start from £600 per zone, whole-property refurbishments, luxury townhouses, and Design & Build contracts are quoted with fully transparent, itemised scopes.
