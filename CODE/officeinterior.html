Before You Sign a Commercial Lease

Test the Space Before You Commit to 5 or 10 Years

Square footage on an agent's brochure is misleading. Two identical 4,000 sq ft offices can perform completely differently due to column grids, fire exit cores, and window placement.

01 Capacity

Real Desk Count Feasibility

Testing how many compliant workstations, meeting rooms, and amenities comfortably fit without violating fire escape codes.

02 Services

03 Landlord

Building Management Rules

Reviewing landlord fit-out handbooks, service lift booking restrictions, out-of-hours noisy working rules, and licence to alter protocols.

04 Savings

Retain vs. Replace Auditing

Identifying high-value existing elements—such as raised access flooring or glazed meeting fronts—that can save substantial capex.
