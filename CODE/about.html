Our Heritage & Philosophy

Our Story: More Than 20 Years of Designing Interiors

Over two decades of practice in London, we have evolved our interior design services to encompass architectural spatial planning, interior styling, and full turnkey delivery.

Oraanj was founded with the belief that good interior design should be personal.

A home shouldn't feel like a copy of somebody else's home. A restaurant shouldn't simply follow the latest trend. An office shouldn't look impressive but fail the people working in it.

We start by listening:

How do you live?
What isn't working in the property?
What do you want to change?
What should the space feel like?
What matters most to you?

From there, we develop the layout, materials, lighting, joinery, furniture and details around the individual project.

Over the years, our work has grown from interior design into a wider studio offering interior architecture, technical design, renovation, procurement and project delivery.

Today, Oraanj works across homes, workplaces and hospitality interiors, while keeping the same personal approach that shaped the studio from the beginning.

Rachana Gupta is the Founder and CEO of Oraanj Interior Design.

With more than 20 years of experience in interior design, Rachana has worked across residential, commercial, workplace and hospitality interiors in London, the UK and internationally.

Her approach begins with understanding the client rather than imposing a particular design style. End-to-End Buildability

Design Is Only Part of the Story

We Think About How It Will Actually Be Delivered

A design can look beautiful on screen and still be difficult to implement. That's why our work considers the practical side of the interior alongside the creative one.

01 Spatial Layout

Space Planning & Furniture

Space planning affects furniture scale and circulation pathways. We evaluate natural walkways and proportions so pieces fit comfortably without overcrowding rooms.

02 Illumination

Furniture & Lighting

Furniture affects lighting positions. We coordinate ambient, decorative and task lighting so fixtures illuminate key functional zones, dining tables and seating accurately.

03 Services

Lighting & Electrical Positions

Lighting affects electrical positions. Early electrical schematics and switch-plate coordination prevent unsightly cable runs and wall channelling later in the build.

04 Architectural Fit

Joinery, Walls & Sockets

Bespoke joinery affects walls, sockets and surrounding finishes. We engineer made-to-measure cabinetry flush with architectural recesses and integrated power feeds.

05 Material Detailing

Materials & Surface Junctions

Materials affect how different surfaces meet. We detail clean stone-to-timber threshold junctions, shadow gaps and expansion margins before contractors break ground.

06 Complex Zones

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Kitchens and bathrooms involve multiple interdependent decisions — plumbing falls, extract ducting, tile setting, sanitaryware and appliance tolerances working together.

Seamless Turnkey Delivery from Drawings to Property

By considering these relationships earlier, the design becomes more useful when the project moves from drawings into the real property. For clients who want wider support, Oraanj can continue beyond the design stage into renovation, procurement, project management and installation.

Collaborative Culture

How We Work Together: Design Is Collaborative

Good interiors aren't created in isolation.

Our projects involve structured conversations between clients, designers, technical specialists, suppliers, craftspeople and the people responsible for implementation:

  • • The designer establishes the core aesthetic idea and spatial vision.
  • • The technical team works through the construction details, regulations and tolerances.
  • • A joinery specialist engineers how bespoke elements should be crafted and fitted.
  • • A supplier helps resolve the right specification, durability and material lead times.
  • • The project delivery team manages site progress and fits the work into the wider programme.

Each person sees the project from a slightly different perspective. Our role is to keep those decisions connected to the original design and the client's priorities.

Real London Case Study

From Drawing Board to Real Interior

See How Our Team's Work Connects

Follow the journey of a genuine Oraanj project as it moves through each collaborative discipline — from initial brief to completed, styled interior.

STAGE 01

Client Brief

Understanding aspirations, practical living requirements, budget benchmarks and key milestones to establish a clear creative and commercial brief.

STAGE 02

Space Planning

Developing scaled 2D spatial layouts, testing alternative circulation routes, and determining optimal furniture orientations and focal points.

STAGE 03

Interior Design & CGI

Translating the brief into photorealistic 3D renders, moodboards and bespoke palettes so clients visualise textures, lighting and ambiance before build starts.

STAGE 04

Technical Design

Producing build-ready CAD elevations, bespoke joinery construction drawings, lighting layouts, and MEP coordination schedules.

STAGE 05

Materials & Finishes

Reviewing physical timber veneers, natural stone slabs, architectural brassware and tactile fabrics to ensure durability and aesthetic harmony.

STAGE 06

On-Site Execution

Managing strip-out, structural adjustments, 1st and 2nd fix mechanical/electrical services, and inspecting site progress against approved drawings.

STAGE 07

Installation & Fit-Out

Overseeing master joinery installation, architectural lighting commissioning, stone placement, and white-glove FF&E delivery and placement.

STAGE 08

Finished Interior

Final styling, professional snagging inspections, client orientation, and a smooth turnkey handover of a home built to last.

Guiding Values

What Matters to Us

Six core commitments that guide every design decision, project and client partnership.

01 Empathy

Listen Before Designing

Understanding the client comes before deciding what the interior should look like. We listen carefully to your daily routines, aspirations and concerns before putting pencil to paper.

02 Usability

Make the Space Work

Layout, circulation, storage and everyday usability matter as much as appearance. A space must function effortlessly when you are actually living or working in it.

03 Context

Design for the Property

We respond to the architecture, light and natural character of the individual space rather than applying the same repetitive look everywhere.

04 Precision

Think Through the Details

Lighting, furniture, joinery and materials need to work together. We coordinate junctions, finishes and services so nothing is left to chance during construction.

05 Integrity

Keep Design & Delivery Connected

Where Oraanj is involved beyond the design stage, we work actively on site to maintain the direct relationship between the approved design drawings and the finished interior.

06 Partnership

Build Long-Term Relationships

Interior projects can take months and involve many critical decisions. Clear communication, transparency and mutual trust matter throughout every stage of that process.
