From Rachana

What Over 20 Years of Interior Design Has Taught Me

I’m Rachana Gupta, Founder & Creative Director of Oraanj Interior Design.

Over the years, I’ve learnt that good interior design is rarely about following a trend. It’s about understanding the property, solving what isn’t working, and making decisions that still feel right once the excitement of something new has passed.

Through this journal, my team and I share lessons from real projects: the decisions that worked, the challenges we encountered, and the considerations that matter most before you start designing or renovating.

I hope it helps you make better decisions for your own space.

Rachana Gupta
Founder & Creative Director, Oraanj Interior Design
