Understanding Drawings

Floor Plans vs. Interior Elevations

Why Complete Projects Require Both Perspectives

A floor plan coordinates horizontal space and movement; an elevation coordinates vertical scale and wall finishes. Successful renovation projects require both drawing types.

1. Floor Plans: Horizontal Flow & Spatial Density

Communicates room layouts, walking corridors, door clearances, functional zones, and how multiple rooms interact across the property.

2. Wall Elevations: Vertical Harmony & Joinery Detail

Communicates finished ceiling heights, joinery doors, wall panelling, mirror alignments, and vertical lighting positions straight on.

3. Cross-Referenced Drawing Key & Notes

Every elevation is keyed to the floor plan with clear view markers, ensuring contractors immediately locate where each wall belongs.

Drawing FAQ
[accordion] [accordion-item title="Can you produce AutoCAD drawings for my interior project?"]

Yes. Oraanj produces agreed interior drawings in AutoCAD for residential homes, apartments, offices, restaurants, and retail spaces, tailored around your project requirements and existing site information.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do I need an existing DWG file to get started?"]

Not necessarily. While an existing CAD DWG from an architect makes drawing faster, we can also work from developer PDFs, estate agent floor plans, or laser site measurements to establish the base geometry.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can my builder and joiner use your drawings on site?"]

Yes. Our drawings are produced to precise metric scales (1:50, 1:20, 1:10) with explicit dimensions, finished floor level (FFL) heights, and item keys specifically designed for trade pricing and site execution.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Are these drawings suitable for Building Regulations or Planning Permission?"]

Our drawings focus on interior design, furniture layout, bespoke joinery, finishes, and electrical/lighting positioning. Structural calculations, fire engineering, and statutory Building Regulations approvals require appropriately certified structural engineers or architects.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you draw bespoke joinery and media wall elevations?"]

Yes. We develop detailed interior design elevations for custom wardrobes, media units, dressing rooms, and home offices showing overall dimensions, door configurations, and material notes ready for specialist cabinetmakers.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you provide drawing services outside London?"]

Yes. We regularly produce remote technical drawing sets for residential and commercial projects across the UK where accurate dimensions, architectural plans, or survey measurements are provided.
