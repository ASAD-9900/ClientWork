The Studio Framework

The Oraanj Method

Brief • Plan • Design • Detail • Source • Present

Our programme follows the six stages of professional interior design thinking used on real client projects.

Instead of disjointed styling exercises, you will master the end-to-end framework that reputable London studios use to shepherd projects from raw client conversation to turnkey handover.

01 Brief

Client & Space Intelligence

Understand the client, property, lifestyle priorities, spatial constraints and practical requirements before beginning any design work.

02 Plan

Spatial Architecture

Develop functional layouts around circulation, ergonomics, natural light and how people genuinely navigate and inhabit the space.

03 Design

Concept & Visual Direction

Build a coherent aesthetic identity through curated colour palettes, tactile material combinations, lighting layers and furniture direction.

04 Detail

Technical Documentation

Understand how plans, interior elevations, lighting layouts, small power coordination and bespoke joinery drawings communicate an approved design.

05 Source

FF&E & Procurement

Learn how designers approach furniture selection, trade suppliers, material finishes, fabric specifications and procurement schedules.

06 Present

Client & Portfolio Delivery

Bring the entire journey together into compelling design presentations and professional case studies that showcase your design thinking.
