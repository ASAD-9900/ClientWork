What Is Space Planning in Interior Design?

Work Out How the Space Should Function Before You Invest

Space planning is the discipline of choreographing furniture, circulation, daylight, and daily activities within a room. We answer the practical questions before you commit capital.

01 Proportions

Scale & Proportion

Ensuring furniture dimensions harmonise with the architecture, preventing oversized sectionals or undersized dining sets from unbalancing the room.

02 Clearances

Circulation & Walkways

Protecting generous 900mm+ pathways between doorways, furniture edges, and terrace sliders for effortless, intuitive everyday movement.

03 Zoning

Defined Functional Zones

Creating discrete zones for dining, lounging, home working, and entertaining without building restrictive physical dividing walls.

04 Daylight

Natural Light & Sightlines

Arranging primary seating and workspaces to capture natural window light while eliminating screen reflections and framing garden views.

Plan Before Renovation or Building Work

Inform Electrical, Lighting and Joinery Locations First

Moving a double socket, radiator, or pendant light after plastering is finished is frustrating and expensive. Resolving furniture layouts first gives your builders clear first-fix instructions.

01 Sockets

Precise Electrical Positioning

Placing floor sockets under free-standing sofas, bedside charging plates at headboard height, and AV points behind televisions.

02 Lighting

Lighting Alignment

Centering dining pendants directly over table locations rather than room centres, and aiming recessed downlights at art rather than seats.

03 Joinery

Bespoke Joinery Integration

Setting dimensions for fitted alcove cabinetry, media walls, and built-in wardrobes before underfloor heating and skirting are installed.

04 Structural

Testing Structural Changes

Verifying whether removing an internal wall or adding an extension genuinely yields superior living layouts before spending on steel beams.

Space Planning vs Interior Architecture & Design

Choosing the Right Level of Service for Your Project

Understanding how spatial planning connects with wider interior design and technical architecture helps you select the exact service package your property needs.

1. Space Planning (How the space functions)

Focuses purely on furniture layouts, dimensions, circulation, zoning, and how you live. Ideal when you want layout certainty without committing to wider styling.

2. Full Interior Design (How the space looks & feels)

Develops the decorative scheme—fabrics, wall finishes, bespoke joinery, decorative lighting, colour palettes, and trade procurement coordination.

3. Interior Architecture (How the space is constructed)

Addresses structural partition alterations, building regulations, MEP coordination, bathroom sanitaryware layouts, and technical tender drawing packs.

Space Planning FAQ
[accordion] [accordion-item title="What is space planning in interior design?"]

Space planning is the process of organising furniture, circulation, and functional zones within a room or property. It establishes how the available floorplate should work and feel before progressing into detailed decorating, joinery orders, or purchasing expensive furniture.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How much does space planning cost in London?"]

Space planning fees depend on property size, the number of rooms, the quality of existing drawings, and the depth of layout alternatives required. Send us your floor plan and requirements for a fast, fixed-fee quotation.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can I book space planning for just one single room?"]

Yes. We frequently plan individual living rooms, open-plan kitchen-diners, master bedroom suites, or loft conversions where clients need layout certainty before furnishing.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you tell me what size sofa or dining table will fit?"]

Yes. We establish exact maximum and recommended dimensions for sectionals, dining tables, and beds, ensuring adequate clearance around furniture for effortless movement.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you need to visit my property in person?"]

Not always. Many space planning schemes are completed using developer or estate agent floor plans, photos, and video walkthroughs. Where dimensions are missing or floorplates are complex, we can arrange an on-site laser survey.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can space planning help before a home renovation?"]

Yes. Resolving furniture layouts before renovation informs first-fix electrical sockets, lighting chandelier positions, underfloor heating zones, and bespoke joinery sizing, preventing expensive retrospective modifications.
