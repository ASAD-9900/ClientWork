Choose Your Interior Design Package

Three Levels of Professional Support Tailored to Your Room

Select the level of design development and technical detail that fits your room and how you plan to deliver it.

1. One Design — £600 Per Room

A straightforward option when you want one clear design direction. Includes 1 final 3D design, basic furniture layout, 1 round of revisions, colour and visual direction, and furniture positioning. Best suited to bedrooms, smaller rooms, or straightforward updates.

2. Unlimited Design — £900 Per Room

For clients who want more flexibility to develop and refine the room. Includes 3D visualisation, unlimited design revisions within the agreed scope, refined furniture layout, and furniture, material, finish, lighting, and styling direction.

3. Design & Technical — £1,500 Per Room

For rooms requiring detailed design information before renovation or contractor works begin. Includes 3D visualisation, detailed space planning, revisions, relevant wall elevations, lighting layout, electrical position intent, and bespoke joinery design where included in the agreed room scope.
