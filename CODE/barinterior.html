Bar Interior Design Questions

Key questions answered about designing, refurbishing, and fitting out commercial bars in London.

London FAQs
[accordion] [accordion-item title="What is included in bar interior design?"]

Bar interior design includes spatial planning, customer journey mapping, bartender workflow ergonomics, bar counter design, back-bar bottle displays, lighting schemes, acoustic treatments, material specifications, bespoke joinery, FF&E procurement, and technical CAD drawings for construction.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Oraanj manage the full bar fit-out too?"]

Yes. Through our integrated commercial fit-out delivery, our construction directors manage physical refurbishment, MEP services, bespoke joinery installation, and final styling under one accountable contract.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="How long does a bar interior design and fit-out project take?"]

Design and technical drawings typically take 4 to 8 weeks. Physical fit-out usually takes 6 to 12 weeks depending on venue size, structural modifications, and bespoke joinery fabrication lead times.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you design for specific bar types like cocktail lounges or pubs?"]

Yes. We design for cocktail bars, wine bars, hotel lounges, gastro pubs, rooftop venues, and private members' clubs across London, tailoring ergonomics and atmosphere to your specific drinks offer.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can you coordinate with our kitchen and bar equipment suppliers?"]

Yes. We work closely with catering and commercial refrigeration equipment suppliers to ensure all counter dimensions, water inlets, waste points, and three-phase power connections align perfectly.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Do you handle licensing and building control requirements?"]

We incorporate building regulations, fire exit strategies, DDA accessibility, and acoustic isolation into our architectural plans, liaising with your licensing lawyers and approved inspectors.
