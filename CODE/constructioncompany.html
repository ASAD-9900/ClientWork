Accurate Costing

Before We Price the Works

What Makes a Construction Quote Meaningful?

A quotation is only as useful as the information behind it. If one contractor prices a basic specification and another assumes premium materials, the figures aren't directly comparable.

01 / SCOPE

Defined Scope of Works

Exactly what work is included in the project boundaries, what is being stripped out, and what structural elements or existing fabric are retained.

02 / DRAWINGS

Technical Drawings & CAD

Clear floor plans, elevations, lighting plans, electrical circuits, and joinery drawings required for contractors to price and build from.

03 / SPEC

Material Specification

Defined selections for sanitaryware, kitchen cabinetry, worktops, flooring types, timber mouldings, paint grades and specialist finishes.

04 / PROGRAMME

Site Conditions & Programme

Target handover dates, building management rules, access restrictions, working hours, and procurement lead times for bespoke items.

The clearer these 4 key factors are, the easier it becomes to understand what a quotation actually covers and ensure true price certainty.

What Happens Before Construction Starts?

A clear, disciplined 4-stage sequence to eliminate ambiguity before physical works begin.

01

Project Information & Site Review

Stage 1: Review

We review the property and available design information in detail, conducting a site visit to assess existing structural fabric and access conditions.

02

Technical Audit & Information Check

Stage 2: Audit

We identify whether critical CAD drawings, MEP layouts, structural engineering calculations or specialist consultant reports are missing.

03

Scope of Works & Costing

Stage 3: Pricing

The proposed construction and refurbishment requirements are clearly defined, providing the rigorous basis for developing an accurate contractor quotation.

04

Procurement, Programme & Pre-Start

Stage 4: Launch

Long-lead items are scheduled, building management licences and party wall notices confirmed, and trade sequence aligned before site works commence.

Who Is Responsible for What?

Clear Responsibilities Make Construction Easier to Manage

A refurbishment involves several professionals. Clear role allocation ensures complete accountability across design, engineering and site execution.

01 / Concept

Interior Designer

Develops concepts, layouts, materials, colours and furniture selection.

02 / Documentation

Technical Design Team

Produces agreed technical interior drawings, joinery details and lighting plans.

03 / Engineering

Structural Engineer

Calculates load-bearing wall alterations and structural steel requirements.

04 / Compliance

Specialist Consultants

Advise on party wall agreements, building control and acoustic testing.

05 / Execution

Construction Team

Delivers the physical works on site, coordinating trades, safety and craft.

06 / Delivery

Project Manager

Coordinates programme milestones, communication, deliveries and procurement.

The exact appointments and responsibilities should be established for each project rather than assumed.
