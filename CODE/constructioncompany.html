Accurate Costing

Before We Price the Works

What Makes a Construction Quote Meaningful?

A quotation is only as useful as the information behind it. If one contractor prices a basic specification and another assumes premium materials, the figures aren't directly comparable.

01. Scope

Exactly what work is included in the project boundaries?

02. Drawings

What are the contractors expected to build from?

03. Specification

Which materials, products and finishes have been selected?

04. Joinery

What bespoke made-to-measure items need to be manufactured?

05. Kitchens & Bathrooms

What is client-supplied and what needs contractor installation?

06. Specialist Work

Are structural, electrical, plumbing or other specialist requirements involved?

07. Site Conditions

Are there access, parking, management-company or working-hour restrictions?

08. Programme

When does the property need to be completed and handed over?

The clearer these items are, the easier it becomes to understand what a quotation actually covers and ensure true price certainty.

London Property Practicalities

Navigating London Buildings with Confidence

Apartments and heritage buildings present unique logistical and structural conditions that require seasoned contractor management.

Apartments & Mansions

Construction in a London Apartment

Check the Building Rules Before Site Starts

Apartment refurbishment can involve practical requirements beyond the work inside the property. Depending on the building, these can include:

Management-company procedures Landlord / freeholder licences Restricted working hours Lift & staircase protection Scheduled deliveries Parking suspensions Waste removal logistics Noise restrictions Protection of communal areas Licence to Alter (LtA)

These requirements vary between properties and should be established before the project programme is finalised.

Heritage & Conservation

Refurbishing a Period Property

Existing Buildings Can Reveal the Unexpected

Older properties sometimes reveal conditions once existing finishes or previous alterations are opened up. This might affect:

Sub-floor timbers & joists Historical wall construction Concealed existing services Substandard previous alterations Original architectural details Moisture & damp ingress Chimney breasts & flues

Where an issue requires structural or specialist advice, appropriate professionals assess it before works continue. A sensible programme allows for the fact that not everything is visible at the beginning.

Budgets & Costing

How Much Does Interior Construction Cost in London?

There isn't one meaningful price for every refurbishment. Construction cost is directly influenced by:

✓Property size & existing condition
✓Extent of strip-out & structural alterations
✓Kitchens & bathroom sanitaryware
✓Electrical rewiring & plumbing overhaul

✓Bespoke fitted joinery complexity
✓Site access, parking & restrictions

A contractor pricing from a detailed design has more information than one pricing from a photograph. That is why we review available drawings before discussing a meaningful budget.

Programmes & Sequencing

How Long Does Interior Construction Take?

Scope and Property Condition Dictate Timelines

A limited refurbishment naturally has a different programme from a complete property involving extensive alterations, new kitchens, bathrooms and bespoke joinery. Timelines are influenced by:

✓Building access & working-hour limits
✓Freeholder licences & statutory approvals
✓Material & stone lead times
✓Bespoke joinery manufacturing duration
✓Specialist trade sequencing
✓Existing building discoveries during strip-out
✓Design variations during works
✓Commissioning & final snagging

Before the main works begin, the proposed sequence and important dependencies are considered as part of the formal project programme.
