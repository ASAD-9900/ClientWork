What Is Feng Shui in Interior Design?

Applying Traditional Principles to the Way a Space Is Planned

Feng Shui is the ancient practice of aligning human living environments with natural harmony. At Oraanj, these considerations sit alongside practical architectural space planning.

01 Placement

Furniture Placement

Arranging key items—beds, desks, and sofas—in secure positions with clear sightlines to entryways without sitting directly in door paths.

02 Circulation

Intuitive Flow & Pathways

Eliminating awkward pinch-points, congested corridors, and visual obstacles to allow uninhibited movement throughout the home.

03 Daylight

Light & Air Quality

Harnessing natural morning illumination, cross-ventilation, and layered warm artificial lighting to sustain daytime vitality.

04 Materiality

Elemental Balance

Balancing tactile textures—natural timber, brushed metals, woven textiles, stone, and glass—for a grounding, peaceful ambience.

Feng Shui for the Bedroom

Create a Room That Promotes Restorative, Unbroken Sleep

The bedroom is where you spend a third of your life recharging. We balance bed placement, headboard grounding, and acoustic calm to foster profound rest.

01 Command

Commanding Bed Placement

Positioning the bed diagonally to the entrance so the doorway is visible, while ensuring feet don't align directly in door paths.

02 Headboard

Solid Headboard Grounding

Selecting substantial upholstered or timber headboards secured against solid internal walls rather than floating under open windows.

03 Symmetry

Bilateral Bedside Balance

Matching bedside tables and lamps on both sides of the bed to promote equality, relational harmony, and comfortable symmetry.

04 Atmosphere

Subdued Sensory Atmosphere

Avoiding mirrors that reflect the bed directly, using blackout drapery, and eliminating electrical chargers from immediate pillow zones.

Home Office & The Command Position

Fostering Focus, Authoritative Composure and Career Clarity

Where you sit to work dictates your subconscious focus. Sitting with your back to an open door breeds low-level stress; sitting in the command position empowers decisive concentration.

1. Facing Into the Room with Solid Wall Support

Positioning your desk so you can see anyone entering the room, backed by a handsome architectural joinery wall or substantial bookcase.

2. Natural Light Without Harsh Screen Reflections

Angling workspaces perpendicular to windows to harness natural daylight for alertness while preventing disruptive screen glare.

3. Concealed Wiring & Dedicated Document Storage

Eliminating tangled electrical cables and towering paper piles with integrated joinery drawers, promoting an uncluttered mental canvas.

Feng Shui Design Questions

Sensible, practical answers to common questions about applying Feng Shui principles to London properties.

Feng Shui FAQ
[accordion] [accordion-item title="Will my home look Asian or 'themed' if we apply Feng Shui?"]

No. Feng Shui is not a decorative style. We apply traditional principles of orientation, circulation, light, and balance to your chosen aesthetic—whether that is Scandinavian minimal, British heritage, contemporary luxury, or eclectic.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Where should my bed go according to Feng Shui?"]

Ideally, your bed is positioned in the 'commanding position'—diagonally opposite the bedroom door so you can see entryways from bed, backed by a solid wall, without feet pointing directly out the doorway ('coffin position'). We balance this against windows, radiator locations, and wardrobe clearances.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can I use Feng Shui principles without replacing all my furniture?"]

Yes. Many significant improvements come from simply repositioning your existing furniture, decluttering circulation routes, altering mirror placements, and softening lighting schemes rather than buying new items.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Where should a home office desk be positioned?"]

A desk should ideally face into the room with a solid wall behind you and a clear view of the door. Avoid sitting with your back to an open doorway, as this creates subconscious tension and disrupts sustained focus.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Does Feng Shui guarantee better health, wealth or relationships?"]

No. We do not present Feng Shui as a mystical guarantee of health, wealth, or personal outcomes. Our work focuses on creating a demonstrably calmer, more functional, and harmonious physical interior that supports the way you live every day.

[/accordion-item] [accordion-item title="Can Feng Shui be incorporated into commercial offices and workspaces?"]

Yes. In commercial environments, Feng Shui principles help optimize reception flow, executive desk placement, team collaboration zones, and acoustic privacy, fostering a productive and welcoming atmosphere for staff and clients.
