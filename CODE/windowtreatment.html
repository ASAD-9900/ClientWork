Fabric & Process

Choosing the Right Fabric & Our Design Process

Designed Alongside Your Furniture, Colour Palette & Lighting

Two fabrics in the same colour can behave completely differently at a window. We evaluate texture, weight, drape, opacity, and how physical samples respond to light in your room.

1. Connected to the Wider Interior

We consider how your window treatments relate to wall colours, furniture, rugs, lighting, flooring, and bespoke joinery—planning them early before renovations finish.

2. Accurate Site Measurement & Verification

Measurements are affected by recess depth, radiators, cornices, window handles, and joinery. Final dimensions for manufacture are verified by the appointed specialist supplier or installer before production.

3. From Samples & Specification to Procurement

Fees are tailored to the number and complexity of windows, with optional fabric sourcing, procurement, and specialist installation coordination according to your appointment.
