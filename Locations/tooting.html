Architectural Guidance · Tooting

How Can You Make an Older Tooting House Feel Brighter and More Spacious?

Many Victorian and Edwardian properties in Tooting have wonderful character, but narrow hallways and compartmentalised rooms can sometimes feel dark or enclosed.

You do not always need to knock down every wall to achieve a light, open feeling. By introducing wider doorways, glazed internal doors and reflective light paint tones, daylight can travel deeper into the ground floor.

Through our turnkey interior design and build service, we plan furniture proportions carefully so movement routes stay clear and rooms feel generous rather than crowded.

Layout & Spatial Optimisation · Tooting

Should You Open Up the Kitchen or Keep a Separate Front Sitting Room?

Many homeowners love the idea of an open-plan kitchen and dining area at the rear, but preserving a cosy, peaceful front sitting room often creates the ideal balance for family life.

A rear kitchen-diner connecting out to the garden provides the perfect hub for weekend family breakfasts, cooking and entertaining. Meanwhile, keeping the front reception room separate gives you a tranquil space in the evening away from the bustle of appliances and toys.

Our tailored kitchen renovation and build schemes explore both possibilities with 3D layouts, ensuring worktop runs, dining seating and garden sightlines work comfortably together.
