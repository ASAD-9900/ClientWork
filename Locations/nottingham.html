Architectural Guidance · Nottingham

How Can You Make a Nottingham City-Centre Apartment Feel More Spacious?

Start with furniture proportions, clear circulation and storage that suits the room.

A large sofa or dining table can make an apartment difficult to move through, even when there appears to be enough floor space.

We test furniture dimensions and positions before finalising selections. Storage is planned around the items you need to accommodate, while architectural lighting design helps make each part of the room useful.

In an open-plan apartment, a defined seating area and compact dining arrangement can bring order without adding partitions.

Layout & Spatial Optimisation · Nottingham

Can You Create a Home Workspace in an Open-Plan Apartment?

Yes. A defined workspace can fit into the wider room when its dimensions and storage are carefully considered.

A compact desk, suitable chair and task lighting provide a starting point. Socket positions, daylight and screen glare also influence where the workspace should sit.

Storage for paperwork and equipment helps the room feel settled when the working day ends. Tailored joinery and custom furniture design can connect the desk with the rest of the interior.

We consider the workspace alongside dining and seating so each activity has sufficient room.

Joinery, Materials & Details · Nottingham

What Should You Check Before Refurbishing a Leasehold Apartment in Nottingham?

Check your lease and building management requirements before committing to the works.

The proposed scope may affect what permissions, drawings or contractor information are needed.

Delivery access, lift dimensions, permitted working hours and protection of communal areas can also influence the installation programme. Flooring changes may need particular attention where sound transmission is a concern.

We identify these coordination requirements early and establish responsibilities within the appointment, working alongside your appointed team where agreed through our apartment renovation and refurbishment services.
