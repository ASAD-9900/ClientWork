The Dalston Restaurant & Bar: Kingsland Road

A Characterful East London Venue with Terracotta Banquettes & Olive Joinery

A contemporary neighbourhood restaurant and cocktail bar refurbishment designed to feel like an authentic, established East London venue.

The brief required converting a ground-floor commercial unit into a 56-cover restaurant with an intimate bar. We retained and sealed original London stock brickwork, introduced bespoke terracotta-upholstered perimeter banquettes, and installed dark-stained oak tables. A compact olive-green fluted bar counter anchors the entrance, complemented by warm brass sconces and focused lighting.
