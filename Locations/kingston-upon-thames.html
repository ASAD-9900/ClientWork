How Can You Make the Most of River Views Without Compromising the Layout?

Plan furniture and circulation alongside the view.

In a riverside home or business, windows can influence the arrangement, but the space still needs practical storage, comfortable seating and clear movement routes.

We explore furniture heights, orientation and the relationship between window seating and the rest of the room. Glare, privacy and window treatments also need consideration.
