Specialist residential interior design, spatial planning, and architectural refurbishment for Grade II* listed apartments and penthouses in the Barbican Estate, City of London.

The Barbican Estate is one of the world's most celebrated examples of Brutalist architecture. Designed by Chamberlin, Powell and Bon, its distinctive bush-hammered concrete, dramatic perimeter glazing, and iconic layouts require an interior design studio with deep respect for architectural heritage, strict estate covenants, and listed building consents.

Oraanj delivers considered residential interior design across the Barbican Estate and the wider City of London. Whether planning the sensitive refurbishment of a highwalk apartment, reorganising a tower flat, or designing bespoke joinery for a duplex penthouse, we balance the robust texture of structural concrete with warm American walnut, honed natural stone, tactile upholstery, and indirect lighting.

Barbican Residence Interior Design

Grade II* Listed Brutalist Apartment Transformation

Our full refurbishment of this Barbican Estate home demonstrates how Brutalist architecture can be paired with refined domestic comfort.

The project centred on establishing an open-plan living, kitchen, and dining environment. A central honed Calacatta marble island provides practical prep space with induction cooking and breakfast bar seating on custom cognac leather stools. Warm American walnut cabinetry softens the bush-hammered concrete backdrop, while multi-circuit architectural track lighting and concealed LED channels illuminate the textured walls.

Every element was engineered in compliance with Barbican Estate Office regulations, incorporating high-performance 43dB acoustic floor underlays and non-invasive joinery fixings.

Because the Barbican Estate is Grade II* listed, alterations to internal layouts, original joinery, kitchens, and bathrooms require formal Listed Building Consent from the City of London. We prepare the complete heritage documentation and coordinate with the Barbican Estate Office. [/toggle] [toggle title="Can you fix new joinery and lighting into Barbican concrete walls?" state="closed"] Fixing directly into bush-hammered structural concrete is strictly regulated to prevent damage to the historic fabric and embedded heating elements. We design freestanding or battened architectural joinery solutions that anchor safely without compromising the concrete. [/toggle] [toggle title="What acoustic flooring regulations apply to Barbican residences?" state="closed"] The Barbican Estate Office enforces strict acoustic regulations to prevent impact noise transfer between flats.
