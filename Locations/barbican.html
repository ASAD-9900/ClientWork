The Barbican Estate is one of the world's most celebrated examples of Brutalist architecture. Designed by Chamberlin, Powell and Bon, its distinctive bush-hammered concrete, dramatic perimeter glazing, and iconic layouts require an interior design studio with deep respect for architectural heritage, strict estate covenants, and listed building consents.

Oraanj delivers considered residential interior design across the Barbican Estate and the wider City of London. Whether planning the sensitive refurbishment of a highwalk apartment, reorganising a tower flat, or designing bespoke joinery for a duplex penthouse, we balance the robust texture of structural concrete with warm American walnut, honed natural stone, tactile upholstery, and indirect lighting.

Barbican Residence Interior Design

Our full refurbishment of this Barbican Estate home demonstrates how Brutalist architecture can be paired with refined domestic comfort.

Estate Regulations & Listed Building Consent in the Barbican

Navigating City of London Guidelines and the Barbican Estate Office

Because the entire Barbican complex is Grade II* listed, almost any interior alteration, including kitchen modernisations, bathroom updates, and partition modifications, requires Listed Building Consent from the City of London Planning Department.

Furthermore, works must adhere to the Barbican Listed Building Management Guidelines and Estate Office regulations, which govern work hours, acoustic underlays, and structural fixings into concrete. Oraanj handles these specialized consents completely.
