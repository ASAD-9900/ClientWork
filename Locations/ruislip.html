Layout & Spatial Optimisation · Ruislip

Should You Combine the Kitchen and Dining Room or Keep Them Separate?

The right arrangement depends on how your household uses both spaces.

A connected kitchen and dining area may make family meals and entertaining easier. A separate dining room may remain useful for larger gatherings, homework or occasional work.

We compare layouts with cabinetry and furniture in place, considering storage, noise and routes to the garden. A wider doorway or partial connection may sometimes provide the improvement you need.

Any proposed structural changes require appropriate assessment.

Joinery, Materials & Details · Ruislip

How Can You Improve a Narrow Hallway Without Making It Feel Crowded?

Prioritise clear movement and storage of the right depth.

A hallway needs to accommodate doors opening, people passing and everyday belongings. Deep cupboards or oversized furniture can make it harder to use.

We assess the items you need to store before exploring hooks, shallow cabinetry, benches or under-stair solutions. Lighting, mirrors and wall finishes can then support the overall arrangement.
