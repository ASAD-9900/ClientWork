Joinery, Materials & Details · Knightsbridge

Where Does Bespoke Joinery Make the Greatest Difference?

Where standard furniture does not adequately address the dimensions or storage requirements.

Alcoves, dressing areas, entrance spaces and living-room walls can benefit from a tailored arrangement. The design should begin with the belongings and equipment it needs to accommodate.

We consider cupboard depth, internal fittings, access, ventilation where needed and the relationship with surrounding furniture. This helps establish whether bespoke cabinetry, freestanding pieces or a combination offers the most useful solution.
