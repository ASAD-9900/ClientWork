What Should You Consider When Renovating in Totteridge?

Much of Totteridge falls within the Totteridge Conservation Area and Metropolitan Green Belt, requiring careful navigation of local planning guidelines and heritage protections when undertaking alterations or extensions.

Working with Barnet planning authorities to secure approvals for extensions, fenestration alterations, and boundary improvements while respecting historic vernacular.

Navigating strict limitations on external footprint expansion by maximising subterranean space planning, lightwells, and internal volume reconfigurations.

Integrating secure perimeter gates, discreet surveillance, extensive home automation, and dedicated staff quarters within luxury residential footprints.
