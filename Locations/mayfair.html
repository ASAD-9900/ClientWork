Architectural Guidance · Mayfair

How Can You Refresh a Mayfair Period Home While Retaining Its Character?

Begin with the architectural features that give the rooms their identity.

Cornices, panelling, fireplaces and doorways can influence furniture placement, lighting and material choices. Contemporary furniture can sit comfortably alongside original details when proportions and finishes are carefully considered.

We explore how the rooms should function before refining the decorative scheme. Where proposed alterations may affect protected features, appropriate specialist advice and permissions should be established before work begins.

How Can a Private Office Feel Welcoming While Remaining Practical?

Plan working requirements and visitor comfort together.

A private office may need space for concentrated work, confidential meetings and informal conversations. Desk placement, seating, storage and equipment should reflect how frequently each activity occurs.

We consider power access, lighting and technology alongside furniture and finishes. Where acoustic privacy or specialist systems are required, the appropriate input should inform the design.
