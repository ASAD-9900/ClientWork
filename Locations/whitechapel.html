Architectural Guidance · Whitechapel

How Can You Make a Whitechapel Apartment Feel More Spacious?

Start with furniture scale, storage and clear movement routes.

Oversized seating, deep cupboards and poorly positioned dining furniture can make a room difficult to use. We test dimensions before recommending individual pieces.

Storage should reflect what you own, while architectural lighting design and window treatments support the room’s proportions and available daylight.

A considered arrangement can make the existing floor area more useful without requiring every item to be bespoke.

How Can You Retain Warehouse Character While Making the Interior Comfortable?

Let the existing structure guide the scheme, then introduce softer elements where needed.

Through sensitive interior architecture and refurbishment, brickwork, beams and columns influence furniture placement and material choices, while upholstery, rugs and curtains balance harder surfaces.

We consider lighting, storage and the relationship between different activities alongside the architectural features. The condition and treatment of existing fabric should be assessed by appropriate specialists before work begins.
