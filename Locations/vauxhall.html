Licence to Alter (LTA) & Building Regulations in Vauxhall Towers

Managing High-Rise Building Logistics with Zero Friction

Renovating an apartment in buildings such as St George Wharf or Riverlight requires compliance with strict leasehold covenants. Landlords and management agents demand formal Licence to Alter approval prior to flooring replacement, structural adjustments, or MEP alterations.

Oraanj handles this administrative burden entirely on your behalf. We prepare detailed architectural drawing packages, structural engineering approvals, acoustic underlay certifications (ensuring airborne and impact sound criteria are satisfied), and contractor insurance documentation.
