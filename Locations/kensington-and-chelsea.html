Bespoke Residential Architecture · Kensington and Chelsea

A Considered Approach to Your Property

A Kensington mansion flat may need better storage and a more comfortable furniture arrangement. A Chelsea townhouse may benefit from a clearer connection between its reception rooms. A mews home may need to accommodate living, working and entertaining within a smaller footprint.

We begin with your individual property and the way you use it. We consider its proportions, natural light, existing features and practical limitations alongside the atmosphere you want to create.

You can approach us with an established brief or simply a floor plan, photographs and a list of things you would like to improve.

Living and Dining Rooms

We plan seating, dining furniture, lighting and storage around everyday comfort and entertaining. Furniture dimensions and circulation are considered early, helping each room feel balanced and easy to use.

Bedrooms and Dressing Spaces

Bed positions, wardrobe access, bedside lighting and dressing areas are developed together. We compare fitted cabinetry and freestanding furniture to find an arrangement suited to your room and storage requirements.

Kitchens and Bathrooms

We develop layouts and material schemes around daily routines, maintenance and the available space. Cabinetry, appliances, fittings and lighting are considered before the main selections are confirmed.

Whole-Home Furnishing and Refurbishment

For larger projects, we establish a design direction that connects adjoining rooms through colour, materials and selected details. Each room can retain its own character while contributing to a coherent home.

Alongside our architectural interior work in Kensington and Chelsea, our studio regularly undertakes residential and commercial commissions across neighbouring areas including Knightsbridge, Mayfair, and Richmond upon Thames.

Architectural Guidance · Kensington and Chelsea

How Can You Modernise a Period Home Without Losing Its Character?

Start with the features that give the property its identity.

Cornices, fireplaces, panelling and room proportions can guide furniture placement, lighting and material choices. Contemporary furniture and finishes can sit comfortably alongside original details when their scale and relationship are carefully considered.

We explore how the rooms should function before developing the decorative scheme. Where proposed changes may affect protected features, the necessary specialist advice and permissions should be established before work begins.

Layout & Spatial Optimisation · Kensington and Chelsea

How Can You Improve Storage in a Kensington Mansion Flat?

Plan storage around specific belongings and daily routines.

Coats, luggage, clothing, paperwork and household equipment need different cupboard dimensions and access. We assess these requirements before recommending fitted cabinetry or freestanding furniture.

Entrance cupboards, bedroom wardrobes and living-room storage should support the room’s main activities while allowing comfortable movement. Building management requirements should also be checked early where refurbishment work is proposed.
