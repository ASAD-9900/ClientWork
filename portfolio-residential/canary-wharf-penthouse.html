Canary Wharf Contemporary Residence

A contemporary docklands residence designed for warmth and architectural depth

This Canary Wharf penthouse features a contemporary interior with warm, earthy colours and natural materials. Terracotta tones, walnut finishes, brushed brass and textured stone bring warmth and character to the open-plan living spaces, while the large windows provide natural light and views across London.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to interior designer in Canary Wharf, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

The layout includes a main living area for entertaining, a cosy reading corner, a dining space and a bespoke bar area. The bathrooms continue the same material palette, with terracotta finishes, stone surfaces and brass fittings creating a comfortable and considered design throughout the apartment.

Our team delivered tailored luxury interior designers in London, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Docklands Living & Detailing

Spatial Organisation, Material Contrast & Considered Lighting

The redesign introduces residential warmth, acoustic comfort, and practical storage across a versatile layout.

Modern docklands residences can easily feel stark without careful acoustic dampening. We mitigated this by introducing textured lime plasters, recessed floor-to-ceiling drapery, and wool-silk area rugs over acoustic underlay. In the bathrooms, handmade terracotta tiles and brushed brass fittings introduce tactile warmth, supported by concealed mirrored cabinets with anti-fog heating, integrated power outlets, and low-level night lighting.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated residential interior design London, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Spatial Planning

Zoned Living Areas

Bespoke joinery partitions and low-profile furniture arrangements define distinct living and dining zones while maintaining natural daylight throughout.

02 Material Specification

Travertine & Walnut Joinery

Honed Roman travertine surfaces and dark American walnut cabinetry absorb natural glare, introducing tactile warmth and long-term durability.

03 Bathrooms & Storage

Integrated Bathroom Cabinetry

Canary Wharf Penthouse · Brief, Challenges & Investment

Client Requirements, Design Challenges & Indicative Investment

Every project undertaken by Oraanj is defined by a tailored architectural brief, rigorous site problem-solving, and transparent, realistic financial planning.

01 Client Requirements

Contemporary Skyline Living & Warm Tactile Luxury

The client desired an inviting, cosmopolitan docklands home that counterbalanced cold glazing with warm organic materials, flexible entertainment zones, and understated residential comfort.

  • Zoned open-plan layout distinguishing intimate reading niches, dining areas, and a cocktail bar.
  • Tactile material palette featuring honed Roman travertine, American walnut, and brushed brass.
  • Bespoke concealed storage throughout entrance corridors and master dressing suites.
02 Challenges & Solutions

High-Rise Acoustic Glare & Vertical Freight Restrictions

Designing within a post-tensioned high-rise structure required solving severe solar heat gain, echo off curtain glass, and strict tower delivery windows.

  • Acoustic reverberation neutralized using multi-layer lime plasters, acoustic underlays, and custom wool-silk rugs.
  • Motorized dual solar-control screens recessed seamlessly into ceiling architectural troughs.
  • Off-site prefabricated modular joinery sized precisely for high-speed tower service elevator dimensions.
