Edwardian Heritage & Contemporary Renewal

A distinguished North London Edwardian villa balancing preserved architectural character and modern family living

Perched on the hilltops of Muswell Hill, this substantial five-bedroom Edwardian residence required a complete architectural refurbishment. The owners wished to honour the home's historic integrity whilst configuring an airy, light-filled layout better suited to contemporary family life and entertaining.

Our approach preserved and restored the original ceiling cornicing, deep skirting boards, and timber sash windows throughout the formal reception rooms. To the rear, a full-width kitchen-dining extension creates an open culinary zone with bespoke sage-green cabinetry, fluted marble island counters, and Crittall-style doors overlooking the garden. Tailored joinery throughout the bedrooms and luxurious microcement and marble bathrooms complete a balanced, enduring design scheme.
