Making More of a Compact London Home

This Hampstead apartment design focuses on how a smaller home can accommodate relaxing, dining, working and storage within a coordinated interior.

Pale timber tones, simple cabinetry and restrained finishes create a consistent design direction. Concealed cupboards reduce visual clutter, while a built-in dining bench makes use of the room’s perimeter.

The approach gives practical decisions—such as cupboard openings, furniture dimensions and routes between rooms—as much attention as colour and styling.

Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise feng shui interior spatial alignment.

01 Challenge

Accommodating Several Activities within a Small Footprint

Living, dining and working arrangements can compete for the same floor space.

Design Response

Develop the furniture layout and storage together. Position larger elements around the room’s perimeter where appropriate, preserving usable central space and clear routes between rooms.

02 Challenge

The benefit is a layout that supports daily routines without relying on moving furniture whenever the room changes function.

Introducing Storage without Overwhelming the Interior Multiple freestanding cupboards can make a compact apartment feel fragmented.

Design Response

Coordinate fitted storage into a consistent run of cabinetry, combining closed cupboards with selected display shelving. Internal arrangements should respond to the items being stored rather than appearance alone.

03 Challenge

An integrated desk can provide a work setting that closes away when it is no longer needed.

Making Room for Dining A conventional dining arrangement requires space for the table, chairs and movement around them.

Design Response

Use a built-in banquette where the room’s dimensions support it. Under-seat storage adds another function, while the table and remaining chairs can be selected around the available clearance.

04 Challenge

Seating capacity should be confirmed from the measured layout before being advertised.

Keeping Circulation Areas Useful and Uncluttered Corridors can become congested when storage projects into walking routes.

Design Response

Check cupboard depths, door swings and access requirements together. Coordinate finishes between the hallway and adjacent rooms to create visual continuity.

05 Challenge

Planning a Practical Shower Room

A compact bathroom requires careful coordination of sanitaryware, storage and access.

Design Response

Establish the shower enclosure, basin position and usable clearances before selecting finishes. Detailed design should also address ventilation, waterproofing, maintenance and access to concealed components.

06 Challenge

01 Investment Guide

What Would a Similar Hampstead Apartment Refurbishment Cost?

£120,000–£300,000 excluding VAT.

This is a broad planning allowance for a substantial internal refurbishment involving an agreed combination of kitchen and shower-room replacement, fitted joinery, internal finishes, lighting, furniture and installation.

  • Design and technical coordination: Survey, layouts, drawings and specifications
  • Kitchen: Cabinetry, worktops, appliances and installation
  • Shower room: Sanitaryware, fittings, finishes and associated works
  • Fitted furniture: Storage walls, desk, shelving and banquette
  • Internal refurbishment: Flooring, decoration and agreed services alterations
  • Furniture and lighting: New items, fittings, delivery and installation
  • Floor area, property condition, joinery requirements and material choices will determine the tailored budget.: Major structural alterations, external works and communal building works are excluded. Professional fees, approvals and building-management charges should be identified separately in the cost plan.

Allow a separate contingency of 10–15%, reviewed following survey. VAT should be added where applicable.

02 Delivery Schedule

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

5–9 months

For initial planning, allow approximately 5–9 months, with design, procurement and other activities overlapping where practical.

  • Brief, measured survey and initial budget: 2–3 weeks
  • Space planning and concept design: 3–6 weeks
  • Detailed design and specification: 4–8 weeks
  • Approvals, where required: Confirm for the proposed work
  • Procurement and joinery manufacture: 8–14 weeks or longer for selected items
  • Refurbishment and installation: 8–14 weeks
  • Snagging and handover: 1–2 weeks
