This Knightsbridge residence scheme brings together practical storage, comfortable furniture and a coordinated material palette.

Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise bathroom renovation company solutions.

01 Challenge

Balancing Connected Rooms with Privacy

Reception and dining rooms may need to function together for entertaining and separately during everyday use.

Design Response

Assess doorway positions, furniture layouts and opportunities for suitable closing doors. Any proposed alteration to openings must be coordinated with the existing structure and relevant approval requirements.

02 Challenge

Introducing Storage without Dominating the Rooms

Large cabinetry can feel visually heavy if its proportions are unrelated to the architecture.

Design Response

Align joinery with the room’s dimensions and architectural features. Combine selected display shelving with closed cupboards, allowing decorative objects and everyday storage to serve different roles.

03 Challenge

Integrating Media Equipment and Lighting

Cables, controls and equipment can interrupt an otherwise carefully planned interior.

Design Response

Coordinate equipment dimensions, ventilation, power and maintenance access before joinery manufacture. Integrated lighting should be detailed with accessible drivers and controls.

04 Challenge

Selecting Finishes for Family Use

Stone, timber and textured fabrics each have different maintenance requirements.

Design Response

Review samples and supplier guidance against the intended use. Flooring should be assessed for suitability in entrances and circulation areas, while upholstery should reflect the household’s expectations for cleaning and durability.

05 Challenge

Creating Different Lighting Settings

A single lighting arrangement rarely suits daytime activities, reading and evening entertaining equally well.

Design Response

Plan ambient, task and accent lighting together, with controls appropriate to the room. Confirm dimming compatibility and fitting positions during detailed design.

  • Internal refurbishment: Flooring, decoration and agreed alterations

  • Fitted joinery: Display shelving, media storage, wardrobes and internal fittings
  • Electrical and lighting works: Fittings, controls and agreed services alterations
  • Furniture and furnishings: Seating, dining furniture, window treatments and rugs
  • Installation and handover: Delivery, fitting, styling and snagging

Major structural alterations, extensions, external works and whole-property services replacement are excluded from this illustrative scope.

02 Delivery Schedule

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

7–12 months

For initial planning, allow approximately 7–12 months for a substantial internal refurbishment, with several stages overlapping.

  • Brief, surveys and feasibility: 3–4 weeks
  • Space planning and concept design: 4–8 weeks
  • Detailed design, specification and tendering: 6–10 weeks
  • Approvals, where required: Confirm for the proposed work
  • Procurement and joinery manufacture: 10–18 weeks or longer for selected items
  • Refurbishment and installation: 12–20 weeks
  • Final styling, snagging and handover: 2–3 weeks
