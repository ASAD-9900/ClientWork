Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions.

01 Challenge

Connecting Period Rooms with Contemporary Spaces

Older reception rooms and a contemporary rear addition can feel unrelated when their finishes are developed separately.

Design Response

Use a shared palette and selected recurring details to connect the spaces. Sage tones, warm timber and brass accents provide continuity, while furniture and lighting respond to each room’s proportions.

02 Challenge

Respecting Decorative Architectural Features

Cornicing, fireplaces and traditional joinery need room to remain visible within an updated interior.

Design Response

Position furniture and proposed cabinetry around those features. Assess their condition before specifying repair, alteration or replacement, with specialist input where needed.

03 Challenge

Balancing Open-Plan Living with Quieter Areas

A large kitchen and dining space supports entertaining, but a family home also benefits from places for conversation, reading and relaxation.

Design Response

Give each room a clear purpose and consider how the spaces connect. Furniture arrangements, lighting and changes in texture can establish different settings without relying on additional partitions.

04 Challenge

Planning Storage without Overcrowding Bedrooms

Large wardrobes and poorly positioned furniture can reduce usable space.

Design Response

Plan bed position, circulation, and storage requirements together. Develop fitted furniture around the room’s dimensions, checking door openings, bedside access and internal storage before manufacture.

05 Challenge

Coordinating Improvements to an Older Property

Existing floors, walls, windows and services may affect the proposed work.

Design Response

Establish their condition through appropriate surveys before finalising the specification. Assess any insulation, glazing, or services upgrades for the specific building rather than selecting them based on appearance alone.

Budget & Delivery

Indicative Investment & Programme Guide

Transparent cost planning, realistic schedule benchmarks, and coordinated turnkey execution.

01 Investment Guide

What Would a Similar Muswell Hill Refurbishment Cost?

£180,000–£400,000 excluding VAT.

This allowance applies to a substantial internal refurbishment across an agreed selection of principal rooms. It may include interior design, kitchen replacement, bathroom improvements, fitted joinery, internal finishes, furniture, lighting and installation.

  • Design and technical coordination: Surveys, layouts, drawings and specifications
  • Period-feature repairs: Condition, repair requirements and specialist involvement
  • Kitchen: Cabinetry, worktops, appliances and installation
  • Bathrooms: Number of rooms, fittings, finishes and services alterations
  • Fitted joinery: Wardrobes, shelving and other agreed storage
  • Internal refurbishment: Flooring, decoration and agreed electrical or plumbing work
  • Furniture and installation: Items retained, new purchases, delivery and placement
  • The final budget depends on floor area, existing condition, specification and the extent of work.: Professional fees, statutory charges, external works and any major services replacement should be identified explicitly in the tailored cost plan. Allow a separate contingency of 10–15%, reviewed following survey. VAT should be added where applicable.
02 Delivery Schedule

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

7–12 months

For an internal refurbishment of this scale, an initial programme could allow approximately 7–12 months, with some stages overlapping.

  • Brief, survey and feasibility: 3–5 weeks
  • Space planning and concept design: 4–8 weeks
  • Detailed design, specification and tendering: 6–10 weeks
  • Approvals, where required: Confirm for the proposed scope
  • Procurement and joinery manufacture: 8–16 weeks or longer for selected items
  • Refurbishment and installation: 12–20 weeks
  • Final styling, snagging and handover: 2–3 weeks
