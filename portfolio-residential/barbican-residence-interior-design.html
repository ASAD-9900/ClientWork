The Design Objectives

The supplied brief centres on balancing architectural character with the practical requirements of city living.

  • Create clearly defined areas for cooking, dining and relaxing.
  • Introduce storage without overwhelming the living space.
  • Bring warmth through timber tones, upholstery and contrasting textures.
  • Develop lighting suited to both daytime activities and evening relaxation.
  • Coordinate proposed alterations with the building’s existing fabric and services.

Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise feng shui interior design.

01 Challenge

Organising Connected Living Spaces

An apartment’s shared spaces need to accommodate several activities while retaining comfortable routes between them.

Design Response

Plan the kitchen, dining furniture and seating together. Check furniture dimensions, chair clearances and cupboard openings before finalising the layout.

02 Challenge

This helps avoid an attractive arrangement becoming awkward in daily use.

Introducing Storage without Visual Clutter Large storage units can dominate an apartment if their proportions and placement are considered too late.

Design Response

Develop cabinetry around specific requirements, including media equipment, household belongings and kitchen storage. Combine closed cupboards with selected display areas to keep the overall composition balanced.

03 Challenge

Bringing Warmth to a Strong Architectural Interior

A restrained palette needs sufficient variation to feel comfortable rather than flat.

Design Response

Pair warm timber tones with lighter surfaces, cognac-coloured upholstery and tactile fabrics. Use rugs and upholstered seating to introduce softness without claiming an untested level of acoustic performance.

04 Challenge

Coordinating Lighting with the Building Fabric

Lighting proposals must respond to the existing ceiling construction, service routes and permitted alterations.

Design Response

Assess suitable surface-mounted fittings, lamps and integrated joinery lighting during detailed design. Confirm fixing methods and electrical requirements before installation.

05 Challenge

Planning a Sensitive Refurbishment

Before work begins, the project team should establish the property’s applicable heritage, lease and building-management requirements.

Design Response

Identify the relevant approvals, survey existing services and agree the proposed interventions before committing to manufacture or site work. Access arrangements, working hours and protection of communal areas should also inform the programme.

These are design considerations for this type of project, rather than verified accounts of difficulties encountered at this property.

Budget & Delivery

Indicative Investment & Programme Guide

Transparent cost planning, realistic schedule benchmarks, and coordinated turnkey execution.

01 Investment Guide

What Would a Similar Barbican Refurbishment Cost?

£80,000–£180,000 excluding VAT.

This is an initial planning allowance for a defined apartment refurbishment, potentially including interior design, kitchen improvements, selected fitted joinery, internal finishes, lighting, furniture and installation.

  • Design and technical coordination: Survey, layouts, drawings and specifications
  • Kitchen: Cabinetry, worktops, appliances and installation
  • Fitted joinery: Storage requirements, dimensions and finishes
  • Internal finishes: Floor treatments, preparation and decoration
  • Electrical and lighting works: Agreed alterations and fittings
  • Furniture and installation: Seating, dining furniture, rugs and delivery

The range must be reviewed against the apartment’s floor area, condition and agreed scope. Extensive bespoke cabinetry, premium natural stone, bathroom replacement or significant services work may require a higher allowance.

02 Delivery Schedule

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

5–9 months

For initial planning, allow approximately 5–9 months, with several stages overlapping.

  • Brief, survey and feasibility: 2–4 weeks
  • Layouts and concept design: 4–6 weeks
  • Detailed design and specification: 4–8 weeks
  • Approval process: Confirm separately for the proposed work
  • Procurement and joinery manufacture: 8–14 weeks or longer for selected items
  • Refurbishment and installation: 8–14 weeks
  • Snagging and handover: 1–2 weeks
