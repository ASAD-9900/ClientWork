Marylebone Townhouse Architecture & Interiors

A grand Marylebone townhouse celebrating modern classical proportions, bespoke joinery, and serene materiality

This full-scale Marylebone townhouse renovation unites period architectural grace with tailored modern luxury. Conceived across every floor plate, the scheme harmonises classic ceiling mouldings and grand proportions with bespoke fluted millwork, hand-finished wall panelling, and a calming palette of warm ecru, taupe, and brushed champagne brass.

Every zone—from the double-height formal reception and sculptural marble island kitchen to the boutique walk-in dressing sanctuaries, restful bedroom suites, and private spa bathrooms—was engineered for elevated family living and effortless hospitality.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Modern Classic Elegance Across Every Floor

Every room within this Marylebone residence was curated as part of a harmonious architectural continuum, marrying refined heritage with modern ease.

From bespoke floor-to-ceiling joinery with integrated recessed LED lighting to tailored dressing rooms, book-matched marble features, and artisanal metalwork, every square metre exemplifies prime central London luxury. Tactile bouclé, warm walnut, and subtle stone surfaces create a tranquil retreat amidst the vibrant capital.
