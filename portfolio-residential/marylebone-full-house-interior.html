A Connected Interior for Contemporary Family Living

This Marylebone house scheme brings shared living spaces and private rooms together through coordinated joinery, soft furnishings and stone finishes.

The Residential Design Brief

The supplied brief centres on adapting a townhouse interior for contemporary family use while retaining its architectural character.

  • Establish a consistent design direction across the house.
  • Coordinate furniture and fitted joinery.
  • Improve bedroom and clothing storage.
  • Balance shared living spaces with quieter private rooms.
  • Develop practical kitchen and bathroom arrangements.
  • Consider lighting, maintenance and everyday use alongside appearance.
  • The condition and status of any historic features should be established before alterations are specified.
Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise lighting design plans.

01 Challenge

Connecting Different Rooms

A whole-house scheme needs continuity without making every space identical.

Design Response

Establish a shared palette of timber tones, upholstery colours and selected details. Adjust the furniture, lighting and finishes to suit each room’s purpose.

02 Challenge

Balancing Storage with Comfortable Proportions

Large wardrobes and shelving can overwhelm a room if their dimensions are considered separately from the furniture.

Design Response

Plan the bed, seating, storage and circulation together. Coordinate joinery with door openings, windows and existing architectural details.

03 Challenge

Making the Dressing Room Practical

Display shelving and decorative seating should not reduce useful storage or access.

Design Response

Establish hanging, drawer, shoe and accessory requirements first. Check wardrobe openings and circulation before introducing an ottoman or central furniture.

04 Challenge

Working around Existing Architectural Features

Fireplaces, cornicing and other original details may influence furniture placement and proposed alterations.

Design Response

Survey their condition and establish any applicable restrictions before finalising the design. Obtain specialist advice where repair or alteration requires it.

05 Challenge

Coordinating Kitchen Materials with Daily Use

A statement island needs to support preparation, seating and cleaning.

Design Response

Resolve dimensions, equipment and circulation before selecting the surface. Review the exact stone or composite material against the household’s maintenance expectations.

06 Challenge

Planning the Bathroom around Selected Fittings

A freestanding bath affects circulation, cleaning access and service arrangements.

Design Response

Confirm the fitting’s dimensions, weight and installation requirements. Coordinate floor capacity, plumbing, ventilation and access with the relevant specialists.

These are design considerations relevant to the supplied scheme. Actual site discoveries, client revisions and completed restoration work require confirmation from project records.

Budget & Delivery

Indicative Investment & Programme Guide

Transparent cost planning, realistic schedule benchmarks, and coordinated turnkey execution.

01 Investment Guide

What Would a Similar Marylebone Refurbishment Cost?

£250,000–£600,000 excluding VAT.

This is an initial estimate for a substantial internal refurbishment involving an agreed combination of kitchen and bathroom replacement, fitted joinery, finishes, furniture, lighting and installation.

  • Design and technical coordination: Surveys, layouts, drawings and specifications
  • Internal refurbishment: Rooms included, preparation, flooring and decoration
  • Kitchen: Cabinetry, worktops, appliances and installation
  • Bathrooms: Number of rooms, fittings, finishes and services alterations
  • Fitted joinery: Wardrobes, dressing furniture, shelving and storage
  • Furniture and lighting: Specification, procurement and installation
  • Handover: Styling, testing, snagging and agreed documentation

Major structural alterations, extensions, external works, specialist conservation work and whole-house services replacement require separate assessment unless expressly included.

02 Delivery Schedule

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

8–14 months

For initial planning, allow approximately 8–14 months for a substantial coordinated refurbishment, with some stages overlapping.

  • Brief, surveys and feasibility: 3–5 weeks
  • Space planning and concept design: 4–8 weeks
  • Detailed design, specification and tendering: 6–12 weeks
  • Approvals, where required: Confirm for the property and proposed work
  • Procurement and joinery manufacture: 10–18 weeks or longer for selected items
  • Refurbishment and installation: 16–26 weeks
  • Final styling, snagging and handover: 2–3 weeks
