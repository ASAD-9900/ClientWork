Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise bathroom renovation company services.

01 Challenge

Connecting Existing Rooms with Proposed New Space

An extension needs to work with the original house in terms of circulation, proportions and use.

Design Response

Develop the existing and proposed layouts together. Resolve furniture positions, kitchen arrangements and routes to the garden before structural openings and services are finalised.

02 Challenge

Coordinating the Kitchen Island with Everyday Movement

A large island can become an obstacle if its dimensions are chosen before the surrounding layout.

Design Response

Establish preparation space, seating, appliance openings and circulation first. Confirm the island’s size and material from the coordinated drawings and approved specification.

03 Challenge

Integrating Storage Across Several Rooms

Separate storage decisions can create inconsistent finishes and missed opportunities.

Design Response

Plan kitchen, utility, media and wardrobe storage around the household’s actual requirements. Coordinate external finishes while allowing internal fittings to suit each use.

04 Challenge

Planning the Interior-to-Garden Transition

A visually continuous threshold also needs appropriate construction detailing.

Design Response

Coordinate internal floor levels, door systems, weather protection and external drainage with the relevant specialists. The final detail must respond to the site conditions and selected products.

05 Challenge

Keeping Design and Construction Decisions Aligned

Late changes to cabinetry, lighting or equipment can affect work already underway.

Design Response

Coordinate drawings and specifications before procurement. Record subsequent changes with their cost and programme implications before proceeding.

These are design considerations relevant to the supplied scheme. Actual structural solutions, site discoveries and occupation arrangements require confirmation from project records.

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

6–10 months

For initial planning, a substantial internal refurbishment could require approximately 6–10 months. A project also involving an extension could require 9–15 months or longer, depending on design development, approvals and construction scope.

  • Brief, surveys and feasibility: 3–5 weeks
  • Layout planning and concept design: 4–8 weeks
  • Technical design, specification and tendering: 6–12 weeks
  • Approvals, where required: Confirm for the proposed work
  • Procurement and joinery manufacture: 8–16 weeks or longer for selected items
  • Internal refurbishment and installation: 12–20 weeks
  • Extension and associated construction: Separately programmed against the defined scope
