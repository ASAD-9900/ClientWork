Richmond Residential Interior Architecture

A riverside sanctuary shaped by earthy serenity

Set within leafy Richmond, this holistic residential project celebrates organic warmth, natural materiality, and considered architectural zoning. Drawing inspiration from the borough’s riverside landscape and verdant parks, the palette balances sage wall panelling with rich American walnut joinery, travertine accents, and warm bronze hardware.

Open-plan living flows effortlessly into the culinary space, anchored by a dramatic marble waterfall island and bespoke timber cabinetry. Calming bedroom suites and a spa-like ensuite featuring a freestanding bathtub complete an enduring sanctuary designed for effortless family comfort.

Craftsmanship & Palette

Natural Materiality & Enduring Architectural Flow

Conceived to bridge indoor comfort with natural serenity, the residence unites earthy tones, tactile finishes, and tailored joinery.

The design language weaves tactile travertine stone, fluted American walnut, and soft olive upholstery across open living zones. Bespoke shelving with recessed architectural lighting showcases curated ceramics, while marble kitchen surfaces provide durable luxury for culinary entertaining.
