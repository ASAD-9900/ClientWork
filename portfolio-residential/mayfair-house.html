Wimbledon Residential Renovation

A substantial family home reconfigured for modern living and entertaining

This comprehensive renovation of a detached period residence in Wimbledon addressed circulation, daylight, and family storage across three storeys. We reconfigured the ground floor to create a natural progression from a formal entrance hall to an expansive open-plan kitchen, dining, and garden room, establishing direct sightlines into the landscaped garden.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to residential interior design London, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

Bespoke fluted wall panelling and integrated cabinetry define the main family living room, housing media equipment and books without visual noise. On the upper floors, we reorganised the master suite to incorporate a dedicated walk-in dressing room and a private bathroom featuring twin stone vanities and a walk-in shower enclosure.

Our team delivered tailored residential interior decor, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Spatial Reconfiguration, Custom Cabinetry & Durable Materiality

Tailored architectural interventions designed for the practical needs of an active family household.

The layout was engineered to support both busy school mornings and weekend hosting. Durable porcelain floor tiles in the kitchen transition into wide-plank engineered oak throughout the reception rooms, combining underfloor heating with easy long-term maintenance. In the master dressing room, floor-to-ceiling joinery provides dedicated sections for hanging, folded knitwear, and accessories, supported by integrated warm LED task lighting and a central upholstered island with soft-close jewellery drawers.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated interior design sourcing, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

Client Requirements, Design Challenges & Indicative Investment

Every project undertaken by Oraanj is defined by a tailored architectural brief, rigorous site problem-solving, and transparent, realistic financial planning.

01 Client Requirements

Contemporary Family Luxury with Sculptural Architectural Flow

A substantial modern townhouse requiring custom joinery, cohesive neutral palettes, and optimized space planning for family entertaining and work-from-home flexibility.

  • Sculptural bespoke media joinery with antiqued bronze details and concealed acoustic audio systems.
  • Open culinary dining zone connecting seamlessly to private landscaped garden patios.
  • Executive study retreat featuring acoustic leather paneling and concealed AV conference screens.
02 Challenges & Solutions

Vertical Circulation & Thermal Stratification

A multi-story stairwell caused significant heat stratification between lower living floors and upper bedrooms, alongside echo across open stair voids.

  • Multi-zone VRF climate sensors balancing vertical airflow across all four floorplates automatically.
  • Custom acoustic ceiling baffles and continuous wool carpet runners dampening stairwell reverberation.
  • Pocket door mechanisms allowing whole floors to be thermally and acoustically compartmentalized.
