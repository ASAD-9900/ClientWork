Wimbledon Residential Renovation

A full-scale Wimbledon transformation balancing period heritage, bespoke joinery, and tailored luxury

This comprehensive renovation of a classic Wimbledon residence reimagines prime South West London living through a lens of tailored sophistication and quiet luxury. Undertaken from concept to completion, the project harmonises timeless architectural proportions with modern family living, uniting bespoke cabinetry, fluted wall panelling, and a calming palette of warm neutrals, muted olive, and brushed brass.

Every space—from the light-filled entrance foyer and sculptural living room media wall to the bespoke dressing suites, open-plan kitchen, and spa-inspired bathrooms—was detailed with exacting precision. The result is an enduring home that delivers exceptional practical storage alongside elevated, hotel-grade residential comfort.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Artisan Joinery, Tailored Suites & Period Refinement

Designed to establish an enduring standard of Wimbledon elegance, every room combines bespoke architectural joinery with refined finishes calibrated for contemporary family life.

From the expansive walk-in dressing suites with backlit wardrobes to the marble living room media wall and cohesive open-plan dining space, the interior achieves architectural harmony throughout. Subtle brass metalwork, hand-selected natural stone, and tactile fabrics enrich the neutral palette, delivering understated luxury across every square foot.
