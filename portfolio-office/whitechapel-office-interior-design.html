Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise technical CAD plans.

01 Challenge

Balancing Collaboration with Concentration

Open-plan working needs to accommodate conversation without making focused tasks unnecessarily difficult.

Design Response

Position collaborative settings away from quieter desk areas where possible. Consider enclosed rooms, furniture placement and acoustic requirements as part of the overall layout.

02 Challenge

Providing Suitable Meeting Privacy

Glazed partitions preserve visual connection, but their appearance does not establish acoustic performance.

Design Response

Define the privacy needed for each room. Coordinate the complete enclosure—including doors, ceilings, service routes and ventilation—with specialist advice where required.

03 Challenge

Avoid promising confidential speech privacy without supporting assessment.

Integrating Power, Data and Equipment Poorly coordinated services can lead to trailing cables, inaccessible connections and disruptive later alterations.

Design Response

Establish desk positions, equipment requirements and connectivity before installation. Coordinate cable routes, access points and maintenance with the furniture and existing building services.

04 Challenge

Selecting Appropriate Workstations

Desk layouts need to respond to the people and tasks they support.

Design Response

Review desk dimensions, seating adjustability, monitor positions, lighting and circulation. Furniture selection should support individual workstation assessment rather than rely on an “ergonomic” label alone.

05 Challenge

Introducing Breakout Space without Losing Useful Capacity

A lounge or café setting needs a clear purpose within the workplace.

Design Response

Establish whether it supports breaks, informal meetings, individual work or several activities. Select furniture and locate power accordingly, while considering noise transfer to adjacent desks.

06 Challenge

Working with an Industrial Interior

Exposed surfaces and visible services can influence sound, maintenance and the proposed fit-out.

Design Response

Survey existing conditions before specifying treatment. Surface repairs, cleaning methods and acoustic interventions should be selected for the actual substrate and room.

These are design considerations relevant to the scheme. Actual site problems, client revisions and installed technical systems require confirmation from project records.
