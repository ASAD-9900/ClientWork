City of London Commercial Workplace Architecture

A multi-storey commercial workplace planned for hybrid collaboration

This 12,000 sq ft office fit-out in the City of London was designed to accommodate a transition to agile hybrid working. We structured the deep floor plate into three complementary zones: focused quiet desking along the window perimeters, open collaboration tables and breakout booths in the core, and enclosed meeting suites fitted with high-specification acoustic glazing.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to office interior design London, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

To manage sound across the open areas, suspended timber slat ceiling rafts backed with recycled acoustic felt were installed above workstation banks. The palette combines white oak joinery, fluted glass screens, and powder-coated steel framework, providing spatial definition without obstructing natural daylight across the floor plate.

Our team delivered tailored London Bridge office project, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Workplace Strategy & Detailing

Acoustic Baffles, Flexible Desking & Environmental Performance

Planned to support varied working styles, from intense solo focus to interactive group workshops.

Modern workplace interiors must adapt as headcounts fluctuate. We installed modular sit-stand desks with integrated cable trays and floor-box power points, allowing teams to reconfigure their workspaces quickly. Small one-to-one phone booths and four-person focus pods provide private spaces for video conferencing, preventing disruption on the general trading floor. The central breakout tea point features a durable solid-surface island, zip taps, and recycling hubs, serving as an informal gathering space for cross-departmental catch-ups.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated office design and build contractors, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Acoustic Engineering

Timber Ceiling Rafts

Suspended slatted oak baffles integrated with acoustic absorption backing and linear LED task lighting, dampening conversational reverberation.

02 Flexible Planning

Modular Desking Pods

Ergonomic sit-stand workstations with integrated desktop power, cable management spines, and biophilic planter dividers that add greenery and screening.

03 Enclosed Meeting Suites

Acoustic Double Glazing

45dB-rated glass partitions with slim black aluminium frames and drop seals, guaranteeing confidentiality for sensitive client consultations.
