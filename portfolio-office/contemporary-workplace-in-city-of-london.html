Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise AutoCAD space planning drawings.

01 Challenge

Balancing Focused Work with Collaboration

Open-plan offices need to accommodate conversation without making concentration unnecessarily difficult.

Design Response

Position collaborative areas with regard to quieter desks. Consider circulation, enclosed rooms and acoustic requirements together rather than relying on furniture screens alone.

02 Challenge

Planning Daylight and Screen Positions

A bright office still needs careful workstation positioning to manage glare and reflections.

Design Response

Coordinate desk orientation, monitor positions, blinds and artificial lighting. Check the arrangement against the actual windows and individual workstation requirements.

03 Challenge

Making Flexibility Practical

Modular desks do not automatically make an office easy to rearrange.

Design Response

Coordinate furniture with power, data, lighting and ventilation. Identify which areas can change without substantial alteration to services.

04 Challenge

Providing Suitable Meeting Privacy

Glazed rooms can maintain visual connection, but confidentiality depends on the complete construction.

Design Response

Define the privacy required for each activity. Coordinate partitions, doors, ceilings and service routes, obtaining specialist assessment where necessary.

05 Challenge

A glazing rating alone should not be presented as a guarantee that conversations cannot be heard.

Coordinating Ceiling Elements and Services Suspended timber features can affect lighting, ventilation and maintenance access.

Design Response

Develop the ceiling layout alongside the building-services design. Confirm support arrangements, access requirements and any intended acoustic performance before manufacture.

06 Challenge

Delivering Work within an Occupied Building

Commercial premises may have restrictions on deliveries, working hours and shared-area access.

Design Response

Establish landlord requirements and site logistics early. Programme deliveries and disruptive activities around the agreed building arrangements.

These are design considerations relevant to the supplied scheme. Actual site problems, client revisions and installed technical systems require confirmation from project records.

01 Investment Guide

What Would a Similar City of London Refurbishment Cost?

Provisional planning allowance: £200,000–£500,000 excluding VAT, for a defined refurbishment scope.

This range may be suitable for selected improvements in premises with retained, serviceable base-build infrastructure. Potential elements include finishes, furniture, selected partitions, reception joinery and agreed lighting or power alterations.

  • Workplace planning and design: Brief, surveys, layouts and specifications
  • Partitions and finishes: Rooms included, flooring, ceilings and decoration
  • Furniture: Desks, task chairs, meeting and lounge furniture
  • Fitted joinery: Reception, storage and agreed refreshment facilities
  • Power, data and lighting: Defined alterations and connections
  • Meeting technology: Agreed equipment and installation
  • Handover: Testing, snagging and agreed documentation
  • Major heating, cooling or ventilation replacement, incoming-services upgrades, structural alterations and substantial acoustic construction require separate assessment unless expressly included.: Lease costs, relocation, business IT infrastructure and ongoing software subscriptions are excluded. Professional fees, surveys and landlord-related charges should be identified separately.

Allow a separate contingency of 10–15%, reviewed following survey, with VAT added where applicable.

02 Delivery Schedule

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

6–12 months

For initial planning, allow approximately 6–12 months for a substantial coordinated refurbishment, with some stages overlapping.

  • Workplace brief, surveys and feasibility: 3–5 weeks
  • Space planning and concept design: 4–8 weeks
  • Detailed design and services coordination: 6–10 weeks
  • Landlord review and approvals: Confirm for the premises and proposed work
  • Procurement and manufacture: 8–16 weeks or longer for selected items
  • Site refurbishment and installation: 10–20 weeks
  • Testing, snagging and handover: 2–3 weeks
