Soho Workplace Architecture

An executive office suite detailed with private members' club refinement

Situated in Soho, this boutique executive office was designed for a private investment practice requiring exceptional discretion and hospitable comfort. The brief was to create an environment closer in character to a private residential library or members' club than a conventional corporate office.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to office interior design London, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

The floor plan centers on a formal reception gallery lined with bespoke walnut shelving and an integrated vertical living wall that introduces natural greenery and acoustic dampening. Individual partner suites flank the central gallery, partitioned with high-performance double-glazed acoustic screens that provide visual transparency while preventing speech transfer.

Our team delivered tailored Holborn corporate headquarters project, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Bespoke Executive Joinery, Acoustic Privacy & Hospitality Breakout

Engineered for absolute client discretion, leadership focus, and high-level entertaining.

In private equity and wealth management offices, confidential conversations are paramount. We detailed all partition walls with staggered acoustic studs, double-layer soundboard, and perimeter acoustic mastic seals, achieving a speech-transmission rating of Rw 48dB. In the executive lounge, a honed Verde Alpi marble kitchen island houses integrated refrigeration, wine storage, and a hidden espresso station, allowing the firm to host private dinners and evening client receptions without outside catering intrusion.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated office space planning, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Client Requirements

Boutique Private Equity Suite & Discreet Executive Salon

A boutique investment firm required an intimate, highly luxurious private office suite in Soho reflecting high-net-worth sophistication, private dining capabilities, and total discretion.

  • Executive private office with bespoke burl walnut desk, integrated humidor, and concealed media screens.
  • Private dining and conference salon accommodating 10 guests with adjacent butler pantry service.
  • Tactile lounge featuring velvet soft seating, curated modern art collections, and low-glare architectural lighting.
02 Challenges & Solutions

Subtle Service Concealment in Compact Prime Footprint

Integrating high-spec trading monitors, secure server racks, and catering facilities without giving the space an obvious corporate feel.

  • Motorized joinery mechanisms concealing multi-screen trading desks within elegant residential-style credenzas.
  • Acoustic leather wall paneling dampening confidential telephone calls and financial discussions.
  • Independent water-cooled internal climate units operating whisper-quietly around the clock.
