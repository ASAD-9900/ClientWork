Soho Workplace Architecture

Discreet executive prestige shaped by living greenery, warm architectural millwork, and refined glass suites

Located in vibrant Soho, this executive workplace pairs prime Central London sophistication with forward-thinking workplace wellbeing. Conceived for a discerning clientele and senior leadership team, the design creates a seamless dialogue between private consultation chambers, glass-enclosed meeting suites, and an open, light-filled collaborative workspace.

Warm timber display niches, living botanical walls, and bespoke marble kitchen joinery elevate the environment to feel more akin to a private members' club than a conventional corporate office. With acoustic glass partitions, concealed task lighting, and tactile natural finishes throughout, every touchpoint balances absolute discretion with an inspiring, hospitable ambience.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Biophilic Grandeur, Acoustic Glass & Members' Club Detailing

Balancing boardroom discretion with an open, restorative atmosphere, this Soho office sets a new benchmark for prime London executive headquarters.

A striking living wall in the arrival lobby immediately signals biophilic vitality and prestige. Floor-to-ceiling acoustic glass walls preserve acoustic confidentiality while allowing natural light to sweep across the layout. Custom warm-wood display millwork, integrated executive coffee bars, and sculptural marble islands offer a refined hospitality setting for both daily operations and private client entertaining.

01 Biophilic Design

Living Green Wall Lobby

An impressive vertical living garden frames the entrance lobby, purifying indoor air and providing a lush, organic contrast to refined stone surfaces.

02 Acoustics & Glazing

Glass-Enclosed Suites

High-performance acoustic double-glazed partitions with slimline bronze-black framing deliver confidential meeting spaces without sacrificing daylight.

03 Hospitality Integration

Marble Breakout Island

A monolithic honed marble kitchen island and bespoke walnut cabinetry anchor the executive breakout zone, supporting premium client entertaining.
