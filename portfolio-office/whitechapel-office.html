Commercial Workplace Architecture

An agile East London headquarters uniting industrial heritage, acoustic zoning and collaborative breakout spaces

Located in Whitechapel's thriving creative and tech corridor, this commercial headquarters fit-out draws inspiration from the area's industrial warehouse architecture and fast-evolving enterprise community.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to interior designer in Whitechapel, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

The project comprises an architectural reception lounge, open-plan ergonomic workstations, acoustic meeting pods, executive boardroom suites, and a vibrant staff breakout café island. A palette of rich terracotta, muted olive green, natural oak, warm stone, and crisp black metal frames provides warmth without sacrificing corporate professionalism.

Our team delivered tailored London Bridge office fit-out, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Workplace Strategy & Wellbeing

Cat A/B Fit-Out, Acoustic Engineering & Hybrid Working

Designing for agile hybrid teams requires seamless integration of power, acoustic insulation, and flexible breakout zones.

Crittall-style black metal glazed partitions deliver acoustic segregation for video conferencing without blocking daylight penetration across the floor. Integrated cable management channels within bespoke oak workbenches ensure clean, clutter-free aesthetics.

Biophilic plant shelving and warm textured wall finishes soften the industrial architecture, fostering employee productivity, collaboration, and focus.
