Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions.

01 Challenge

Separating Visitor and Staff Activity

Clients need to reach reception and meeting rooms without unnecessary movement through working areas.

Design Response

Develop the visitor journey alongside the desk layout. Coordinate reception oversight, access arrangements and routes between client-facing rooms.

02 Challenge

Supporting Focus within an Open-Plan Office

Conversation and movement can interrupt nearby desk users.

Design Response

Locate collaborative settings with regard to quieter work areas. Consider enclosed rooms, furniture placement and acoustic treatment as part of the overall workplace plan.

03 Challenge

Providing Appropriate Meeting Privacy

Confidential discussions require more than decorative wall panels or glazed partitions.

Design Response

Define the required level of privacy and coordinate the complete room enclosure, including doors, ceilings and service routes. Obtain specialist assessment where needed rather than publishing an unsupported acoustic rating.

04 Challenge

Integrating Meeting Technology

Screens, cameras and microphones need to work with the room’s furniture and lighting.

Design Response

Confirm the equipment schedule before detailing the conference table and surrounding joinery. Check sightlines, connection points, camera positions and maintenance access.

05 Challenge

Coordinating Workstations with Services

Desk changes can affect cable routes, floor boxes and lighting positions.

Design Response

Establish workstation dimensions and equipment requirements early. Coordinate power and data access with the furniture, allowing suitable access for maintenance and future adjustments.

06 Challenge

Balancing Visual Character with Practical Maintenance

Reception surfaces, upholstery and fitted furniture experience different types of wear.

Design Response

Review samples and supplier information for the intended use, cleaning requirements and repair options. Confirm exact materials before describing their species, composition or performance.

These are design considerations relevant to the scheme. Actual site problems, client revisions and installed technical systems require confirmation from project records.

01 Investment Guide

What Would a Similar Holborn Office Fit-Out Cost?

Provisional occupier fit-out allowance: £250,000–£650,000 excluding VAT.

This range is intended for a defined corporate office fit-out in premises with suitable existing base-build services. It may include partitions, finishes, reception joinery, furniture, lighting alterations, meeting-room technology and installation.

  • Workplace planning and design: Brief, surveys, layouts and specifications
  • Partitions and finishes: Meeting rooms, flooring, ceilings and decoration
  • Reception and fitted joinery: Desk, storage and equipment integration
  • Furniture: Workstations, task chairs, meeting tables and visitor seating
  • Power, data and lighting: Defined alterations and connection requirements
  • Meeting technology: Agreed equipment, controls and installation
  • Handover: Testing, snagging and agreed documentation
  • Major heating, cooling or ventilation replacement, incoming-services upgrades, structural alterations, server infrastructure and substantial acoustic construction require separate assessment unless expressly included.: Lease costs, relocation, business IT infrastructure and ongoing software subscriptions are excluded. Professional fees, surveys and landlord-related charges should be identified separately.

Allow a separate contingency of 10–15%, reviewed following survey, with VAT added where applicable.

02 Delivery Schedule

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

6–12 months

For initial planning, allow approximately 6–12 months, with some stages overlapping.

  • Workplace brief, surveys and feasibility: 3–5 weeks
  • Space planning and concept design: 4–6 weeks
  • Detailed design and services coordination: 6–10 weeks
  • Landlord review and approvals: Confirm for the premises and proposed work

  • Site fit-out and installation: 10–20 weeks
