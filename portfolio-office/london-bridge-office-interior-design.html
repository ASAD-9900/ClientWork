This London Bridge office scheme brings together the practical needs of a working team and the experience of visiting clients.

Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise 3D CGI render services.

01 Challenge

Separating Visitor Routes from Working Areas

Clients need to reach reception, meeting rooms and hospitality spaces without unnecessary movement through staff workstations.

Design Response

Plan arrival and visitor circulation alongside the operational layout. Consider access arrangements, staff movement and the relationship between reception and meeting rooms.

02 Challenge

Supporting Confidential Discussions

Visual screening and acoustic privacy are different requirements.

Design Response

Define the level of privacy needed in each meeting room. Coordinate the complete enclosure, including doors, ceilings, partitions, service penetrations and ventilation, with specialist assessment where required.

03 Challenge

Textured glass can obscure views, but should not be described as guaranteeing speech confidentiality.

Balancing Open-Plan Activity with Concentration Conversation and movement can affect nearby desk users.

Design Response

Position collaborative settings and circulation routes with regard to quieter working areas. Assess acoustic treatment as part of the room design rather than assuming that soft colours or timber finishes will control noise.

04 Challenge

Integrating Conference Technology

Poor camera positions, lighting or audio arrangements can make meeting rooms difficult to use.

Design Response

Coordinate screens, cameras, microphones, power and connectivity with the furniture layout. Check sightlines and lighting before installation, allowing access for maintenance.

05 Challenge

Making Client Hospitality Practical

Refreshment points need to be convenient without interrupting meetings or creating clutter.

Design Response

Plan storage, equipment and replenishment routes together. Select finishes around cleaning requirements and provide suitable service access.

06 Challenge

Coordinating the Fit-Out with Existing Services

New enclosed rooms can affect lighting, ventilation and other building systems.

Design Response

Survey the premises and coordinate specialist requirements before finalising partitions and ceilings. Confirm landlord interfaces and the condition of retained services.

These are design considerations relevant to the supplied scheme. Actual site problems, client revisions and installed technical systems require confirmation from project records.

01 Investment Guide

What Would a Similar London Bridge Office Fit-Out Cost?

£150,000–£350,000 excluding VAT.

This is an initial editorial estimate for a defined occupier fit-out in premises with suitable existing base-build services. It may include selected partitions, internal finishes, reception joinery, furniture, lighting alterations and installation.

  • Workplace planning and design: Brief, surveys, layouts and specifications
  • Partitions and finishes: Meeting rooms, private offices, flooring and decoration
  • Furniture: Desks, task chairs, meeting tables and lounge seating
  • Fitted joinery: Reception, storage and refreshment cabinetry
  • Power, data and lighting: Defined alterations and connection requirements
  • Meeting technology: Agreed equipment and installation
  • Handover: Testing, snagging and agreed documentation
  • Major heating, cooling or ventilation replacement, incoming-services upgrades, structural alterations and substantial acoustic construction require separate assessment unless expressly included.: Lease costs, relocation, business IT infrastructure and ongoing software subscriptions are excluded. Professional fees, surveys and landlord-related charges should be identified separately.

Allow a separate contingency of 10–15%, reviewed following survey, with VAT added where applicable.

02 Delivery Schedule

How Long Would a Similar Project Take?

5–9 months

For initial planning, allow approximately 5–9 months, with some stages overlapping.

  • Workplace brief, surveys and feasibility: 2–4 weeks
  • Space planning and concept design: 4–6 weeks
  • Detailed design and services coordination: 4–8 weeks
  • Landlord review and approvals: Confirm for the premises and proposed work
  • Procurement and manufacture: 8–14 weeks or longer for selected items
  • Site fit-out and installation: 8–14 weeks
  • Testing, snagging and handover: 1–2 weeks
