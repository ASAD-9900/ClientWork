Westminster Healthcare Architecture

A specialist dental clinic balancing clinical precision and patient calm

This private dental practice in Westminster required a spatial scheme that meets CQC healthcare regulations whilst offering an environment that reassures nervous patients. The layout separates patient circulation from clinical clean-and-dirty utility routes, allowing staff to move dental instruments safely between the central decontamination room and surgery suites.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to commercial interior design, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

In the reception and consultation rooms, we introduced warm European oak panelling, soft acoustic ceiling rafts, and comfortable loose armchairs to eliminate clinical intimidation. Within the surgeries, Corian worktops and continuous vinyl flooring provide hygienic, non-porous surfaces that withstand stringent medical disinfectant protocols without staining or degrading.

Our team delivered tailored Harrow clinic project, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Healthcare Compliance, Acoustic Isolation & Practitioner Ergonomics

Rigorous spatial planning that satisfies clinical standards while enhancing patient comfort.

Medical technology can easily dominate a surgery. We designed custom cabinetry with flush push-to-open doors to house dental delivery units, suction apparatus, and waste disposal systems out of immediate patient sightlines. Surgery lighting combines high-CRI 5000K daylight-calibrated examination task luminaires with warm 2700K indirect perimeter coves, allowing the dentist to perform precise colour matching for restorative work whilst maintaining a relaxing atmosphere for the patient in the chair.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated clinic space planning, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Clinical Planning

Separated Utility Routes

Dedicated clean and dirty decontamination pathways compliant with HTM 01-05 standards, streamlining infection control workflows for clinical staff.

02 Cabinetry & Hygiene

Continuous Solid Surfacing

Acoustic Wall Panelling

Every project undertaken by Oraanj is defined by a tailored architectural brief, rigorous site problem-solving, and transparent, realistic financial planning.

01 Client Requirements

Flagship Harley-Street Standard Private Dental Suites

An elite central London dental practice required an immaculate, high-spec clinical fit-out featuring private patient lounges, state-of-the-art surgery suites, and CBCT imaging capabilities.

  • Seamless Corian clinical cabinetry with integrated dental suction, surgical lights, and monitor arms.
  • Dedicated lead-lined radiation-shielded radiography room for 3D digital CBCT scanner equipment.
  • VIP waiting lounge offering private hospitality refreshment bays, plush seating, and sound privacy.
02 Challenges & Solutions

Subfloor Lead-Lining & Historical Basement Structural Slabs

Working within a heritage central London property requiring radiation protection certification and subfloor structural reinforcement for heavy imaging equipment.

  • Code 5 lead-sheet radiation shielding integrated into partition walls and certified by RPA medical physicists.
  • Precision trenching through thick concrete slabs for dental chair suction lines, compressed air, and water treatment.
  • High-specification acoustic door assemblies achieving 48dB noise reduction between operating surgeries.
