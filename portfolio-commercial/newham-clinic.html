Healthcare & Wellness Architecture

A welcoming East London clinic uniting clinical excellence, calming tones and natural timber craftsmanship

Designed for a contemporary private medical and wellness practice in Newham, this turnkey commercial interior balances stringent clinical hygiene requirements with the comforting, hospitality-inspired atmosphere essential for modern patient wellbeing.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to interior designer in Newham, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

The layout features an inviting reception desk, a serene patient waiting lounge, dedicated soundproofed consultation offices, and fully equipped treatment suites. A restorative palette of muted olive green, terracotta accents, natural European oak, warm cream, and textured stone creates an unhurried, reassuring environment.

Our team delivered tailored Harrow clinic project, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Technical Precision & Spatial Flow

Bespoke Joinery & Clinical Compliance in East London

Delivering an exceptional healthcare environment requires reconciling strict hygiene and CQC regulations with high-end interior architecture.

Acoustic engineering was paramount across the scheme. We installed double-glazed acoustic partitions delivering high sound attenuation between consultation suites, ensuring complete patient confidentiality while allowing natural daylight to illuminate internal circulation routes.

Durable, non-porous composite stone surfaces, coved seamless vinyl flooring, and touch-free sensor brassware meet healthcare infection control standards while maintaining tactile luxury throughout the clinic.

Client Requirements, Design Challenges & Indicative Investment

Every project undertaken by Oraanj is defined by a tailored architectural brief, rigorous site problem-solving, and transparent, realistic financial planning.

01 Client Requirements

Comprehensive Community Medical Facility & Poly-Clinic

A state-of-the-art poly-clinic demanding high patient throughput, durable antimicrobial materials, full DDA accessibility, and rapid room turnarounds.

  • Spacious accessible reception with dual-height check-in counters and digital self-service kiosks.
  • Multiple standardized clinical examination rooms with Bio-Cote non-porous surfaces and coved floors.
  • Acoustic consultation doors with automatic mechanical drop seals ensuring total patient confidentiality.
02 Challenges & Solutions

High-Volume Traffic Durability & Infection Control Rigor

Designing spaces that resist aggressive chemical cleaning, wheelchair scuffs, and continuous footfall while avoiding a cold, institutional aesthetic.

  • Heavy-duty impact protection wall sheets with timber-look finishes balancing hygiene and visual warmth.
  • HEPA-filtered mechanical ventilation providing 12 air changes per hour in minor procedure rooms.
  • Color-coded intuitive wayfinding graphics integrated seamlessly into flooring and door thresholds.
