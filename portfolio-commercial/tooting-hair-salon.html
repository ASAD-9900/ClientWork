Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise electrical and lighting layout plans.

01 Challenge

Balancing Styling Capacity with Working Space

Adding stations can reduce the space available for stylists to move around clients.

Design Response

Plan chair positions, mirror units and circulation together. Check working clearances with chairs in use before confirming capacity.

02 Challenge

Coordinating Lighting for Hair Services

Decorative mirror lighting alone may not provide suitable illumination for cutting and colour assessment.

Design Response

Coordinate mirror lighting with overhead task lighting, considering colour rendering, consistency, glare and shadows. Confirm performance through product information and on-site review.

03 Challenge

Explore our lighting design services.

Keeping Tools and Cables Organised Hairdryers, styling tools and charging cables can clutter work surfaces and circulation routes.

Design Response

Integrate suitable tool storage and cable management into the joinery. Coordinate electrical connections and heat protection with the selected equipment.

04 Challenge

Providing Reliable Wash-Area Services

Several basins operating together can place demands on water supply, heating and drainage.

Design Response

Establish the expected usage and basin specifications before services are designed. Have appropriate specialists assess capacity rather than selecting a cylinder size from appearance or promising unlimited supply.

05 Challenge

Selecting Surfaces for Colour Products and Cleaning

Hair colourants and cleaning products can affect worktops, flooring and cabinetry.

Design Response

Review the actual materials against supplier guidance and the salon’s products. Confirm cleaning procedures and resistance to expected exposure; avoid describing finishes as universally stain-proof or chemical-proof.

06 Challenge

Creating a More Relaxed Wash Setting

Running water, conversation and dryers can affect the atmosphere around the basins.

Design Response

Consider the wash area’s position, lighting and acoustic requirements together. Any acoustic treatment must also be suitable for its location and maintenance needs.

These are design considerations relevant to the supplied scheme. Actual site problems, client revisions and installed technical systems require confirmation from project records.

Allow a separate contingency of 10–15%, reviewed following survey, with VAT added where applicable.
