Tooting Commercial & Wellness Design

A wellness salon shaped by serene greenery

Conceived to elevate the salon and wellness journey in South West London, this contemporary Tooting hair salon reimagines commercial salon design through the lens of quiet, spa-like luxury. Clean architectural proportions, soft neutral palettes, and integrated indoor greenery replace clinical conventions with an atmosphere of calm sophistication.

Bespoke styling pods, custom reception millwork, and ergonomic wash basins are purposefully zoned to ensure seamless movement and client privacy. Calibrated task lighting, backlit halo mirrors, warm oak accents, and textured finishes come together to create an uplifting, functional space for stylists and a tranquil escape for clients.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Ergonomic Precision, Intuitive Flow & Biophilic Calm

Every square metre of this Tooting salon was developed to balance high-performance commercial demands with the rejuvenating serenity of a wellness spa.

Thoughtful acoustic screening, shadow-free task lighting, and fully concealed storage maintain clear, uncluttered workstations. Tactile plasterwork, blonde oak cabinetry, and integrated air-purifying greenery create a restorative sensory journey from the welcome lounge to the wash sanctuary.

01 Ergonomics

Custom Styling Pods

Bespoke joinery units feature integrated cable management, hidden product recesses, and precision backlit halo mirrors for optimal stylist accuracy.

02 Wellness

Tranquil Wash Sanctuary

A visually and acoustically isolated hair spa zone fitted with reclining ergonomic wash basins, soft indirect illumination, and deep comfort.

03 Biophilic Design

Living Botanicals

Curated indoor foliage, natural timber accents, and organic textures soften the modern lines while enhancing air purity and guest calm.
