Fitzrovia Commercial & Retail Design

A luxury beauty salon designed for tailored rituals

Bridging elevated retail merchandising with intimate salon hospitality, this commercial interior in Fitzrovia was crafted to create an engaging customer journey. Moving beyond conventional retail layouts, the space integrates tactile materiality, warm minimalist shelving displays, and soft indirect illumination to establish a vibrant, lifestyle-driven destination.

Custom joinery displays showcase curated beauty and wellness products at the entrance, drawing visitors seamlessly into dedicated styling stations and secluded client wash zones. Every touchpoint balances high-traffic commercial durability with residential warmth, offering an inspiring blend of boutique retail and personalized salon luxury.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Experiential Merchandising, Sculpted Millwork & Ambient Glow

Conceived as a contemporary commercial destination in Fitzrovia, every corner was engineered to maximize retail display while cultivating a restful, boutique salon ambiance.

The interior unites minimalist retail shelving, backlit arched mirrors, and tactile acoustic wall treatments. Handcrafted timber joinery, custom product display alcoves, and biophilic touches create an effortless balance between brand storytelling, merchandising precision, and bespoke client wellness.
