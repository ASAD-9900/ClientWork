Ilford Commercial & Wellness Retail

A wellness boutique planned around sculptural joinery and restorative calm

This commercial interior in Ilford integrates high-end skincare retail with private spa treatment rooms. The floor plan establishes an intuitive customer journey that moves from an open, naturally lit reception lounge into acoustic-dampened treatment suites positioned toward the quieter rear of the building.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to interior designer in Ilford, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

We designed bespoke midnight blue lacquered cabinetry with integrated warm 2700K shelf illumination to highlight skincare bottles without producing distracting glare. Curved plaster partitions and soft linen window dressings filter harsh daylight from the expansive perimeter glazing, providing clients with visual privacy from the high street whilst maintaining an airy, welcoming street presence.

Our team delivered tailored retail interior design, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Custom Millwork, Acoustic Damping & Zoned Retail Lighting

Planned to balance rigorous operational practicality with a relaxed, unhurried customer experience.

Commercial wellness spaces demand precise environmental control. We specified sound-insulated stud partitions and solid core doors with acoustic drop seals to prevent treatment room conversations from carrying into the retail floor. Bespoke display units feature fluted timber panels and solid brass hardware chosen for their tactile weight and durability under constant commercial use. In the consultation lounge, curved banquette seating upholstered in stain-resistant wool provides comfortable posture support during client assessments.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated wellness colour consultation, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.
