Workplace Strategy & Interior Architecture

A Central London commercial interior uniting acoustic focus, collaborative energy and hospitality-led client spaces

Located in the heart of London Bridge's commercial district, this project required re-engineering an open-plan floorplate into a high-performance workplace for a fast-growing financial advisory firm. The client brief required a balance between energetic open-plan team desks, confidential boardroom facilities, private executive offices, and a dedicated client lounge that sets an unhurried, professional tone for high-level meetings.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to interior designer in London Bridge, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

Our spatial layout divides the premises into distinct public and operational wings. Visitors arrive into an architectural reception anchored by a bespoke timber desk with honed natural stone surfaces and recessed plinth illumination. Black-framed reeded glass partitions divide circulation routes from client meeting suites, providing essential visual privacy without impeding natural daylight. Throughout the interior, warm European oak joinery, muted olive upholstery, soft taupe wall treatments, and terracotta accents soften corporate acoustics and support productive daily work.

Our team delivered tailored office interior design London, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Workplace Engineering & Craft

Acoustic Engineering, Integrated AV & Bespoke Joinery

Balancing open-plan collaborative productivity with high acoustic privacy in meeting suites.

To resolve open-plan reverberation, sound-absorbing micro-perforated ceiling rafts were positioned directly above desking zones. The client boardroom incorporates hidden perimeter acoustic seals, double-glazed acoustic partitioning, and bespoke oak wall panelling with concealed cable conduits for seamless video conferencing.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated City of London workplace project, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Acoustics

Sound-Absorbing Ceiling Rafts

Felt-lined micro-perforated acoustic rafts above desking reduce ambient chatter without compromising high ceiling volumes.

02 Partitions

Reeded Glass & Steel Screens

Industrial black-framed reeded glazing diffuses natural light whilst providing 45dB speech privacy for confidential meetings.

03 Hospitality

Executive Client Lounge

A dedicated hospitality suite featuring soft olive seating, warm oak coffee credenzas, and integrated refreshment points.
