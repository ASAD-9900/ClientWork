Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise AutoCAD space planning drawings.

01 Challenge

Making Arrival and Check-In Clear

An unclear reception arrangement can leave visitors unsure where to wait or ask for assistance.

Design Response

Position the desk as a recognisable focal point. Coordinate signage, waiting furniture and circulation so visitors can understand the space without unnecessary movement.

02 Challenge

Balancing Comfort with Cleaning Requirements

Textiles and timber-look finishes can introduce warmth, but suitability depends on their location and maintenance.

Design Response

Assess waiting-area furniture separately from treatment-room finishes. Review cleaning methods, surface detailing and supplier information before selection.

03 Challenge

Supporting Privacy

Reception conversations and consultations may involve sensitive information.

Design Response

Consider waiting-seat positions, staff screens and arrangements for discreet conversations. Define the required acoustic privacy for enclosed rooms and assess the complete construction where specialist input is needed.

04 Challenge

Doors or insulation alone should not be described as guaranteeing confidentiality.

Planning Rooms Around Actual Treatments An examination room, consultation office and minor-procedure room can have different requirements.

Design Response

Confirm the activities and equipment schedule before detailed design. Coordinate clearances, storage, plumbing, lighting and ventilation with the appropriate specialists.

05 Challenge

A room’s appearance does not establish clinical suitability.

Selecting Basins and Surrounding Surfaces A decorative vanity and a clinical handwashing point serve different purposes.

Design Response

Establish the intended use before specifying the basin, tapware and adjacent finishes. Confirm cleaning requirements, maintenance access and suitability with the provider’s advisers.

06 Challenge

Keeping Circulation Accessible and Understandable

Furniture, doors and equipment can reduce usable corridor space.

Design Response

Review circulation dimensions, door operation and wayfinding together. Assess accessibility for the actual premises and proposed services rather than making a general compliance claim.
