Notting Hill Hospitality Design

A Mediterranean kitchen shaped by sun-drenched warmth

Evoking the relaxed warmth of coastal Mediterranean dining within the storied elegance of the Royal Borough, this destination restaurant and bistro celebrates tactile materiality, sculptural forms, and convivial hospitality. A rich architectural dialogue unfolds through grand plaster archways, bespoke terrazzo surfaces, and deep terracotta niches paired with muted olive velvet banquettes and solid walnut furnishings.

Every dining zone is carefully orchestrated to balance lively bistro atmosphere with secluded intimacy. From the animated terrazzo bar surrounding the artisanal wood-fired oven to the sunlit perimeter tables along arched windows, the scheme delivers an immersive, sun-drenched culinary journey rooted in timeless London refinement.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Sculptural Arches, Artisanal Terrazzo & Warm Earth Tones

Balancing culinary theatre with intimate dining alcoves, this Notting Hill space translates coastal Mediterranean architecture into an enduring London landmark.

Hand-trowelled plaster arches frame views across the restaurant, framing soft terracotta dining booths and a central terrazzo bar anchored by a wood-fired hearth. Deep olive velvets, natural walnut millwork, and brass accent lighting build layered warmth that invites guests to linger from golden afternoon lunches into vibrant candlelit evenings.

01 Architecture

Sculptural Arches & Niches

Hand-finished plaster archways and terracotta-lined alcoves provide natural spatial zoning, acoustic softening, and an atmospheric sense of place.

02 Materiality

Terrazzo & Walnut Craft

Bespoke cast terrazzo counters, honed marble dining surfaces, and rich walnut joinery introduce tactile organic honesty to the interior.

03 Hospitality

Convivial Dining Zones

Deep olive green banquettes, curved corner booths, and an open hearth dining counter create seamless variety between intimate dates and celebratory feasts.
