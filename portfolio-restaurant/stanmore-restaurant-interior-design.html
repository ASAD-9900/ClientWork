A Neighbourhood Venue with a Warm Identity

This Stanmore restaurant and café scheme brings together comfortable seating, earthy colours and coordinated finishes.

Terracotta banquettes provide a distinctive focal point, complemented by olive upholstery and walnut tones. Pale table surfaces introduce contrast, while metallic details add warmth without overwhelming the interior.

The design direction supports different types of visit: a quick coffee, a relaxed lunch or a longer evening meal. Seating, lighting and service arrangements need to work together so the venue can accommodate these uses comfortably.

Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise AutoCAD space planning drawings.

01 Challenge

Supporting Café and Restaurant Service

Morning coffee and evening meals place different demands on ordering, preparation and table service.

Design Response

Establish the menu and service model before finalising the layout. Coordinate counter positions, collection points and waiter stations so the different uses can operate without unnecessary congestion.

02 Challenge

Combining Banquettes with Flexible Tables

Fixed seating can make effective use of the room’s perimeter, but the venue also needs flexibility for different parties.

Design Response

Develop banquettes alongside movable tables and chairs. Check table combinations, seating clearances and service access before confirming capacity.

03 Challenge

Private-event arrangements should be assessed separately rather than inferred from the everyday layout.

Creating a Comfortable Sound Environment Conversation, crockery and equipment can contribute to noise during busy periods.

Design Response

Consider acoustic requirements alongside ceilings, walls and furniture. Upholstery may contribute to sound absorption, but any specialist treatment should be specified and assessed for the actual room.

04 Challenge

Adjusting the Atmosphere Throughout the Day

A bright café setting and a more intimate dining room need different lighting arrangements.

Design Response

Coordinate ambient, table and task lighting with suitable controls. Evening settings should retain sufficient visibility for menus, circulation and staff duties.

05 Challenge

Selecting Practical Upholstery and Surfaces

Terracotta and olive fabrics give the venue character, but their suitability depends on the complete specification.

Design Response

Review cleaning instructions, durability information and relevant supplier documentation. Table surfaces should also be selected around spills, maintenance and repair expectations.

These are design considerations relevant to the scheme. Actual site problems, installed systems and client-requested changes require confirmation from project records.
