An East London Dining Space with Warmth and Character

This Dalston restaurant scheme balances an industrial material palette with comfortable seating and warm lighting.

Terracotta-toned banquettes define the dining area, while dark tables provide contrast. An olive-green fluted counter gives the bar its own identity, complemented by brick textures, open shelving and metallic lighting details.

The design connects dining and drinks service within a consistent interior. Its character comes from the relationship between colour, texture and proportion, supported by practical planning for guests and staff.

Design Problem Solving

Design Challenges & Architectural Responses

Every project is shaped by physical site parameters, technical regulations, and tailored spatial solutions. Detailed architectural coordination was resolved prior to site commencement using precise hospitality lighting design.

01 Challenge

Balancing Seating Capacity with Comfort

Increasing table numbers can reduce usable space for guests and staff if clearances are considered too late.

Design Response

Develop banquette positions, table dimensions and circulation together. Confirm capacity from the measured layout and relevant project assessments, rather than using an unsupported cover count.

02 Challenge

Making Perimeter Seating Practical

Continuous banquettes can use wall space effectively, but their dimensions affect both comfort and access.

Design Response

Coordinate seat height, depth, back support and table position before manufacture. Review sample upholstery and consider how seating will be cleaned and maintained.

03 Challenge

Connecting Dining and Bar Service

The bar needs to support drinks preparation without creating congestion around nearby tables.

Design Response

Position equipment, storage and service points around the operator’s menu and staffing model. Check routes for serving, clearing and replenishment during layout development.

04 Challenge

Managing Sound around Hard Finishes

Brick, stone and timber surfaces can contribute to a lively sound environment.

Design Response

Assess acoustic requirements alongside the ceiling, wall finishes and furnishings. Upholstery can contribute softness, but specialist acoustic performance should be specified and verified separately.

05 Challenge

Coordinating Cooking and Building Services

The proposed menu influences equipment, ventilation, drainage and power requirements.

Design Response

Establish the cooking methods and equipment schedule early. Coordinate specialist services against the premises and any neighbouring uses before finalising the fit-out.

06 Challenge

Selecting Finishes for Spills and Wear

Tables, counters and upholstery experience frequent cleaning and repeated contact with food and drinks.

Design Response

Review supplier information for suitability, maintenance and repair. Avoid promises that surfaces are stain-proof or that ventilation will eliminate odours without supporting evidence.

These are design considerations relevant to the scheme. Actual site problems, installed systems and client-requested changes require confirmation from project records.

01 Investment Guide

What Would a Similar Dalston Fit-Out Cost?

Provisional interior fit-out allowance: £150,000–£350,000 excluding VAT.

This range applies to a defined scope involving finishes, bar joinery, fitted seating, furniture, decorative lighting and installation.

  • Design and technical coordination: Surveys, layouts, drawings and specifications
  • Interior fit-out: Flooring, ceilings, decoration and agreed partitions
  • Bar and service joinery: Counter, shelving, storage and equipment interfaces
  • Seating and furniture: Banquettes, chairs and tables
  • Lighting and electrical work: Fittings, controls and agreed alterations
  • Specialist services: Defined ventilation, plumbing and acoustic requirements
  • Installation and handover: Delivery, fitting, commissioning coordination and snagging
  • Commercial kitchen equipment, extraction, specialist refrigeration, substantial acoustic isolation and major services upgrades require separate quotations unless expressly included.: Property acquisition, rent deposits, licensing costs, operating stock and pre-opening staffing are excluded. Professional fees and approval-related charges should be identified separately.

Allow a separate contingency of 10–15%, reviewed following survey, with VAT added where applicable.
