Angel Hospitality & Dining Design

An all-day bistro and bar planned for high-capacity neighbourhood dining

This all-day restaurant and bar in Angel was designed to transition practically across three distinct service periods: fast-paced morning coffees, casual lunch sittings, and high-energy dinner service. We configured the 140-cover dining floor around a central island bar and an open kitchen pass, ensuring clear circulation paths for waitstaff between kitchen, bar, and tables.

Deep olive leather banquette seating lines the perimeter walls, built over sturdy solid oak plinths that resist scuffing from customer footwear. Honed terrazzo floor tiles were selected for their slip resistance and ease of cleaning, transitioning to warm timber parquet in the raised dining gallery to demarcate casual and formal seating areas.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Cover Density, Service Routes & All-Day Lighting Transitions

Balancing operational speed, table turnover, and customer comfort throughout all-day service.

Acoustic control is critical in a busy 140-cover restaurant. We incorporated acoustic foam panels behind decorative slatted timber ceiling rafts, absorbing dining chatter so conversations remain effortless even during peak weekend services. Lighting runs on a scheduled four-scene DALI controller, shifting automatically from bright, energising 3000K daylight settings at breakfast down to low, atmospheric 2200K candlelight tones by evening service, supported by dimmable picture lights that illuminate framed artwork along the walls.
