Angel Hospitality & Dining Design

An all-day bistro and bar planned for high-capacity neighbourhood dining

This all-day restaurant and bar in Angel was designed to transition practically across three distinct service periods: fast-paced morning coffees, casual lunch sittings, and high-energy dinner service. We configured the 140-cover dining floor around a central island bar and an open kitchen pass, ensuring clear circulation paths for waitstaff between kitchen, bar, and tables.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to restaurant interior design London, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

Deep olive leather banquette seating lines the perimeter walls, built over sturdy solid oak plinths that resist scuffing from customer footwear. Honed terrazzo floor tiles were selected for their slip resistance and ease of cleaning, transitioning to warm timber parquet in the raised dining gallery to demarcate casual and formal seating areas.

Our team delivered tailored Dalston restaurant project, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Cover Density, Service Routes & All-Day Lighting Transitions

Balancing operational speed, table turnover, and customer comfort throughout all-day service.

Acoustic control is critical in a busy 140-cover restaurant. We incorporated acoustic foam panels behind decorative slatted timber ceiling rafts, absorbing dining chatter so conversations remain effortless even during peak weekend services. Lighting runs on a scheduled four-scene DALI controller, shifting automatically from bright, energising 3000K daylight settings at breakfast down to low, atmospheric 2200K candlelight tones by evening service, supported by dimmable picture lights that illuminate framed artwork along the walls.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated custom restaurant furniture, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Client Requirements

Neighborhood All-Day Dining & Vibrant Social Gathering Hub

An energetic Islington bistro and café bar requiring a playful, vibrant interior with warm mid-century furniture, exposed brickwork, terrazzo floors, and flexible group seating.

  • Custom terrazzo bar counter with fluted timber base and integrated under-counter draft beer taps.
  • High-density leather banquettes and flexible dining tables accommodating casual brunches or large dinner parties.
  • Acoustic felt ceiling rafts absorbing weekend brunch chatter while keeping the room lively and energetic.
02 Challenges & Solutions

High-Turnover Table Ergonomics & Rapid Weekend Service

Optimizing circulation paths for fast-paced weekend brunch turnover where hundreds of covers must be served smoothly without bottle-necking waitstaff.

  • Central service waiter station with cutlery drops, POS terminals, and glassware storage reducing staff footsteps by 35%.
  • Ultra-durable contract upholstery with 100,000 Martindale rub ratings and wipe-clean stain protection.
  • Zoned audio system providing energetic music near the bar while maintaining comfortable conversation levels at rear booths.
