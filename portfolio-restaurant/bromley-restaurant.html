Angel Hospitality & Dining Design

An inviting all-day bistro shaped by olive leather, warm fluted timber, and open-kitchen craft

Envisioned as a vibrant neighbourhood destination in Angel, Islington, this all-day restaurant and café bar transitions effortlessly from sunlit morning coffee and brunch into candlelit evening dining. The scheme anchors the expansive venue with rich olive green leather banquettes, fluted natural timber joinery, honed stone countertops, and soft architectural illumination.

A central open-kitchen pass and custom wrap-around bar create an engaging focal point for diners, while quiet banquette niches along the perimeter offer relaxed comfort for lingering meals. Balancing urban bistro sophistication with warm, community-driven hospitality, the interior creates an enduring culinary gathering space.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Artisan Millwork, Olive Leather & Convivial Hospitality

Designed to serve Angel as an elevated dining destination, every seating zone harmonises durable commercial practicality with warm residential charm.

Hand-crafted fluted timber panelling, tactile olive leather banquettes, and ribbed glass screens establish visual softness across the dining floor. Warm-dim lighting accents, acoustic ceiling treatments, and custom stool joinery create an inviting setting where guests can enjoy quick morning espressos or long, celebratory dinners.
