Marylebone Hospitality Design

A dynamic culinary destination blending copper warmth, rich cognac leathers, and open-kitchen theatre

Conceived as a vibrant culinary anchor in Marylebone, this contemporary restaurant and brasserie marries the theatrical energy of an open-flame kitchen with refined dining comfort. A rich material palette of hand-stitched cognac leather booths, warm walnut joinery, hammered copper accents, and muted olive green upholstery introduces an immediate sense of relaxed luxury.

The layout is thoughtfully choreographed around the central open kitchen, offering diners front-row views of culinary artistry while preserving pockets of quiet intimacy. Sun-drenched perimeter seating, bespoke acoustic wall features, curated abstract artworks, and low-level ambient lighting craft an effortlessly social atmosphere that thrives from casual afternoon lunches through to vibrant evening service.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Culinary Theatre, Rich Leather Craft & Tailored Acoustic Warmth

Designed to offer an immersive sensory dining experience in Marylebone, every zone combines tactile materiality with theatrical kitchen sightlines.

Hammered copper kitchen hoods, deep fluted cognac leather booths, and solid walnut millwork bring organic warmth and durability to the high-traffic dining floor. Carefully integrated acoustic panels and warm-dim lighting allow the vibrant hum of the open kitchen to energise the room while maintaining clear conversational intimacy at every table.

01 Culinary Theatre

Copper Open Kitchen

A striking open pass framed in polished copper, bespoke stainless steel, and warm task illumination connects diners directly to culinary craftsmanship.

02 Banquette Craft

Cognac & Olive Seating

Tailored deep-buttoned cognac leather booths and curved olive banquettes provide tactile luxury, durability, and intimate dining nooks.

03 Materiality & Art

Walnut & Abstract Art

Custom walnut joinery and curated contemporary abstract paintings introduce residential warmth and visual rhythm throughout the venue.
