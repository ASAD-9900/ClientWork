Marylebone Hospitality Design

A Marylebone brasserie planned around an open-flame theatre kitchen

This brasserie in Marylebone was planned around a theatrical open kitchen pass framed in hand-hammered copper and blackened steel. The layout establishes a clear gradient of energy, starting with vibrant high-top counter seating along the open pass and moving outward to deep, leather-lined booths for longer, leisurely dinners.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to restaurant interior design London, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

Deep cognac leather, fluted walnut wall panelling, and honed basalt dining tables were chosen to provide durable commercial surfaces that look richer as they age. Acoustic plaster ceilings absorb high-frequency kitchen clatter, allowing the hum of the dining room to feel energetic without forcing guests to raise their voices across the table.

Our team delivered tailored Angel restaurant design project, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Open Kitchen Extraction, Banquette Detailing & Acoustic Engineering

Engineered to deliver high operational speed, heat management, and lasting guest comfort.

The relationship between the kitchen pass and the dining floor determines service efficiency. We planned the waitstations and glass storage directly adjacent to the kitchen pass, reducing staff transit steps and keeping hot plates moving quickly to tables. Deep-buttoned booth seating incorporates high-resilience commercial foam and wipeable pull-up leather, providing substantial lumbar support for extended evening sittings. Lighting is dimmed along independent circuits to highlight the warm copper kitchen canopy whilst keeping dining tables in soft, flattering light.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated kitchen layout CAD drawings, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Kitchen Integration

Hammered Copper Pass

A bespoke copper and stainless steel pass with integrated plate warmers and service shelves, forming the culinary centrepiece of the room.

02 Dining Comfort

Buttoned Cognac Booths

Ergonomically pitched booth seating in hardwearing cognac leather with walnut divider panels, offering privacy and acoustic shielding between parties.

03 Table Surfaces

Honed Basalt & Walnut

Heat-resistant natural basalt tabletops with solid walnut edges, eliminating the need for tablecloths whilst offering excellent scratch and stain resistance.
