A Relaxed Setting with Natural Character
This Hampstead lounge and bar scheme takes its direction from natural textures, planting and comfortable seating.
Timber finishes and woven details create a warm backdrop, while greenery introduces colour and variation. Textured upholstery and smaller seating arrangements give guests different places to settle, from sociable dining tables to quieter lounge corners.
The design approach considers how the venue can feel welcoming during the day and more intimate in the evening, without losing the practical visibility and circulation needed for service.

The Design Brief
The supplied brief centres on creating a welcoming venue with a distinctive identity and comfortable settings for socialising.
The principal objectives are to:
Establish a consistent interior palette using timber, woven textures and greenery.
Provide a choice of dining and lounge seating.
Support movement between the entrance, tables and bar.
Create lighting suitable for daytime and evening service.
Integrate practical storage and service requirements.
Select finishes appropriate for hospitality use.
The atmosphere should support the venue’s offering while remaining practical for the team operating it.
Design Challenges and Responses
Supporting Both Daytime and Evening Use
A venue that serves throughout the day needs more than one lighting setting.
Design response: Combine decorative pendants with ambient and task lighting, using suitable controls to adjust the atmosphere. Coordinate this with available daylight and test table visibility during commissioning.
Explore our lighting design services.
Introducing Greenery without Obstructing the Space
Planting can become difficult to maintain or interfere with circulation when considered only as decoration.
Design response: Plan planting positions alongside seating, lighting and access routes. Establish whether plants are live or artificial, how they will be maintained, and how any suspended elements will be supported.
Greenery should not be presented as a substitute for assessed acoustic treatment.
Balancing Intimate Seating with Clear Service Routes
Smaller seating pockets can create character, but their arrangement must allow staff and guests to move comfortably.
Design response: Coordinate table sizes, chair movement, banquette positions and service access. Confirm capacity from the measured layout and relevant project assessments.
Making the Bar Practical to Operate
A visually prominent bar also needs an efficient arrangement behind the counter.
Design response: Develop the layout around the drinks menu, staffing model and equipment schedule. Coordinate refrigeration, glasswashing, ice, waste and storage before manufacture.
The bar’s shape should follow the premises and operational needs rather than be assumed from the concept.
Specifying Textured Materials for Commercial Use
Woven finishes and textured fabrics contribute to the atmosphere but need careful selection.
Design response: Review supplier information for cleaning, durability and suitability for the intended application. Upholstery performance should be confirmed for the complete specified construction.
Managing Sound and Equipment Noise
Conversation, music, glasswashing and refrigeration all contribute to the sound environment.
Design response: Coordinate equipment placement and obtain specialist acoustic advice where required. Avoid promises of eliminating noise or achieving a particular acoustic result without supporting assessment.
These are design considerations relevant to the scheme. Actual site problems, installed systems and client-requested changes require confirmation from project records.
Key Design Features
Woven Statement Lighting
Textured pendants add character and help define individual settings. Their scale, mounting position and light output should be coordinated with the tables and circulation below.
Warm Timber Details
Timber tones provide continuity across furniture and joinery, creating a welcoming framework for the interior.
Botanical Accents
Planting introduces variation and helps frame selected views and seating areas, with maintenance considered from the outset.
Comfortable Seating Choices
A combination of lounge seating, banquettes and dining furniture can accommodate different guest preferences and visit lengths.
Coordinated Service Storage
Discreet cupboards and service stations support daily operation while keeping guest-facing areas organised.
