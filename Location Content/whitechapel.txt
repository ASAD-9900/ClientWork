How Can You Make a Whitechapel Apartment Feel More Spacious?
Start with furniture scale, storage and clear movement routes.
Oversized seating, deep cupboards and poorly positioned dining furniture can make a room difficult to use. We test dimensions before recommending individual pieces.
Storage should reflect what you own, while lighting and window treatments should support the room’s proportions and available daylight.
A considered arrangement can make the existing floor area more useful without requiring every item to be bespoke.
Discuss Your Apartment Layout
How Can You Retain Warehouse Character While Making the Interior Comfortable?
Let the existing structure guide the scheme, then introduce softer elements where needed.
Brickwork, beams and columns can influence furniture placement and material choices. Upholstery, rugs, curtains and suitable acoustic treatments may help balance harder surfaces.
We consider lighting, storage and the relationship between different activities alongside the architectural features. The condition and treatment of existing fabric should be assessed by appropriate specialists before work begins.
What Does a Hybrid Office Need Beyond Desks?
Plan for the activities that bring people into the workplace.
A team may need space for project discussions, video calls, presentations and focused work alongside individual workstations.
We discuss attendance patterns, equipment and meeting frequency before exploring the layout. Shared storage, power access and suitable lighting also contribute to everyday comfort.
Furniture should support the proposed arrangement and remain practical to move or adapt where flexibility is required.
How Can You Provide Quieter Calls and Meetings in an Open Office?
Consider enclosure, location, ventilation and sound together.
A phone booth or meeting room needs to accommodate its users comfortably as well as reduce disturbance. Its position should allow convenient access without interrupting nearby workstations.
Sound can travel through doors, ceilings, ventilation routes and surrounding construction. We establish the intended level of privacy and coordinate specialist input where required.
Product ratings should be assessed within the proposed installation rather than treated as a guarantee of complete privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can you design one room in Whitechapel?
Yes. We can prepare a proposal for an individual living room, bedroom, kitchen, home office or another defined space, as well as larger projects.
Do you work on homes as well as offices?
Yes. Our service covers residential and commercial interiors, with the approach tailored to the property and its use.

Yes. We can develop the agreed interior design information alongside your appointed team. Responsibilities for measurements, specialist details and installation are established at the outset.
Can we retain existing furniture?
Yes. Share photographs and dimensions so these pieces can be considered within the layout and wider scheme.
Can you coordinate with our landlord or building manager?

Yes, where the scope allows. An overall design direction helps the phases connect and identifies decisions that could affect later work.
