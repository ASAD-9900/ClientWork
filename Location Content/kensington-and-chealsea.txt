How Can You Modernise a Period Home Without Losing Its Character?
Start with the features that give the property its identity.
Cornices, fireplaces, panelling and room proportions can guide furniture placement, lighting and material choices. Contemporary furniture and finishes can sit comfortably alongside original details when their scale and relationship are carefully considered.
We explore how the rooms should function before developing the decorative scheme. Where proposed changes may affect protected features, the necessary specialist advice and permissions should be established before work begins.
How Can You Improve Storage in a Kensington Mansion Flat?
Plan storage around specific belongings and daily routines.
Coats, luggage, clothing, paperwork and household equipment need different cupboard dimensions and access. We assess these requirements before recommending fitted cabinetry or freestanding furniture.
Entrance cupboards, bedroom wardrobes and living-room storage should support the room’s main activities while allowing comfortable movement. Building management requirements should also be checked early where refurbishment work is proposed.

Can You Furnish a Chelsea Property While We Are Abroad?
Yes. Remote design and sourcing support can form part of your appointment.
Online meetings, layouts and presentations help establish the brief and record your design approvals. We agree how surveys, samples, purchasing, deliveries and any site coordination will be handled.
An agreed decision schedule helps the project progress when you cannot regularly visit the property. Your proposal confirms the level of support and reporting included.
How Can You Make a Restaurant or Bar Feel More Comfortable?
Consider seating, lighting, circulation and sound as part of one scheme.
A visually distinctive interior also needs comfortable tables, practical service routes and suitable storage. Lighting should support the intended atmosphere while allowing customers and staff to use the space comfortably.
Upholstery, window treatments and appropriate acoustic finishes may contribute to comfort, with specialist assessment where required. We develop the layout around the experience you want to offer and the way your team operates.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can you design a single room in Kensington or Chelsea?
Yes. We can prepare a proposal for an individual living room, bedroom, kitchen, home office or another defined space, as well as larger projects.
Can you work alongside our architect or builder?
Yes. We can develop the interior scheme alongside your appointed team, with responsibilities and information requirements agreed at the outset.
Can we retain our existing furniture and artwork?
Yes. Share photographs and dimensions so we can consider these pieces within the layout and wider design.
Can you help with interiors in a listed property?
We can develop an interior brief that considers the property’s character. Proposed alterations should be reviewed with the appropriate conservation, architectural and technical specialists.
Can refurbishment work be completed in stages?
Yes, where the scope allows. An overall design direction helps the phases connect and identifies decisions that could affect later work.
Do you source furniture, lighting and fabrics?
Yes. Product selection and procurement support can be included within your appointment, with purchasing and delivery responsibilities agreed in advance.
How long will the project take?
Timing depends on the design scope, approvals, product lead times and building work involved. An indicative programme is established once these requirements are understood.
