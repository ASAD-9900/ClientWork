How Can You Modernise a Richmond Period Home Without Losing Its Character?
Begin with the architectural features you want to preserve and enjoy.
Fireplaces, cornices, panelling and original doors can guide furniture placement, lighting and material choices. Contemporary pieces can sit comfortably alongside these details when scale and finishes are carefully considered.
We explore how the rooms should function before refining the decorative scheme. Where alterations may affect protected features, appropriate permissions and specialist advice should be established before work begins.
Discuss Your Period Home
How Can You Connect the Kitchen and Garden Without Losing Useful Space?
Test the interior arrangement alongside the proposed doors and glazing.
Larger openings can strengthen the visual connection with the garden, but they also reduce wall space available for cabinetry, radiators and furniture.
We explore kitchen units, dining chairs and seating together, checking that access outdoors remains comfortable. Storage, lighting and privacy should develop alongside the layout.
Where changes involve building work, technical details need to be coordinated with the appropriate specialists.
Can You Keep a Separate Sitting Room Alongside an Open Kitchen and Dining Area?
Yes. Different rooms can support different parts of family life.
A connected kitchen and dining area may suit cooking, homework and shared meals. A separate sitting room can provide somewhere quieter for reading, television or conversation.
We consider who uses each room, at what time and for which activities. Furniture, doors and lighting can help establish distinct settings while maintaining a consistent design direction.
How Can You Introduce Colour Into a Home Without Making Every Room Different?
Develop a palette that relates to the light, existing finishes and adjoining spaces.
Colour can appear through upholstery, cabinetry, curtains, artwork or painted walls. You do not need to use the same shade throughout the property to create a connected interior.
We consider how colours look alongside timber, flooring and other materials, using samples and presentations to help you assess the relationships before selections are confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can you design one room in Richmond upon Thames?
Yes. We can prepare a proposal for an individual kitchen, living room, bedroom, home office or another defined space, as well as larger projects.
Can you work with our existing architect or builder?
Yes. We can develop the agreed interior design information alongside your appointed team. Responsibilities for measurements, specialist details and installation are established at the outset.
Can you help with the interior of a listed property?
We can develop a design brief that considers the property’s character. Proposed alterations should be reviewed with the appropriate conservation, architectural and technical specialists.
Can we retain our existing furniture and artwork?
Yes. Share photographs and dimensions so these pieces can be considered within the layout and wider scheme.
Can the refurbishment be completed in phases?
Yes, where the scope allows. An overall design direction helps the phases connect and identifies decisions that could affect later work.
Do you design commercial premises as well as homes?
Yes. Our service covers residential and commercial interiors, with the approach tailored to the property and its use.
