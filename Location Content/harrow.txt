Can You Improve a Harrow Home Without a Full Renovation?
A focused design scheme can address specific problems while retaining suitable existing elements.
You may need better furniture placement, more useful lighting or storage close to where belongings are used. A room can also feel disconnected because its colours, finishes and furniture were selected at different times.
We assess what deserves to stay before recommending replacements. New upholstery, window treatments or a carefully chosen cabinet may help bring the room together.
Where building work would offer a greater benefit, we explain how it relates to the wider brief.
Should You Plan the Interior Before Starting an Extension?
Developing the interior alongside the architectural layout helps test what the new space needs to do.
We examine the proposed footprint using furniture, kitchen and storage dimensions. This can reveal whether dining chairs interrupt a route to the garden, whether a seating area has sufficient wall space or where lighting and sockets will be needed.
The work can inform discussions with your architect before key dimensions are fixed. Responsibilities for structural and specialist design remain clearly identified.
