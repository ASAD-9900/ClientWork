How Can You Make an Open-Plan Apartment Feel More Comfortable?
Give each part of the room a clear role before choosing furniture.
An open room can feel unfinished when everything sits against the perimeter or when several large pieces compete for the same space.
We begin with the main activities: cooking, eating, relaxing, working and entertaining. Furniture positions then establish useful relationships between them.
A reading chair may sit away from the television area. A dining pendant can provide focus above the table. A rug can bring the main seating together. The arrangement should leave clear movement routes and preserve access to windows, doors and building services.
How Do You Manage Privacy and Glare Around Large Windows?
Window treatments and furniture placement should be planned together.
The right solution depends on the apartment’s orientation, neighbouring views and how you use each room.
A living area may need daytime screening while retaining useful daylight. A bedroom may need a separate blackout layer. A desk or television may work better in a different position rather than relying entirely on blinds to control reflections.
We consider the appearance of the treatment alongside its operation, fixing requirements and access for maintenance. Specialist advice may be needed where overheating is a concern.
Can You Add a Home Office Without Losing Your Living Space?
A permanent desk is not always the best answer for a compact apartment.
Depending on the layout, options may include a shallow workstation, a desk integrated into storage or a defined position within a larger bedroom. We assess seating comfort, lighting, sockets and somewhere to put equipment when work finishes.
For two people working from home, privacy and competing calls may matter more than fitting two desks into the same room. Understanding the routine helps us test the most useful arrangement.
What Should You Check Before Refurbishing a Canary Wharf Apartment?
Establish the building’s requirements before committing to work or ordering large items.
Ask your managing agent for the information relevant to your proposed scope. Depending on the building and work involved, this may cover permissions, contractor documentation, working arrangements and protection of shared areas.
Delivery planning is equally useful. Check lift dimensions, access routes, booking procedures and where items can be received. A piece of furniture that fits the room may still be difficult to bring into the property.
Where included in our appointment, we can help coordinate the design information and access arrangements with your appointed team.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do you work on smaller apartments as well as penthouses?
Yes. We can develop a proposal for a compact apartment, an individual room or a larger residence. The scope is based on your requirements.
Can you furnish our apartment while we are overseas?
We can discuss a service covering design, sourcing and agreed installation coordination. Property access, approvals, purchasing decisions and updates are established at the outset.
Can we keep the developer’s kitchen and bathrooms?
Yes, where they remain suitable. We can develop the surrounding interior around existing fittings, directing the budget towards furniture, lighting, storage or other priorities.
Can you work with our existing contractor?
Yes. We can provide the agreed design information alongside your appointed contractor. Responsibilities for measurements, specialist details and installation are confirmed before work begins.
Can you help an apartment feel less echoey?
Soft furnishings, rugs and suitable upholstered elements may improve how the room sounds. More significant noise or vibration problems require assessment by an appropriate acoustic specialist.

The programme depends on the scope, decisions required, product availability and access arrangements. We establish these dependencies before agreeing a timetable.
