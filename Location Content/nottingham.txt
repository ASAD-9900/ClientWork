How Can You Make a Nottingham City-Centre Apartment Feel More Spacious?
Start with furniture proportions, clear circulation and storage that suits the room.
A large sofa or dining table can make an apartment difficult to move through, even when there appears to be enough floor space.
We test furniture dimensions and positions before finalising selections. Storage is planned around the items you need to accommodate, while lighting helps make each part of the room useful.
In an open-plan apartment, a defined seating area and compact dining arrangement can bring order without adding partitions.

Can You Create a Home Workspace in an Open-Plan Apartment?
Yes. A defined workspace can fit into the wider room when its dimensions and storage are carefully considered.
A compact desk, suitable chair and task lighting provide a starting point. Socket positions, daylight and screen glare also influence where the workspace should sit.
Storage for paperwork and equipment helps the room feel settled when the working day ends. Coordinated colours and materials can connect the desk with the rest of the interior.
We consider the workspace alongside dining and seating so each activity has sufficient room.
What Should You Check Before Refurbishing a Leasehold Apartment in Nottingham?
Check your lease and building management requirements before committing to the works.
The proposed scope may affect what permissions, drawings or contractor information are needed.
Delivery access, lift dimensions, permitted working hours and protection of communal areas can also influence the installation programme. Flooring changes may need particular attention where sound transmission is a concern.
We identify these coordination requirements early and establish responsibilities within the appointment, working alongside your appointed team where agreed.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can you design one room in Nottingham?
Yes. We can prepare a proposal for an individual living room, bedroom, kitchen, home office or another defined space, as well as larger projects.
Can you work with our existing builder or architect?
Yes. We can develop the agreed interior design information alongside your appointed team. Responsibilities for measurements, specialist details and installation are established at the outset.
Can you help furnish a city-centre rental apartment?
Yes. We can develop a furnishing proposal around the property’s dimensions, intended occupants, maintenance requirements and agreed budget.
Can we retain existing furniture?
Yes. Share photographs and dimensions so these pieces can be considered within the layout and wider design.
Can you redesign a restaurant without replacing the entire interior?
Yes, where the existing premises allow. We can assess how revised seating, lighting, finishes or selected furniture could support your objectives.
Can the project be completed in phases?
Yes, where the scope allows. An overall design direction helps the phases connect and identifies decisions that could affect later work.
How are site visits and meetings arranged?
The proposal sets out the agreed meetings, site attendance and any associated travel charges.
How long does an interior design project take?
The programme depends on the scope, design decisions, specialist input and product availability. We discuss the anticipated timescale when preparing your proposal.
