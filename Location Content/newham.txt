How Can You Make an Open-Plan Apartment Feel More Organised?
Give each activity a clear place within the layout.
A sofa, dining table and desk need to work together without blocking access to windows, doors or storage.
We explore furniture scale, circulation and the relationship between living and kitchen areas. Rugs, lighting and selected finishes can help define spaces without adding partitions.
The aim is a comfortable arrangement suited to your routines and the room’s dimensions.
