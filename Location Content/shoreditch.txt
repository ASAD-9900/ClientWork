How Can You Add Storage Without Hiding Exposed Brickwork or Original Features?
Identify the features you want to emphasise, then plan storage around them.
A freestanding cabinet, low media unit or fitted cupboard within a recess may provide useful storage while leaving the main architectural elements visible.
We consider the items you need to accommodate before choosing open shelving, closed cupboards or a combination of both.
Dimensions, access and the condition of existing surfaces help determine the most appropriate solution. Any fixings or alterations should be assessed for the individual property.

Build the identity around a clear concept and a workable layout.

We consider seating options, counter positions and service routes before developing the material palette.
Finishes should suit the expected wear and cleaning requirements. Where neighbouring properties or specialist equipment affect the brief, the appropriate technical input should inform the design.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can you design one room in Shoreditch?
Yes. We can prepare a proposal for an individual living room, bedroom, home office or another defined space, as well as larger projects.
Can you work with exposed brickwork and existing industrial features?
Yes. We can develop the interior around features you want to retain, considering furniture proportions, materials and lighting alongside the existing architecture.
Can you work with our builder or architect?
Yes. We can develop the agreed interior design information alongside your appointed team. Responsibilities for measurements, specialist details and installation are established at the outset.
Can you help with noise concerns in a hospitality venue?
We can consider the interior layout and finishes alongside advice from a qualified acoustic specialist. The appropriate solution depends on the building, proposed use and assessment findings.
Can you retain our existing furniture?
Yes. Share photographs and dimensions so these pieces can be assessed within the layout and wider design.
Can you design a venue that operates during the day and evening?
Yes. We can explore furniture arrangements, lighting controls and storage around the different activities and service requirements.
Can the refurbishment be completed in phases?
Yes, where the scope allows. An overall design direction helps connect the phases and identify decisions that could affect later work.
How long will the project take?
The programme depends on the scope, design decisions, specialist input and product availability. We discuss the anticipated timescale when preparing your proposal.
