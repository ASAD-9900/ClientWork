How Can You Update a 1930s Ruislip Home Without Losing Its Character?
Start with the features you enjoy and the practical changes you need.
Bay windows, fireplaces, internal doors and room proportions can guide furniture placement and material choices. Contemporary furniture and lighting can work alongside these features without requiring the same style throughout.
We assess the layout before developing the decorative scheme. Where proposed alterations affect the structure or protected features, appropriate specialist advice and permissions should be established before work begins.
Discuss Your Family Home
Should You Combine the Kitchen and Dining Room or Keep Them Separate?
The right arrangement depends on how your household uses both spaces.
A connected kitchen and dining area may make family meals and entertaining easier. A separate dining room may remain useful for larger gatherings, homework or occasional work.
We compare layouts with cabinetry and furniture in place, considering storage, noise and routes to the garden. A wider doorway or partial connection may sometimes provide the improvement you need.
Any proposed structural changes require appropriate assessment.
How Can You Improve a Narrow Hallway Without Making It Feel Crowded?
Prioritise clear movement and storage of the right depth.
A hallway needs to accommodate doors opening, people passing and everyday belongings. Deep cupboards or oversized furniture can make it harder to use.
We assess the items you need to store before exploring hooks, shallow cabinetry, benches or under-stair solutions. Lighting, mirrors and wall finishes can then support the overall arrangement.
Which Flooring Is Practical for a Busy Family Home?
Compare maintenance, comfort and durability alongside appearance.
Entrance halls, kitchens and living rooms experience different levels of wear, moisture and cleaning. Timber, luxury vinyl and porcelain each have advantages and limitations that should be assessed for the intended room.
We consider the subfloor, transitions between spaces, compatibility with any heating system and the manufacturer’s care requirements. Samples help you assess colour and texture alongside the wider scheme.
