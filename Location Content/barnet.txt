Can You Improve a Barnet House Without Extending?
A better layout may make the existing floor area more useful.
Before planning additional space, we examine why the current arrangement feels restrictive. A doorway may interrupt kitchen storage, a dining room may sit unused, or the route to the garden may run through the main seating area.
For a semi-detached or detached house, we can explore different relationships between the kitchen, dining room and reception spaces. A separate front room may remain valuable for quieter activities, while the rear rooms could benefit from a closer connection.
We test these options using furniture dimensions and practical circulation. Any proposal involving structural changes or altered services needs the appropriate specialist assessment before implementation.
Discuss Your Ground-Floor Layout
When Should You Involve an Interior Designer in an Extension?
Ideally, while the architectural layout is still being developed.
An extension’s dimensions should accommodate the way you intend to use it. Testing the interior early helps establish whether there is enough space for cabinetry, dining chairs, seating and access to the garden.
We consider:
Kitchen storage and appliance positions.
Dining-table dimensions and clearance around chairs.
Television placement and daylight glare.
Useful wall space for furniture.
Lighting, sockets and switches.
The relationship between shared areas and quieter rooms.
This work can inform discussions with your architect before key decisions are fixed.
How Can Bespoke Storage Make Family Life Easier?
The most effective storage sits close to where it is needed.
In the hallway, that might mean shallow cupboards for coats and school bags. In a reception room, it could be cabinetry that conceals cables and everyday clutter while leaving space for books and artwork.
Bedrooms need a different approach: hanging space, drawers and shelving should reflect what you own. A home office may require accessible paperwork storage and somewhere to put equipment when the working day ends.
We compare fitted and freestanding options, recommending bespoke work where it offers a useful advantage.
What Should You Budget for Interior Design in Barnet?

Furniture, lighting and window treatments.
Kitchens, bathrooms and fitted joinery.
Building works and installation.
Relevant surveys or specialist appointments.
VAT, delivery charges and contingency where applicable.
Setting priorities early helps us recommend where to invest and where existing elements can be retained. For larger projects, we can also discuss a phased approach.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I appoint Oraanj for just one room in Barnet?
Yes. We can prepare a proposal for an individual living room, bedroom, home office or another defined space. The scope is tailored to the design support you need.
Can you work with my existing architect or builder?
Yes. We can develop the interior scheme alongside your appointed team. Responsibilities for measurements, drawings, specialist information and installation are established at the outset.
Can you retain our existing furniture?
Yes. Share photographs and dimensions of the pieces you want to keep. We consider their placement before recommending complementary furniture, finishes and lighting.
Can you help with an older Barnet property?
Yes. We can develop designs around existing features and the property’s proportions. Where proposed alterations require specialist advice or approval, that needs to be established before work proceeds.
Can our home be redesigned in stages?
Yes, where the project allows. Agreeing an overall direction first helps separate phases connect, particularly when flooring, lighting or building services affect several rooms.
How long will the project take?
We prepare a timetable after reviewing the scope. Design development, decision-making, product availability and any necessary specialist work all influence the programme.
