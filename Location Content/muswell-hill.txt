How Can You Update an Edwardian Home Without Losing Its Character?
Start with the features you want to preserve and enjoy.
Fireplaces, stained glass, ceiling details and original doors can influence furniture placement, colours and lighting. Contemporary pieces can sit comfortably alongside these features when proportions and finishes are carefully considered.
We explore how the rooms should function before developing the decorative scheme. Where alterations may affect protected features, appropriate permissions and specialist advice should be established before work begins.
Discuss Your Period Home
Should You Open Up the Rear of the House and Keep a Separate Front Lounge?
The right arrangement depends on how your household spends time at home.
A connected kitchen and dining area can support cooking, homework and shared meals. A separate front room may remain valuable for quiet reading, television or private conversations.
We compare layouts with furniture in place, considering noise, storage, doorways and routes through the house. Sometimes a revised opening or glazed division can improve the relationship between rooms without combining everything into one space.
Any proposed structural changes require appropriate assessment.
How Can You Keep a Busy Family Hallway Organised?
Place storage where belongings naturally arrive.
Coats, shoes, school bags and deliveries can quickly occupy circulation space. We assess the available width, door openings and items you need to store before recommending an arrangement.
Hooks, cupboards, benches and shoe drawers serve different purposes. A useful solution provides convenient everyday access while leaving enough space to move comfortably through the entrance.
What Should You Plan Before Designing the Interior of a Kitchen Extension?
Test the proposed space with cabinetry and furniture in place.
An extension should accommodate the activities you want to bring into it. Kitchen units, dining chairs, seating and garden access all need sufficient space.
Large windows and doors can influence storage, lighting and furniture positions. We consider these relationships alongside the connection to the existing house.
Developing the interior layout early helps inform discussions with your architect, kitchen supplier and contractor.
