Frequently Asked Questions
What is the design style most requested in Totteridge?
Most clients in Totteridge seek a blend of timeless classic English architectural elegance with clean, contemporary luxury. We frequently combine traditional mouldings, bespoke wood panelling, and natural stone with contemporary lighting, sleek kitchens, and bespoke tailored furniture.
Can Oraanj manage the physical renovation works in Totteridge?
Yes. We offer complete end-to-end design and build management. We prepare all architectural drawings, submit planning and conservation applications, tender works to trusted contractors, and supervise construction to completion.
Do you work on properties in the Totteridge Conservation Area?
Yes. A significant portion of Totteridge is covered by the Totteridge Conservation Area and Green Belt protections. We work closely with the London Borough of Barnet planning and conservation officers to ensure all proposals respect local architectural character.
How long does a typical interior design and refurbishment project take in Totteridge?
Timelines depend on the scale of work. A focused interior redesign of key rooms typically takes 3 to 5 months. A comprehensive whole-house architectural renovation and extension typically spans 9 to 18 months including design, planning approvals, and build phases.
Can we live in the property during the renovation work?
For full-scale turnkey renovations and structural works, we generally recommend vacating the property to ensure safety, accelerate construction timelines, and avoid dust and disruption. For phased interior projects, living on site can be accommodated with careful sequencing.
Can you design around fine art and antique collections?
Yes. Many of our Totteridge clients possess notable art collections and family heirlooms. We design bespoke display joinery, conservation-grade directional lighting, and curated colour palettes specifically around your existing pieces.
