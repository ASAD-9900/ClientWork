Should You Connect the Kitchen, Dining and Living Areas in a Family Home?
The right arrangement depends on how your household uses each space.
A connected kitchen and dining area can make shared activities easier. A separate sitting room may remain valuable for quiet work, television or time away from a busy kitchen.
We test layouts with furniture in place, considering storage, doorways and routes to the garden. Any proposed structural changes require appropriate specialist assessment.
How Can You Improve Storage in a Kingston Apartment?
Start with the belongings you need to accommodate.
Coats, shoes, luggage, cleaning equipment and paperwork each need different storage dimensions and access.
We assess entrance areas, bedroom walls and underused corners before comparing fitted cabinetry with freestanding furniture. The solution should improve organisation while allowing enough space for the room’s main activities.
Where refurbishment work is proposed, building management requirements should be checked early.
