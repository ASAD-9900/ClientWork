How Can You Update a Period Interior While Retaining Its Character?
Start with the features that give the rooms their identity.
Cornices, fireplaces, doorways and ceiling heights can guide furniture placement, lighting and material choices. Contemporary pieces can sit comfortably alongside original details when scale and finishes are carefully considered.

Where Does Bespoke Joinery Make the Greatest Difference?
Where standard furniture does not adequately address the dimensions or storage requirements.
