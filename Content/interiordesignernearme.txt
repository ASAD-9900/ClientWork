How Are Zones Counted?
Different functional areas are treated as separate zones even when they are within the same open-plan room.
For example:
Kitchen — 1 zone
Dining — 1 zone
Living — 1 zone
An open-plan kitchen, dining and living space is therefore 3 zones.
For larger whole-property projects, send us the floor plan first so we can discuss the appropriate service rather than assuming individual room packages are the best approach.
