What Does Luxury Interior Design Mean to Us?
Not More Decoration. Better Decisions.
A high-end interior is not created by filling a property with expensive products.
It comes from getting hundreds of individual decisions to work together.
The proportions of the furniture
How people move through the property
Where lighting falls
How joinery relates to the architecture
The quality and suitability of materials
What is concealed and what is displayed
How rooms connect visually
How the property feels during the day and evening
How the home works when entertaining
How it works when nobody else is there
The result should feel considered without feeling over-designed.

Luxury Interior Design Questions
What does a luxury interior designer do?
A luxury interior designer can help coordinate the many decisions involved in creating a high-end home, from layout and interior architecture through materials, lighting, bespoke joinery, furniture and finishing details. The exact scope depends on the property and appointment.
What makes an interior feel luxurious?
There is no single material or style that makes an interior luxurious. Proportion, space planning, material quality, lighting, bespoke detailing, furniture and consistency all contribute to the finished result.
Does Oraanj have one signature design style?
No. The interior is developed around the individual client, property and brief rather than applying the same visual style to every home.
Do you design complete London homes?
Yes. Oraanj works on individual spaces and complete residential properties. Larger whole-home projects are individually scoped.
Do you work on period properties?
Yes. The approach depends on the individual building and can consider existing architectural features alongside contemporary interior requirements.
Do you design penthouses and apartments?
Yes. Oraanj works on apartments and larger London properties, with the design developed around the particular layout, architecture and client brief.
Can you design bespoke furniture and joinery?
Yes. Bespoke joinery can include wardrobes, media walls, dressing rooms, home offices, shelving and other fitted elements according to the project.
Can you source furniture and lighting?
Yes. Furniture, lighting and FF&E sourcing or procurement can form part of the wider appointment.
Can you manage the renovation as well?
Yes. For suitable projects, Oraanj's Design & Build service can continue from interior design into renovation and project delivery according to the agreed scope.
Can you work with my architect or builder?
Yes. Oraanj can work alongside an existing project team, with responsibilities and required design information established at the beginning.
Can you manage my London property project while I am overseas?
Yes, for suitable projects. Oraanj provides London-based design and project support for overseas clients according to the agreed scope.
How much does luxury interior design cost in London?
The cost depends on the size of the property, design scope, furniture, joinery, materials, renovation requirements and level of project delivery. Substantial properties are individually scoped rather than priced using a single standard fee.
