Window Treatment Design in London
Bespoke Curtains & Blinds Designed Around Your Interior
Choosing curtains or blinds can become surprisingly complicated.
What heading should you use? Should curtains sit inside or outside the window? Do you need sheer and blackout layers? Which fabric will hang properly? Where should the track go? And what will actually work with the furniture and rest of the room?
Oraanj provides window treatment design for London homes and selected commercial interiors, helping you bring together style, fabric, privacy, light control and the surrounding interior.
From bespoke curtains and Roman blinds to sheers, tracks, linings and selected motorised solutions, each treatment is considered around the individual window and room.
Discuss Your Windows

What Does Your Window Need to Do?
Start With Function Before Choosing the Fabric
A beautiful fabric isn't enough if the treatment doesn't work for the room.
We first consider what you need from the window.
Privacy
Do neighbouring properties overlook the room?
Do you need privacy throughout the day or mainly at night?
Light Control
Do you want to soften strong daylight without making the room dark?
Blackout
Bedrooms and children's rooms may require greater control over external light.
Glare
Living rooms and home offices may need to manage sunlight around televisions or computer screens.
Atmosphere
Curtains and blinds can substantially change how soft, warm or formal a room feels.
Architecture
The shape, proportions and position of the window influence which treatments will work visually.
Furniture
Radiators, sofas, beds, desks and fitted joinery can all affect how curtains or blinds should be planned.
Once these requirements are understood, we can consider the most appropriate treatment.

Curtains or Blinds?
Which Is Right for Your Room?
There isn't one answer for every window.
Curtains

Living Rooms

Roman Blinds

Venetian Blinds
Selected Specialist Blind Systems
Curtains & Blinds Together
Some windows benefit from layering.
For example, a blind may provide privacy or light control while curtains add softness and complete the wider interior.
The right solution depends on the room rather than a predetermined style.

Curtain Heading Styles
The Top of the Curtain Changes the Look
Different headings create different visual effects.
Depending on the room, fabric and track system, options may include:
Wave Curtains
A clean, contemporary appearance with a regular flowing shape.
Pinch Pleat Curtains
A more structured heading that can suit both contemporary and more traditional interiors.
Double or Triple Pleat
A tailored treatment that creates greater structure at the top of the curtain.
Other Bespoke Headings
Alternative treatments can be considered where appropriate to the design and selected fabric.
The choice should be based on the window, room and desired appearance rather than the heading style alone.

Roman Blinds
Soft Fabric With a More Compact Finish
Roman blinds can work particularly well where you want the softness of fabric without full curtains.
They may be appropriate for:
Bedrooms
Living Rooms
Dining Rooms
Home Offices
Smaller Windows
Windows Above Furniture
Selected Bay Windows
Fabric, lining, proportions and positioning need to be considered carefully because they affect how the blind looks both open and closed.

Sheer Curtains
Privacy Without Losing All the Daylight
Sheers can be particularly useful in London properties where windows face neighbouring buildings or busy streets.
They can soften daylight and provide a degree of daytime screening while retaining a lighter appearance.
They can be used independently or layered with heavier curtains depending on the room.

Blackout Window Treatments
For Bedrooms & Rooms Where Light Control Matters
Where stronger light control is required, the lining and overall treatment need to be considered as part of the design.
Blackout solutions may be useful for:
Principal Bedrooms
Children's Bedrooms
Guest Bedrooms
Media Rooms
The effectiveness of a blackout treatment depends not only on the fabric or lining but also on how the treatment relates to the window and surrounding openings.
We therefore avoid treating “blackout” as simply a fabric choice.

Bay Window Curtains & Blinds
Treat the Shape of the Window Carefully
Bay windows are common in London homes and often require more thought than a standard rectangular window.
Questions can include:
Should the curtains follow the shape of the bay?
Should they sit across the front of it?
Would blinds work better within individual sections?
Is there furniture or a radiator inside the bay?
How much natural light should remain when the treatment is open?
Where can the track or hardware be positioned?
The right approach depends on the individual bay and how the surrounding room is arranged.

Can We Work With Existing Curtains?
Sometimes the Best Solution Isn't Starting Again
If you already own curtains or blinds that you would like to retain, we can review whether they can work within the new interior.
This might involve considering:
Whether the size suits the new window
Whether the fabric works with the proposed palette
Whether the heading or hardware can be adapted
Whether the treatment would work better in another room
Not every existing treatment will be suitable, but it doesn't automatically need replacing.

Window Treatment Questions
What is a window treatment?
Window treatment is a general term for curtains, blinds, sheers and other products used to dress a window for privacy, light control and interior appearance.
Do you design bespoke curtains?
Yes. Bespoke curtain design can consider fabric, heading, lining, length, fullness and relevant hardware direction according to the agreed scope.
Do you design blinds?
Yes. Depending on the project, this can include Roman blinds, roller blinds and selected other blind systems.
Can you help me choose between curtains and blinds?
Yes. We consider the window, room, privacy, daylight, furniture and desired appearance before recommending a direction.
Can you help with bay windows?
Yes. Bay windows often require careful consideration of tracks, blinds, furniture, radiators and how the treatment relates to the shape of the bay.
Do you offer blackout curtains?
Blackout linings and other light-control solutions can be considered where appropriate. The overall performance also depends on how the treatment is designed and installed around the window.
Can you design motorised curtains or blinds?
Motorised treatments can be considered where appropriate, with relevant specialist supplier and installation requirements coordinated according to the project scope.
Do you supply the fabric?
Where procurement is included, approved fabrics and relevant window-treatment products can be sourced through suitable suppliers.
Do you measure the windows?
Measurement requirements depend on the service. Final dimensions for bespoke manufacture should be verified by the appointed specialist supplier or installer before production.
Can you use fabric I have already chosen?
Potentially, yes. We can review the fabric in relation to the treatment, room and practical requirements.
Can window treatments be part of my interior design project?
Yes. This is often the best approach where furniture, lighting, colour, joinery and other elements are also being designed.
