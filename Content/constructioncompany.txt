Before We Price the Works
What Makes a Construction Quote Meaningful?
A quotation is only as useful as the information behind it.
If one contractor prices a basic specification and another assumes premium materials, the figures aren't directly comparable.
Before pricing, it helps to define:
SCOPE
Exactly what work is included?
DRAWINGS
What are the contractors expected to build?
SPECIFICATION
Which materials, products and finishes have been selected?
JOINERY
What bespoke items need to be manufactured?
KITCHENS & BATHROOMS
What is supplied and what needs installation?
SPECIALIST WORK
Are structural, electrical, plumbing or other specialist requirements involved?
SITE CONDITIONS
Are there access, parking, management-company or working-hour restrictions?
PROGRAMME
When does the property need to be completed?
The clearer these items are, the easier it becomes to understand what a quotation actually covers.

Do I need drawings before asking for a construction quotation?
The level of information required depends on the project, but clearer drawings and specifications generally provide a better basis for understanding and pricing the proposed works.
Can Oraanj design the project as well?
Yes. If the design hasn't been developed, our Design & Build service can combine interior design, technical planning and project delivery.

Do you manage electrical and plumbing work?

Can you install bespoke joinery?
Yes. Bespoke wardrobes, media walls, cabinetry, counters and other made-to-measure interior elements can form part of the project.
