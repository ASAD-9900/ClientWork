What Is Feng Shui in Interior Design?
Applying Traditional Principles to the Way a Space Is Planned
Feng Shui is a traditional Chinese practice concerned with the relationship between people and their surroundings.
Within an interior, Feng Shui principles can influence considerations such as:
Furniture placement
Room orientation
Entrance and circulation
Balance within the room
Natural light
Colour
Materials
The relationship between different areas
The five elements traditionally associated with Feng Shui
At Oraanj, these considerations can be incorporated alongside practical interior design rather than used to create a particular decorative style.
Your home can remain contemporary, traditional, minimal or highly individual.
Feng Shui does not need to determine how the interior looks.

Feng Shui Isn't an Interior Style
Your Home Doesn't Need to Look "Feng Shui"
A common misconception is that a Feng Shui interior needs to have a particular appearance.
It doesn't.
You don't necessarily need:
Bamboo
Water features
Chinese decorative objects
A particular colour palette
A minimalist interior
Specific ornaments
Feng Shui principles can be considered within many different interior styles.
The starting point is the relationship between the person and the space rather than creating a themed interior.

The Five Elements
A Traditional Feng Shui Framework
Traditional Feng Shui commonly refers to five elements:
WOOD
Traditionally associated with growth and vitality.
FIRE
Traditionally associated with energy and activity.
EARTH
Traditionally associated with stability and grounding.
METAL
Traditionally associated with clarity and precision.
WATER
Traditionally associated with flow and movement.
Within an interior-design discussion, these ideas may be interpreted through colour, form, texture and material.
They do not necessarily require literal representations of each element.

Feng Shui Interior Design Questions
What is Feng Shui?
Feng Shui is a traditional Chinese practice concerned with the relationship between people and their surroundings. In interior design, its principles may be considered in relation to layout, orientation, furniture placement, balance and the use of space.
Do I need to redesign my whole home?
No. Feng Shui principles can be considered within an existing room as well as during a wider renovation or complete interior-design project.
Can Feng Shui work with a modern interior?
Yes. Feng Shui is not a particular decorative style. Its principles can be considered within contemporary, traditional and other interior styles.
Can you help with bed placement?
Yes. We can review possible bed positions while considering Feng Shui principles alongside doors, windows, wardrobes, circulation and the practical limitations of the room.
Can you help with furniture placement?
Yes. Furniture layout and circulation can form an important part of the consultation.
Can Feng Shui be incorporated into a renovation?
Yes. Considering the principles during the planning stage can provide more flexibility than trying to make changes after the renovation is complete.
Can you provide a remote consultation?
For suitable projects, yes. Floor plans, photographs, measurements and a video consultation can be used to review the space.
Does Feng Shui guarantee better health, wealth or relationships?
No. We do not present Feng Shui as a guarantee of health, wealth, relationships or other personal outcomes. Our service focuses on applying relevant Feng Shui principles alongside practical interior design and space planning.
Do you provide formal Feng Shui certification or compliance reports?
This depends on the professional credentials of the consultant appointed to your project. Any specialist certification or formal Feng Shui analysis should only be advertised where it is genuinely provided by an appropriately trained practitioner.
