The agreed design and installation scope is brought together for the completed interior.

Full-Service Interior Design Questions
What is full-service interior design?
Full-Service Interior Design manages more than the visual concept. Depending on the agreed scope, it can include space planning, interior design, furniture and lighting selection, bespoke joinery, sourcing, procurement, installation coordination and final styling.
What is the difference between Design-Only and Full-Service Interior Design?
Design-Only gives you the professional design, which you can then implement yourself. Full-Service Interior Design continues further into product selection, sourcing, procurement and agreed installation coordination.
Does Full-Service Interior Design include construction?
Not automatically. If physical renovation and construction are required, we can discuss Design & Build or Turnkey Interior Design.
Can you work with my existing builder?
Yes. Oraanj can provide the interior design service alongside your appointed contractor, subject to the agreed project scope.
Can you furnish an entire house?
Yes. Full-Service Interior Design is particularly suitable for multi-room and whole-home projects.
Can you source all the furniture?
Furniture, decorative lighting, rugs, window treatments and other agreed FF&E can be sourced according to the project scope.
Do you manage furniture orders?
Yes, where procurement is included in the agreed service.
Can you design bespoke furniture?
Yes. Bespoke joinery and custom furniture can form part of the interior design.
Can you work with furniture I already own?
Yes. Existing pieces can be incorporated into the new design where appropriate.
Can you work with clients who live overseas?
Yes. Remote design reviews and approvals can form part of the service for clients with London properties.
When should I appoint a full-service interior designer?
Ideally before major furniture, lighting, joinery or renovation decisions have been finalised. Early involvement gives the different parts of the interior more opportunity to be planned together.
