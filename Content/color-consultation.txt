Colour consultation considers the shade as part of the complete interior.

How Does Natural Light Affect Paint Colour?
The Same Colour Can Look Different From Room to Room
Natural light has a significant influence on how colour is perceived.
A room receiving strong daylight may display a colour differently from a room with more limited natural light.
The appearance can also change between morning, afternoon and evening.
This is why we don't choose colours simply because they look attractive on a paint chart.
We consider the room itself.
Where appropriate, physical samples should be reviewed within the actual space and observed under the lighting conditions in which the room will normally be used.

Understanding Undertones
Why Two Similar Colours Can Feel Completely Different
Two paints may both appear to be beige, grey, white or green and still behave very differently within the same room.
This is often because of their underlying tone.
A neutral may lean warmer or cooler.
A white may appear crisp in one setting and unexpectedly creamy in another.
A grey may reveal blue, green or warmer undertones depending on the surrounding materials and light.
These differences become particularly important when the wall colour needs to work alongside:
Timber Flooring
Stone
Kitchen Cabinetry
Upholstery
Curtains
Rugs
Existing Paintwork
Metal Finishes
Bespoke Joinery
Looking at these relationships together can help create a more coherent result.

Choosing white paint can sometimes be more difficult than choosing a stronger colour.
There are cool whites, warm whites, softer whites and whites with subtle grey, cream or other undertones.

Colour Consultation Questions
What does an interior colour consultant do?
An interior colour consultant helps you develop a colour palette that works with the room's light, existing materials, furniture and wider interior rather than choosing paint colours in isolation.
How do I choose the right paint colour for my home?
Start by considering the room's natural light, flooring, furniture, fixed finishes and adjoining spaces. Physical samples can then be reviewed within the actual room before making the final decision.
Why does my paint colour look different on the wall?
Colour can appear different because of natural light, artificial lighting, surrounding materials, the size of the painted area and other colours within the room.
Which paint colours make a room look bigger?
Lighter colours can sometimes contribute to a brighter, more open appearance, but room size is also affected by lighting, furniture scale, contrast and the wider design. There isn't one paint colour that automatically makes every room appear larger.
Can you help me choose white paint?
Yes. Different whites have different undertones and can behave differently depending on the light and surrounding materials.
Can you help with a whole-house colour scheme?
Yes. Colour can be developed across multiple rooms so individual spaces have their own character while still relating to the wider property.
Can you work with furniture and flooring I already have?
Yes. Existing items can be important reference points when developing the colour palette.
Should I test paint samples before decorating?
Where practical, yes. Reviewing physical samples within the actual room and under different lighting conditions can help before committing to a larger painted area.
Can you help choose paint finishes as well as colours?
Yes, depending on the agreed consultation scope. The appropriate finish should also take account of the surface, room conditions and required durability.
Can colour consultation be booked without full interior design?
Yes. Where your main requirement is colour guidance rather than a complete room redesign, a standalone consultation may be appropriate.
Can you provide colour consultation for commercial interiors?
Yes. Colour direction can also form part of selected office, hospitality and retail interior projects.
Can you help if I'm renovating the entire property?
Yes, although where colour is only one part of a much larger set of design decisions, a wider Interior Design service may be more appropriate.
