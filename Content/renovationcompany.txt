Bathrooms

Furniture & FF&E

The Decisions That Are Expensive to Change Later
Plan Them Before Work Begins
Renovations become harder when major decisions are left until they are urgently needed on site.
SOCKET POSITIONS
These need to relate to furniture, televisions, bedside tables, desks and equipment.
LIGHTING POSITIONS
Lighting works best when it relates to the final layout rather than being positioned before furniture is planned.
JOINERY
Made-to-measure furniture may affect sockets, lighting, skirting, cornices and surrounding finishes.
KITCHEN LAYOUT
Cabinetry, appliances, plumbing, lighting and electrical requirements all need coordination.
BATHROOM LAYOUT
Sanitaryware positions can affect plumbing, tiling, storage and lighting.
FLOOR FINISHES
Transitions between different materials need to be considered before installation.
FURNITURE
Large pieces can influence circulation, lighting and even the position of architectural elements.
Planning these relationships earlier can reduce avoidable redesign and site changes.

Can You Live in Your Home During a Renovation?
Sometimes — but it depends heavily on the work.
A focused renovation affecting one part of a property can be very different from a complete refurbishment involving kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, electrical work and multiple rooms.
Things to consider include:
Will you have a working kitchen?
Will a usable bathroom remain available?
How much dust and noise will the works create?
Will electricity or water need to be interrupted?
Are trades working throughout the property?
Will flooring or decoration affect circulation?
For larger renovations, temporary accommodation may be more practical.
The appropriate arrangement should be considered once the actual scope and programme are understood.

Home Renovation Questions
Should I speak to an interior designer or builder first?
If the renovation requires significant design decisions, it can be useful to define the layout and interior before asking contractors to price the complete works. This gives the contractor a clearer scope to consider.
How much does a home renovation cost in London?
It depends on the property's size, condition, scope and specification. Kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, layout changes, materials and level of finish can significantly affect the overall budget.
Can Oraanj renovate my whole house?
Yes. Oraanj works on complete property renovations as well as individual areas.
Do you renovate London apartments?
Yes. Apartment renovations can include interior design, space planning, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery and wider refurbishment depending on the project.
Can I stay in my property while it is being renovated?
Possibly. It depends on the extent and location of the works and whether essential facilities remain usable. Larger renovations may make temporary accommodation more practical.
Do I need planning permission for an internal renovation?
Not every internal renovation requires planning permission. Requirements depend on the property and proposed alterations. Listed buildings, structural alterations and some other works may require additional consents or specialist advice.
Can I use my own builder?
Yes. Oraanj can be appointed for design services where you already have your own contractor. Alternatively, we can discuss wider Design & Build support.
Do you design kitchens and bathrooms?
Yes. Kitchens and bathrooms can be designed as part of a wider renovation or, depending on the project, as individual areas.
Can you manage bespoke joinery?
Yes. Bespoke wardrobes, media walls, storage and other fitted furniture can form part of the renovation.
Can you manage my renovation while I live overseas?
Yes. Oraanj works with overseas clients renovating London properties, with the exact design, procurement and project-management responsibilities agreed according to the project.
