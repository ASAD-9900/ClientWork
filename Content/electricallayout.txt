Why Plan the Electrical Layout With the Interior?
Because Furniture Changes Where Electrical Points Need to Go
An electrical plan should relate to the way the room will actually be used.
For example:
Bed position affects bedside sockets and switches
Sofa position affects floor and table lamp requirements
Television position affects power and data locations
Dining-table position affects pendant placement
Kitchen layout affects appliance requirements
Desk position affects sockets and charging points
Wardrobe design can affect lighting and switch positions
Media walls can require concealed electrical provision
When these decisions are made separately, electrical points can end up in inconvenient or visually awkward positions.

Electrical & Lighting Layout Questions
What is an interior electrical layout drawing?
An interior electrical layout shows proposed positions for elements such as sockets, switches and lighting in relation to the room layout and interior design.
When should electrical positions be planned?
Ideally after the main furniture and room layout has been established but before electrical installation or first-fix work progresses too far.
Why should furniture be planned before sockets?
Because socket positions often depend on beds, televisions, desks, side tables, lamps, joinery and other furniture.
Can you plan sockets and switches for one room?
Yes. The service can be discussed for individual rooms as well as larger properties.
Can you produce an electrical plan for my whole house?
Yes, depending on the project information and required scope.
Can you plan the lighting too?
Yes. Interior lighting positions can be coordinated with the furniture layout and wider design where included within the appointment.
Can you coordinate electrics with bespoke joinery?
Yes. Proposed electrical positions can be coordinated with media walls, wardrobes, home offices and other bespoke fitted furniture.
Are these electrical engineering drawings?
No. Oraanj's service focuses on interior electrical and lighting coordination. Electrical engineering, circuit design, loads, installation, testing, certification and regulatory compliance remain with the appropriately qualified electrical professionals.
Do you provide M&E engineering?
Not as part of this interior electrical layout service. Where specialist mechanical or electrical engineering is required, the relevant M&E professionals should be appointed.
Can my electrician use the drawing?
The drawing can communicate the proposed interior requirements to your electrician. The appointed electrician should review the information, verify site conditions and determine the final electrical design and installation requirements.
Do you provide AutoCAD files?
DWG or PDF files can be provided where included within the agreed appointment. Confirm the required format before work begins.
Can you work from my architect's drawings?
Yes, subject to the information being suitable and sufficiently accurate for the required interior drawing work.
Can this be part of a wider renovation?
Yes. Electrical and lighting coordination can form part of Oraanj's wider Interior Architecture, Design & Technical or Design & Build services according to scope.
