What Are Technical Interior Design Drawings?
Moving From the Approved Design Into Detail
A 3D visual helps you understand what an interior could look like.
A technical interior drawing communicates specific information about the design in a measured two-dimensional format.
Depending on the project, drawings can help communicate:
Where furniture is positioned
How interior walls are arranged
How fitted joinery relates to the room
Where proposed lighting is positioned
Where proposed sockets and switches relate to furniture and joinery
How wall elevations are composed
Key dimensions
Selected material and finish information
The exact drawing package depends on what has been designed and what information is required for the agreed interior scope.

When Do You Need Technical Drawings?
Your Interior Design Is Approved
Once the visual direction has been agreed, more detailed drawings may be required to communicate particular elements of the design.
Your Contractor Needs More Information
A 3D image or mood board may not provide enough information to understand dimensions, positions and relationships between different interior elements.
You're Planning Bespoke Joinery
Wardrobes, media walls, shelving, cabinetry and other fitted elements need to be developed in relation to the surrounding room.
You're Planning Lighting & Electrical Positions
Sockets, switches and lighting positions need to relate to furniture, kitchens, joinery and the way the room will be used.
You're Renovating Several Rooms
More detailed design information can help communicate the relationship between different parts of the approved interior.
Your Design Has Several Bespoke Elements
The more customised an interior becomes, the more useful clearly organised design information can be.

Do You Need M&E Drawings?
M&E Is Different From Interior Technical Design
M&E means Mechanical & Electrical services.
A full M&E design can involve specialist information relating to electrical systems, mechanical services, ventilation, heating, cooling and other building services.
Oraanj's interior technical drawing service should not automatically be interpreted as a full M&E engineering service.
Where specialist M&E design is required, this should be undertaken or verified by the appropriately qualified consultant.
Oraanj can coordinate relevant interior design information with the wider consultant team where included within the project scope.

Technical Drawing Questions
What is a technical interior drawing?
A technical interior drawing communicates specific information about an approved interior design using measured plans, elevations or other two-dimensional drawings.
What is the difference between a floor plan and an elevation?
A floor plan looks down on the room from above and shows the arrangement of the space. An elevation looks directly at a selected wall and can show elements such as joinery, furniture, lighting and finishes.
Can I book technical drawings without interior design?
Potentially. If your design is already sufficiently developed and reliable dimensions and information are available, we can review whether a standalone technical drawing service is appropriate.
Do you provide DWG files?
DWG files can be provided where included within the agreed service.
Can you create drawings from a PDF?
Potentially, depending on the quality and accuracy of the information available. We need to review the existing file before confirming what can be produced from it.
Do you provide lighting drawings?
Yes. Interior lighting layouts can be developed where included within the agreed scope.
Do you provide electrical drawings?
Oraanj can coordinate proposed interior positions for sockets, switches, lighting controls and selected electrical points. These drawings do not replace specialist electrical engineering, installation design, testing or certification.
Do you provide bespoke joinery drawings?
Yes. Interior joinery drawings can communicate the intended appearance, arrangement and relationship with the surrounding interior. Specialist fabricators remain responsible for verifying dimensions and producing any manufacturing information required for fabrication.
Are Oraanj's drawings construction drawings?
They are technical interior design drawings developed according to the agreed scope. They should not automatically be interpreted as complete construction, structural, M&E, statutory or specialist fabrication documentation.
Can you coordinate with my contractor?
Where included within the appointment, Oraanj can communicate and coordinate relevant interior design information with the wider project team.
How long do technical drawings take?
The programme depends on the number and complexity of drawings and the quality of the information available. Timing should be confirmed once the drawing scope has been established.
