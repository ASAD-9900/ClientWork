Courtyards

Landscape & Garden Design Questions
What does a garden designer do?
A garden designer plans how an outdoor space should look and function, considering elements such as layout, seating, dining, materials, planting, lighting, privacy and circulation according to the project scope.
What is the difference between garden design and landscaping?
Garden design establishes the proposed layout and appearance of the outdoor space. Landscaping refers to the physical work involved in creating it.
Can you design a small London garden?
Yes. Smaller gardens and courtyards can particularly benefit from careful space planning because the available area needs to accommodate the client's priorities without becoming overcrowded.
Can you design a roof terrace?
Yes, depending on the project. Roof terraces may also require specialist advice relating to structural loading, drainage, waterproofing, wind, balustrades and other technical matters.
Can you design an outdoor dining area?
Yes. The design can consider table size, seating, circulation, lighting and its relationship with the house, garden and any outdoor cooking area.
Can you help with garden lighting?
Garden lighting can form part of the wider design direction. Detailed electrical design and installation should be handled by appropriately qualified professionals where required.
Do you provide planting plans?
Planting direction can form part of the landscape design scope. Where detailed horticultural specifications or specialist planting advice are required, appropriate specialist input may be recommended.
Can you create a 3D design of my garden?
Yes, where included within the project scope. 3D visualisation can help show the relationship between furniture, materials, planting and the wider garden before implementation.
Can you design the garden if I already have a landscaper?
Yes. Oraanj can discuss developing the design separately for implementation by your existing landscape contractor.
Can landscape design be part of my home renovation?
Yes. This can be particularly useful where new doors, glazing, kitchen areas, interior flooring or lighting affect the relationship between the house and garden.
Do you design commercial outdoor spaces?
Oraanj can develop outdoor design for selected commercial projects including restaurant, café, office and hospitality terraces.
How do I start?
Send us photographs, plans or dimensions, tell us how you want to use the outdoor space and let us know whether you are considering design only or wider project delivery.
