How Do We Count Rooms & Zones?
Packages Are Priced Per Room or Functional Zone
Each room or functional area is treated as an individual design zone.
This is particularly important for open-plan interiors.
For Example: Kitchen + Dining + Living = 3 Zones
Even if all three areas are within one open-plan room, they have different functions and design requirements.
They are therefore treated as:
Kitchen — 1 zone
Dining — 1 zone
Living — 1 zone

What Is a Functional Zone?
Different Uses Can Mean Different Design Areas
A functional zone is an area with its own purpose and design requirements.
Examples can include:
Kitchen
Dining Area
Living Area
Home Office
Reading Area
Dressing Area
Entrance / Hallway
A large open-plan interior may therefore contain several individual zones even though there are no walls separating them.
If you're unsure how your property will be counted, send us your floor plan before choosing a package.

Interior Design Package Questions
How much are Oraanj's interior design packages?
Oraanj offers three room-based packages: One Design at £600, Unlimited Design at £900 and Design & Technical at £1,500 per room or functional zone.
What counts as a zone?
A zone is an area with its own function and design requirements. For example, an open-plan kitchen, dining and living area is treated as three zones: kitchen, dining and living.
How much would kitchen, dining and living cost?
As these are three functional zones, the total would be £1,800 with One Design, £2,700 with Unlimited Design or £4,500 with Design & Technical.
What is included in One Design?
One Design includes one final 3D design, a basic furniture layout, one round of revisions and the overall visual direction for the room.
What is included in Unlimited Design?
Unlimited Design includes 3D visualisation, unlimited design revisions within the agreed brief, a refined furniture layout, furniture and styling guidance and colour, material and finish direction.
What is included in Design & Technical?
Depending on the room and agreed scope, Design & Technical can include 3D design, detailed space planning, wall elevations, lighting and electrical layouts, bespoke joinery design and technical interior information.
Does Design & Technical include building work?
No. Design & Technical is an interior design and technical design package. Physical renovation, refurbishment or construction is separate.
Can I use my own builder?
Yes. You can appoint Oraanj for the design and use your own contractor for implementation.
Is furniture included in the package price?
No. The package price covers the agreed design service. Furniture, lighting, materials and other products are separate unless specifically stated otherwise.
Can Oraanj source the furniture?
Yes. Interior sourcing is available separately.
Can Oraanj order everything for me?
Yes. Procurement can be provided separately where you want Oraanj to manage agreed purchasing, suppliers and deliveries.
Can Oraanj renovate the property too?
Yes. Design & Build is available separately for clients who want Oraanj to continue from interior design into renovation and project delivery.
What if I'm designing my whole house?
Send us the complete floor plan before purchasing individual room packages. We can review the number of rooms and zones and discuss whether room-based packages or a wider project appointment is more appropriate.
