A Clear Route From Design to Completion

Some clients want us to create the design and then manage the implementation themselves.

Others prefer Oraanj to take responsibility for the entire process.

Our services are divided into distinct stages so you can clearly understand what we manage, where each service begins and ends, and how much involvement you want from our team.

⸻

1. Interior Design

We establish how your interior should look and function.

This is the creative foundation of the project.

We develop the spatial arrangement, visual direction, palette, materials and overall character of the interior before purchasing or implementation begins.

This may include

* Interior design concept
* Space planning
* Furniture layouts
* Colour palette
* Material and finish direction
* Lighting direction
* Furniture style direction
* 3D visualisation
* Design presentation
* Design development and agreed revisions

Best suited to

Clients who want a professionally resolved interior concept but may wish to manage the later stages independently.

Next: If you want us to select the actual pieces required to create the design, move to Furniture Selection & Shopping List.

⸻

2. Furniture Selection & Shopping List

We select what should be purchased for the room.

Once the design direction is agreed, we identify the actual furniture, decorative lighting, rugs, fabrics and finishing pieces needed to create it.

This may include

* Furniture selection
* Decorative lighting
* Rugs
* Fabrics and soft furnishings
* Accessories
* Finishes and colour options
* Product dimensions
* Supplier information
* Availability considerations
* Lead-time considerations
* Suitable alternatives where required
* Presentation of the recommended selections

What you receive

A clear, considered selection of the products proposed for your interior.

Best suited to

Clients who want Oraanj to handle the research and selection but prefer to place and manage the orders themselves.

Next: If you would rather not manage suppliers and individual orders, you can appoint us for Trade Purchasing & Order Management.

⸻

3. Trade Purchasing & Order Management

We manage the purchasing process for you.

Instead of dealing with multiple suppliers, quotations, orders, lead times and product queries yourself, Oraanj manages the approved purchasing process on your behalf.

Where appropriate, we can purchase through our established supplier and trade network, with applicable trade pricing available on selected products and suppliers.

This may include

* Final product and specification checks
* Checking dimensions against the approved design
* Confirming quantities
* Confirming colours and finishes
* Checking availability
* Reviewing lead times
* Obtaining supplier quotations
* Presenting items for client approval
* Placing approved orders
* Supplier communication
* Monitoring order progress
* Following up manufacturing or preparation
* Managing supplier queries
* Following up delayed orders
* Liaising regarding damaged or incorrect goods
* Coordinating replacement items where required

Client approval

No product is purchased without the required client approval.

Best suited to

Clients who value their time and prefer one point of contact rather than managing multiple suppliers themselves.

Where this service ends

This service manages the purchasing and order process until the goods are ready for delivery.

Physical delivery and property access are managed separately through our Delivery & Access Coordination service.

⸻

4. Delivery & Access Coordination

We manage getting the purchased items from the supplier to your property.

Ordering a piece of furniture and physically getting it into a property are two very different parts of the process.

Large furniture, restricted streets, apartments, lifts, staircases, timed delivery slots and multiple suppliers can all require careful coordination.

This may include

* Coordinating delivery dates
* Planning a delivery schedule
* Coordinating multiple suppliers
* Reviewing property access
* Checking lifts and staircases where required
* Planning access for oversized furniture
* Liaising with concierge or building management
* Arranging suitable delivery slots
* Coordinating delivery communication
* Identifying specialist transport requirements
* Coordinating alternative vehicles where required
* Coordinating storage or consolidated deliveries
* Managing delivery-day communication
* Resolving failed or delayed delivery arrangements

Best suited to

Clients who do not want to personally coordinate multiple deliveries, suppliers, building access arrangements or specialist transport.

Third-party costs

Supplier delivery charges, specialist vehicles, storage, warehousing, parking arrangements, lifting equipment and other external logistics costs are separate from Oraanj’s professional management fee.

Next: Once everything reaches the property, Oraanj can manage installation and final styling.

⸻

5. Installation Management & Styling

We manage the final stage of bringing the interior together.

Once the furniture and products arrive, our team coordinates installation, positioning and final styling so the completed interior reflects the approved design.

This may include

* Coordinating installation dates
* Coordinating furniture assembly
* Coordinating specialist installers
* Furniture positioning
* Checking placement against the approved layout
* Coordinating decorative lighting installation
* Coordinating curtains and blinds where applicable
* Artwork placement
* Accessory placement
* Final styling
* Installation-related snagging
* Identifying missing, damaged or incorrectly installed items
* Final review of the completed space

Best suited to

Clients who want the interior fully coordinated through to completion, rather than managing the final installation themselves.

Third-party costs

The cost of electricians, installers, handymen, joiners, artwork installers, curtain installers and other external trades is separate from Oraanj’s professional management fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to appoint Oraanj for every stage?

No.

You can appoint us for the services that are appropriate for your project.

Some clients require design only, while others ask us to manage everything through purchasing, delivery, installation and construction.

⸻

Can I use the Furniture Selection & Shopping List and purchase the items myself?

Yes.

We can create the furniture and product selection for you, after which you are free to manage the purchasing directly.

⸻

What is the difference between Furniture Selection and Trade Purchasing?

Furniture Selection determines what should be purchased.

Trade Purchasing & Order Management means Oraanj takes responsibility for managing the approved purchasing process and suppliers on your behalf.

⸻

What is the difference between Purchasing and Delivery Coordination?

Purchasing manages the order.

Delivery & Access Coordination manages how the purchased item gets from the supplier to your property.

These are separate stages because supplier ordering and physical property logistics involve different work and responsibilities.

⸻

Are furniture costs included in Oraanj’s professional fee?

No.

Professional fees cover the agreed Oraanj services.

Furniture, lighting, accessories and other products are additional project costs.

⸻

Are delivery-company charges included?

No.

Our Delivery & Access Coordination service covers Oraanj’s professional time and coordination.

The actual cost of couriers, delivery companies, specialist vehicles, storage and other logistics services is paid separately.

⸻

Do you provide your fees online?

Every project is different, particularly where multiple rooms, bespoke pieces, specialist suppliers or construction are involved.

We therefore prepare a tailored fee proposal after understanding your property and requirements.
