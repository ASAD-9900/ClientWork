What Does Design & Build Mean?
Design First. Build From an Agreed Plan.
At Oraanj, Design & Build doesn't mean starting construction and making design decisions as the project progresses.
We develop the interior first.
Layouts, lighting, materials, joinery and other important design decisions are considered before the main renovation begins.
The approved design can then be developed into the information required for implementation.
This creates a clearer basis for costing, procurement and the works on site.

The completed interior is reviewed and prepared for the client.

Design & Build Questions
What does Design & Build mean?
Design & Build brings the design and implementation stages of a project together. At Oraanj, this can include interior design, technical planning, procurement, renovation, project management, installation and handover depending on the agreed scope.
Is Design & Build the same as hiring a builder?
Not exactly. A builder primarily undertakes construction works. A Design & Build service starts with developing the design and then connects it with the wider implementation of the project.
Can Oraanj renovate my whole property?
Yes. Oraanj works on complete residential renovations as well as selected individual areas and commercial interiors.
Can I use Oraanj only for interior design?
Yes. You can commission interior design separately and use your own contractor for the implementation.
When will I know the construction cost?
A meaningful construction cost requires a reasonably defined scope. Developing the design, specifications and relevant technical information first provides a clearer basis for pricing the works.
Do you handle planning permission?
Whether planning permission or another consent is required depends on the property and proposed work. Where relevant, the appropriate requirements and specialist appointments can be identified and coordinated as part of the wider project scope.
Do you work with structural engineers and other consultants?
Where specialist input is required, relevant consultants can be coordinated as part of the wider project subject to the agreed appointments and scope.
Can you manage bespoke joinery and furniture?
Yes. Bespoke joinery, furniture, procurement and installation can be included within a wider Design & Build project.
Do you undertake commercial Design & Build?
Yes. Oraanj works on selected commercial interiors including offices, restaurants, cafés and other business environments.
Where do you provide Design & Build services?
Our principal Design & Build work is across London. Projects elsewhere can be considered depending on location and scope.
