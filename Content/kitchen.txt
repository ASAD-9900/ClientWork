Do You Have Space for an Island?
Don't Force One Into the Room
Kitchen islands are popular, but they aren't right for every space.
Before introducing an island, we consider:
Room dimensions
Circulation around it
Cabinet and appliance doors
Seating
Worktop requirements
Dining-table position
Lighting
Sockets
Plumbing, where relevant
How the kitchen connects to adjoining rooms
Sometimes an island improves the kitchen enormously.
Sometimes a peninsula or a clearer open layout works better.
The floor plan should decide — not the trend.

Kitchen Lighting

A scheme may combine:
GENERAL LIGHTING

TASK LIGHTING
For preparation and work surfaces.
ISLAND LIGHTING

UNDER-CABINET LIGHTING
To illuminate work surfaces where appropriate.
DECORATIVE LIGHTING
Pendants or wall lights that contribute to the wider interior.
EVENING LIGHTING
Softer lighting for dining and entertaining when the kitchen isn't being used for preparation.
Lighting positions should be developed alongside the cabinetry and furniture rather than added after the kitchen has been ordered.

Kitchen Renovation Questions
What does a kitchen renovation include?
It depends on the property and agreed scope. A kitchen renovation may involve layout changes, cabinetry, worktops, appliances, lighting, flooring, plumbing, electrical work, decoration and installation.
Can Oraanj design and renovate my kitchen?
Yes. You can appoint Oraanj for kitchen design or discuss a wider service that continues into renovation and project delivery.
Can you redesign my kitchen layout?
Yes. Layout planning can consider cabinetry, appliances, storage, circulation and how the kitchen relates to dining or living areas.
Can you design an open-plan kitchen and living space?
Yes. We can consider the kitchen, dining and living areas together rather than designing the kitchen in isolation.
Can you design bespoke kitchen cabinetry?
Bespoke cabinetry and other custom joinery can be developed where required as part of the agreed design scope.
Can you help with kitchen lighting?
Yes. Lighting can be developed alongside the cabinetry, furniture and wider interior.
Can I use my own kitchen supplier?
Yes. Oraanj can potentially work alongside your chosen kitchen supplier while developing the wider interior and renovation.
Can I use my own builder?
Yes. You can appoint Oraanj for design and technical support while using your own contractor, subject to agreeing the appropriate scope and responsibilities.
How long does a kitchen renovation take?
It depends on the extent of the renovation, product lead times, bespoke elements, site conditions and whether wider structural or property works are involved.
When should I contact Oraanj?
Ideally before ordering the kitchen or finalising important electrical and plumbing positions. This gives more opportunity to coordinate the layout and wider interior properly.
