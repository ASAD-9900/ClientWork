Furniture Doesn't Come at the End
Procurement Needs to Start Earlier Than Many Clients Expect
Some furniture, lighting and bespoke items can have significant lead times.
Waiting until the renovation is almost complete before choosing them can affect the final programme.
During a turnkey project, procurement planning can consider:
FURNITURE
Sofas, dining tables, beds, chairs and other selected pieces.
DECORATIVE LIGHTING
Pendants, wall lights, table lamps and floor lamps.
BESPOKE FURNITURE
Items being designed or manufactured specifically for the property.
WINDOW TREATMENTS
Curtains, blinds and associated hardware where included.
RUGS
Sizing and positioning considered alongside the furniture layout.
ARTWORK & ACCESSORIES
Where included within the agreed interior scheme.
DELIVERY
Access, storage and the appropriate installation sequence.
The exact procurement responsibility is agreed at the beginning of the project.

Turnkey Interior Design Questions
What is turnkey interior design?
Turnkey interior design is a more fully managed approach that can take an interior from initial design through renovation, procurement, furniture installation and final styling, depending on the agreed scope.
What is the difference between turnkey and Design & Build?
Design & Build primarily connects interior design with the renovation and implementation stages. Turnkey can continue further into furniture procurement, deliveries, installation and styling.
Does turnkey mean everything is included?
Not automatically. The exact responsibilities, items and services included should be agreed in the project scope.
Can Oraanj manage my London property while I live overseas?
Yes. Oraanj works with overseas clients who own properties in London. The exact communication and project-management arrangements are agreed for each project.
Can you furnish the whole property?
Furniture, lighting and other interior items can be sourced and coordinated where they form part of the agreed turnkey scope.
Does turnkey include renovation?
It can. A turnkey project may involve complete renovation, a lighter refurbishment or primarily furnishing and installation depending on the condition of the property.
Can you provide turnkey design for a newly purchased property?
Yes. Newly purchased properties are particularly suitable where the client wants the interior designed, renovated and prepared before moving in.
Do you provide turnkey commercial interiors?
Yes. Selected office, restaurant, café and other commercial projects can be considered for a wider turnkey service.
How much does turnkey interior design cost?
The overall investment depends on the property, renovation requirements, furniture, materials, bespoke work and level of project management required.
When should I appoint a turnkey interior designer?
Ideally before major renovation or procurement begins. Earlier involvement allows the design, technical information, long-lead items and programme to be considered together.
