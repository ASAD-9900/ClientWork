Technical Drawings for Renovation Projects
Coordinate the Interior Before Work Progresses Too Far
Renovation projects can involve several interconnected decisions.
For example:
A bed position can affect sockets and bedside lighting.
A dining table position can affect the pendant location.
A media wall can affect electrical points, television position and storage.
A wardrobe can affect lighting, switches and circulation.
A kitchen layout can influence the adjoining dining and living areas.
Developing these elements together can make the intended interior easier to communicate to the wider project team.
For larger renovation projects, technical interior drawings may form part of Oraanj's Interior Architecture, Design & Technical or Design & Build services.

AutoCAD & Interior Drawing Questions
Can you produce AutoCAD drawings for my interior project?
Yes. Oraanj can produce agreed interior drawings in AutoCAD for residential and commercial projects depending on the information available and scope required.
Do I need a DWG file?
Not necessarily. However, an accurate existing DWG can make it easier to develop additional drawings. If no suitable CAD plan exists, we will need to establish what information or measurement is required first.
Can you create a furniture layout?
Yes. Furniture and space-planning layouts can form part of the drawing service.
Can you draw interior elevations?
Yes. Interior elevations can be produced to communicate agreed wall layouts, joinery, lighting, electrical positions, finishes and other interior elements according to scope.
Can you produce lighting and electrical layouts?
Yes, interior lighting and electrical positioning can be developed where included within the appointment. Specialist electrical design, calculations, installation and certification remain the responsibility of the relevant qualified professionals.
Can you produce bespoke joinery drawings?
Yes. Oraanj can develop interior design drawings for bespoke joinery. Final manufacturing and fabrication details should be confirmed by the appointed joinery manufacturer.
Can my builder use the drawings?
The drawings can communicate the agreed interior design information to your builder and wider project team. Before implementation, the contractor and relevant specialists should review the information applicable to their work and confirm site dimensions and specialist requirements.
Are the drawings suitable for planning permission or Building Regulations?
Not automatically. This service focuses on interior design drawings. Planning, Building Regulations, structural, fire, MEP and other specialist or statutory information may require appropriately qualified professionals depending on the project.
Can you work with my architect's drawings?
Yes. Where suitable project information already exists, Oraanj can work alongside the wider project team and develop the agreed interior information.
Can you provide DWG files?
Yes, where DWG delivery forms part of the agreed appointment. Confirm your required file format when requesting the quotation.
Do you work outside London?
Drawing services may be provided remotely for suitable projects elsewhere in the UK where sufficient and accurate project information is available.
