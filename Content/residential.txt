Whole-Home Interior Design
One Home, Rather Than a Collection of Separate Rooms
Designing a complete property requires a different approach from decorating rooms individually.
Flooring moves from one area into another. Lighting needs to work across the property. Joinery, doors and architectural details repeat. Colours need to connect without every room looking identical.
We develop the home as one overall design while considering how each individual room needs to function.
This is particularly valuable when renovating a newly purchased property or making significant changes across an existing family home.
If required, the approved residential design can then move into our wider Design & Build service for renovation and implementation.

Our Residential Interior Design Process
01 — Consultation
We start by understanding your property, lifestyle, priorities, design preferences and what you would like to change.
02 — Space Planning
Before focusing on finishes, we establish how the rooms should work.
Furniture positions, circulation, storage and relationships between spaces are considered at this stage.
03 — Concept Design
We develop the creative direction through colours, materials, furniture, lighting and reference imagery.
04 — 3D Design & Development
Where included within the service, 3D visuals help you understand the proposed design and refine important decisions.
05 — Technical Design
For more detailed projects, the approved concept can be developed through the required layouts, elevations, lighting, electrical and joinery information.
06 — Furniture & Material Selection
Furniture, lighting, fabrics, finishes and other selected elements are coordinated around the approved design.
07 — Procurement
Where included, selected products can be sourced and ordered in line with the wider project programme.
08 — Implementation
You can use the approved design with your own contractor or ask Oraanj to continue into renovation and project delivery.

Designing London Homes
Understanding the Property Matters
London homes come with very different opportunities and constraints.
Period Houses
Victorian, Edwardian and other period properties can have beautiful proportions and original features but layouts that no longer suit modern family life.
Apartments
Apartment design often depends on making intelligent use of space, improving storage and working around fixed services or building-management requirements.
Family Homes
Family homes need to balance shared spaces with privacy, storage, work, study and entertaining.
Prime London Properties
Larger residences often require consistency across several rooms alongside detailed lighting, joinery, furniture and material coordination.
Our approach isn't to impose the same visual style on every property. We understand the architecture and the people living there before developing the design.

Frequently Asked Questions
What does a residential interior designer do?
A residential interior designer plans and develops the interior of a home around the people living there. This can include layouts, colours, materials, furniture, lighting, bespoke joinery and technical design depending on the project.
How much does residential interior design cost in London?
The cost depends on the property, number of rooms and level of service required. A single-room concept and a complete home design involving technical information and procurement require very different levels of work.
Can Oraanj design just one room?
Yes. Residential projects can range from individual rooms to complete homes.
Do you design flats and apartments?
Yes. We design London apartments as well as houses, period properties, family homes and larger residences.
Can you work with furniture I already own?
Yes. Existing furniture, artwork or other pieces can be considered within the new design where you would like to retain them.
Can you design bespoke wardrobes and storage?
Yes. Bespoke joinery can form part of the design and may include wardrobes, media units, shelving, cabinetry and other built-in storage.
Do you design kitchens and bathrooms?
Yes. Kitchens and bathrooms can be designed individually or as part of a wider residential interior project.
Do I need an interior designer before renovating?
For substantial renovations, involving a designer before work begins can help establish layouts, lighting, materials, joinery and other important decisions before they reach site.
Can Oraanj manage the renovation as well?
Yes. Clients who want support beyond the design stage can discuss our Design & Build, renovation and turnkey services.
Which areas of London do you work in?
We work across London on residential projects. Our Areas We Cover section provides further information about specific locations.
