Before You Design a Restaurant, Get These Five Decisions Right
Restaurant design starts long before choosing colours, chairs or pendant lights.
These five decisions influence almost everything that follows.
01 — Who Is Your Customer?
A neighbourhood café, destination restaurant, cocktail bar and quick-service concept need very different environments.
We look at who you want to attract, how long customers are likely to stay and the experience you want them to remember.
02 — How Many Covers Do You Need?
More tables don't automatically create a better restaurant.
The right layout needs to balance seating capacity with circulation, comfort, service and the character of the venue.
03 — How Will Staff Move?
Routes between dining areas, service points, bars and back-of-house spaces need to be considered early.
A restaurant can look excellent but become difficult to operate if these relationships haven't been properly planned.
04 — What Should the Restaurant Feel Like?
Relaxed? Energetic? Intimate? Refined? Fast-moving?
Lighting, materials, acoustics, furniture spacing and visual character all contribute to that experience.
05 — What Is the Project Budget?
The design needs to respond to the commercial reality of the project.
Understanding the intended budget early helps us prioritise where investment matters most and develop the design accordingly.

Designing the Guest Journey
Restaurant design is experienced as a sequence.
ARRIVE
What does the customer see first?
The frontage, entrance and immediate view into the restaurant begin shaping expectations before they sit down.
ENTER
Is it obvious where to go?
Depending on the concept, the customer may need to understand where to wait, order, speak to a host or move towards the dining area.
SIT
The relationship between tables, chairs, booths and surrounding circulation affects both comfort and atmosphere.
EXPERIENCE
Lighting, materials, sound, views, furniture and the relationship between different zones influence how the restaurant feels while guests are there.
LEAVE
Even the final moments matter.
A coherent restaurant experience should feel considered from arrival through to departure.

How Much Does Restaurant Interior Design Cost in London?
There isn't one meaningful price per room for a restaurant.
A 600 sq ft neighbourhood café and a 4,000 sq ft restaurant with a bar, bespoke joinery and substantial fit-out requirements are completely different projects.
The main cost drivers can include:
Size of the premises
Larger spaces generally require more design and potentially more physical work.
Condition of the property
An existing restaurant may require less intervention than an empty shell or premises being converted from another use.
Level of fit-out
Changes to floors, ceilings, partitions, lighting, washrooms and services can significantly affect the wider project budget.
Bespoke joinery
Bars, counters, banquettes and specialist furniture can represent an important part of restaurant investment.
Furniture & finishes
The quantity, specification and sourcing route all influence cost.
Specialist requirements
Commercial kitchens, extraction, acoustics, fire strategy and other technical requirements may require specialist professional input.
Once we understand the premises, concept and intended scope, we can establish the appropriate design service and next steps.

Questions Restaurant Owners Ask Us
How many covers can I fit into my restaurant?
That depends on the floor area, furniture dimensions, circulation, service requirements, accessibility and the type of dining experience you're creating. Space planning should test these relationships rather than simply fitting the maximum number of tables onto a plan.
Should I appoint a restaurant designer before a contractor?
Ideally, important layout and design decisions should be developed before the main fit-out begins. This gives the contractor clearer information and allows more decisions to be considered before reaching site.
Can you redesign an existing restaurant?
Yes. We can work with existing venues as well as new restaurant spaces.
Do you design cafés and bars?
Yes. Oraanj works across restaurants, cafés, bars and other hospitality interiors.
Can you design the bar and bespoke seating?
Yes. Bespoke joinery can include counters, bars, banquettes, display units and other made-to-measure elements depending on the project.
Can Oraanj manage the fit-out?
Yes. Where required, our wider Design & Build service can continue beyond interior design into the agreed fit-out, procurement and project-management stages.
Do you design commercial kitchens and extraction systems?
We consider the relationship between the restaurant interior and these specialist requirements, but detailed commercial kitchen, extraction and specialist engineering design should be undertaken by the relevant qualified specialists.
How long does a restaurant project take?
It depends on the size and condition of the premises, the scope of design and fit-out, required approvals, specialist works, bespoke manufacturing and procurement lead times. A programme can be discussed once the project requirements are understood.
