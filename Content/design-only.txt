What Does Design-Only Mean?
We Design It. You Decide How It Is Delivered.
Design-Only means Oraanj develops the agreed interior design without requiring you to appoint us for the subsequent renovation or installation.
Once your design is complete, you can:
Buy the furniture yourself

Appoint your own builder

This gives you professional design direction without committing to complete project delivery from the beginning.

One Design — When Is It Enough?
Best for Straightforward Rooms
The £600 One Design option can work well when:
The room layout is relatively simple
You want one strong visual direction
Major renovation isn't required
There is little or no bespoke joinery
You don't need extensive technical information
You are comfortable implementing the design yourself
For example, it could suit a bedroom where the main decisions are the bed, bedside furniture, colours, lighting and overall appearance.

Unlimited Design — Why Choose It?
Useful When You Want to Explore the Room Properly
Sometimes the first idea leads to another.
You may want to see:
A different sofa
Another colour direction
A different TV wall
A different bed
Alternative furniture positions
Different lighting
A simpler design
A more detailed design
Unlimited Design gives you greater flexibility to refine the visual design before approving the final direction.
The revisions apply to the agreed room and design scope rather than changing the project into a different room or service.

Design & Technical — When Do You Need More Detail?
When the Design Needs to Move Towards Construction
A 3D image shows what the room should look like.
It doesn't necessarily give a contractor all the information needed to build every element.
For rooms involving more detailed work, additional interior information may be needed.
Depending on the agreed scope, this could include:
Space planning
Wall elevations
Lighting positions
Electrical position intent
Joinery elevations
Relevant dimensions
Material references
Interior design notes
The exact drawings required depend on what is changing within the room.
Where specialist architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical or other regulated design is required, this should be undertaken or confirmed by the appropriate qualified professional.

Design Package Questions
What is the difference between £600 and £900?
The £600 One Design package provides one final 3D design with one round of revisions. The £900 Unlimited Design package allows more flexibility to refine the agreed room design through unlimited design revisions.
Which package should I choose for a bedroom?
For a straightforward bedroom where you mainly need visual direction, One Design may be enough. If you want greater flexibility to explore and refine the design, Unlimited Design may be more suitable.
Which package should I choose for a living room?
It depends on the complexity of the room and how much flexibility you want. Many living rooms involve several connected decisions around furniture, TV position, lighting, storage and materials, so Unlimited Design can provide more opportunity to refine them.
When should I choose Design & Technical?
Choose Design & Technical when the room involves more detailed renovation, joinery, lighting or contractor coordination and you need more than a visual design.
Can I use my own builder?
Yes. Design-Only does not require you to use Oraanj for construction.
Can I buy the furniture myself?
Yes. You can use the final design as a guide and purchase the furniture independently.
Do you provide product links?
Product sourcing is a separate service unless specifically included within your agreed package. If you need help finding particular furniture and lighting, you can add Furniture Sourcing.
Can I upgrade later?
Yes. Depending on the project, you can discuss additional sourcing, procurement, technical design, renovation, Design & Build or Turnkey support later.
Does £1,500 include building work?
No. Design & Technical is a design service. The £1,500 per-room fee does not include construction, materials or contractor labour.

One important change: rename the £1,500 package
The current page has already improved it from “Design & Build Pack” to “Ready to Build Pack”, which is better.

Design & Technical — £1,500 per room

Define “unlimited”
This is important commercially.
Your current £900 and £1,500 packages say Unlimited Changes/Unlimited revisions.
Keep it if this is a strong selling point, but your terms should clarify that unlimited revisions apply to the agreed room and agreed brief, rather than allowing an endlessly changing scope.
For example, if a client starts with:
Contemporary living room
and after eight revisions says:
Actually, let's make it a home cinema with full bespoke joinery and change the adjoining dining room too.
That isn't simply a revision.
So on the public page I'd use:
Unlimited design revisions within the agreed room and design scope.
Much safer.
