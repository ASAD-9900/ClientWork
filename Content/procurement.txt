Sourcing vs Procurement
What's the Difference?
These services are related, but they solve different problems.

What should we buy?
Sourcing focuses on identifying suitable furniture, lighting, fabrics, finishes and accessories for the interior.
The designer researches and recommends products that work with the design, property, budget and brief.

FF&E & Procurement
How do we get everything ordered and delivered?
Procurement begins once products and specifications have been approved.
It focuses on quotations, orders, suppliers, lead times, tracking and delivery coordination.

FF&E & Procurement Questions
What does FF&E stand for?
FF&E stands for Furniture, Fixtures and Equipment. Within an interior project, it generally refers to the furniture and other agreed items required to furnish and complete the space.
What is the difference between sourcing and procurement?
Sourcing is primarily about identifying and selecting what to buy. Procurement is about managing approved purchases, including quotations, orders, suppliers, lead times and agreed delivery coordination.
Can I use procurement without a full interior design service?
Potentially, yes. If you already have sufficiently detailed and approved selections, we can review the information and establish whether standalone procurement is appropriate.
Can Oraanj source the furniture as well?
Yes. If products have not yet been selected, our Furniture Sourcing or wider Interior Design service may be required before procurement.
Can you procure furniture for an entire house?
Yes. Procurement can be particularly useful for whole-home furnishing projects involving multiple rooms and suppliers.
Can you procure lighting?
Decorative lighting can be included within the agreed FF&E procurement scope. Technical electrical design and installation remain separate specialist responsibilities.
Can you manage bespoke furniture?
Bespoke furniture can form part of the wider project where included within the agreed design and procurement scope.
Do you work with trade suppliers?
Yes. Depending on the project, Oraanj works with trade suppliers, manufacturers, specialist makers and selected retail suppliers.
Will I receive trade discounts?
Trade pricing and discounts vary by supplier and product. Any applicable commercial arrangement should be confirmed as part of the agreed procurement service rather than assumed.
Do you manage deliveries?
Delivery coordination can be included within the agreed procurement scope. The exact responsibilities depend on the project, suppliers, site access and appointment.
Do you install the furniture?
Installation coordination can form part of the wider service where agreed. Requirements vary depending on the products and project.
Can procurement be coordinated with my renovation?
Yes. Where Oraanj is involved in the wider project, agreed procurement can be coordinated with the renovation programme.
Do you provide procurement for commercial projects?
Yes. Procurement can support selected office, restaurant, café, bar, retail and other commercial interior projects depending on scope.
