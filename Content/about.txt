About Oraanj Interior Design
Designing Interiors Around People, Property and the Way They Live
Oraanj is an award-winning interior design studio in London, creating residential and commercial interiors across the UK and internationally.
Founded by Rachana Gupta, the studio brings together interior design, interior architecture, technical design, bespoke joinery, procurement and project delivery.
For more than 20 years, our approach has remained simple: understand the client, understand the property and create an interior that works beautifully for both.

Our Story
More Than 20 Years of Designing Interiors
Oraanj was founded with the belief that good interior design should be personal.
A home shouldn't feel like a copy of somebody else's home. A restaurant shouldn't simply follow the latest trend. An office shouldn't look impressive but fail the people working in it.
We start by listening.
How do you live?
What isn't working in the property?
What do you want to change?
What should the space feel like?
What matters most to you?
From there, we develop the layout, materials, lighting, joinery, furniture and details around the individual project.
Over the years, our work has grown from interior design into a wider studio offering interior architecture, technical design, renovation, procurement and project delivery.
Today, Oraanj works across homes, workplaces and hospitality interiors, while keeping the same personal approach that shaped the studio from the beginning.

Meet Rachana Gupta
Founder & CEO of Oraanj
Rachana Gupta is the Founder and CEO of Oraanj Interior Design.
With more than 20 years of experience in interior design, Rachana has worked across residential, commercial, workplace and hospitality interiors in London, the UK and internationally.
Her approach begins with understanding the client rather than imposing a particular design style.
She believes the best interiors come from getting the practical decisions right first — how the space flows, how it will be used, where furniture belongs and what the property itself needs — before developing the materials and details that give it character.
Rachana remains closely involved in the creative direction of Oraanj and its projects, working with the wider team from the early design stages through to the finished interior.
Her work has received industry recognition, including Interior Designer of the Year at the Eastern Eye Property Awards 2025.

We Don't Design Every Interior to Look the Same
Your Property Should Still Feel Like Yours
We don't begin a project with a fixed Oraanj style.
We begin with you.
Some clients want something calm and understated.
Others want colour, pattern and personality.
A period property may call for a completely different response from a contemporary apartment. A restaurant has different priorities from a family home. An office needs to work differently again.
Our role is to understand those differences and create an interior that feels right for the property and the people using it.
That means listening first, questioning where necessary and making design decisions for a reason rather than simply following what is fashionable.

Design Is Only Part of the Story
We Think About How It Will Actually Be Delivered
A design can look beautiful on screen and still be difficult to implement.
That's why our work considers the practical side of the interior alongside the creative one.
Space planning affects furniture.
Furniture affects lighting.
Lighting affects electrical positions.
Joinery affects walls, sockets and surrounding finishes.
Materials affect how different surfaces meet.
Kitchens and bathrooms involve several decisions that need to work together.
By considering these relationships earlier, the design becomes more useful when the project moves from drawings into the real property.
For clients who want wider support, Oraanj can continue beyond the design stage into renovation, procurement, project management and installation.

How We Work Together
Design Is Collaborative
Good interiors aren't created in isolation.
Our projects involve conversations between clients, designers, technical specialists, suppliers, craftspeople and the people responsible for implementation.
The designer may establish the idea.
The technical team works through the detail.
A joinery specialist considers how something should be made.
A supplier helps resolve the right material or product.
The project team considers how it fits into the wider programme.
Each person sees the project from a slightly different perspective.
Our role is to keep those decisions connected to the original design and the client's priorities.

London Is Home
But Our Clients Aren't Always Based Here
Oraanj's studio is based in London, and much of our work involves London homes and commercial properties.
Some of the people who own those properties, however, live elsewhere.
We work with overseas clients who need a design team in London to help develop and coordinate their property while they are away.
