Renovating a London Apartment?
Check the Building Requirements Early
Many London apartment projects involve more than the interior design itself.
Depending on the property and proposed work, you may need to consider requirements relating to:
Freeholder or landlord consent
Managing-agent procedures
Contractor access
Working hours
Lift protection
Deliveries
Waste removal
Noise
Flooring
Insurance documentation
Water shut-offs
Alterations to services
The requirements vary considerably between buildings.
Where these affect the interior project, Oraanj can help coordinate the design information with the relevant project team and building-management process according to our agreed scope.

Renovating a Period or Listed Property?
Understand the Property Before Changing It
Some London properties may be listed or located within conservation areas.
This does not automatically mean every internal change requires consent, but certain works can involve additional restrictions or approvals depending on the property and proposal.
Where specialist planning, heritage, structural or statutory advice is required, this should be provided by the appropriate qualified consultant or authority.
Our role is to develop the interior design with the property's architecture and known project requirements in mind.
