What Is Space Planning in Interior Design?
Space planning is the process of deciding how furniture, circulation and different activities should be arranged within a room or property.
A professional space plan considers the dimensions of the room alongside furniture size, walking routes, doors, windows, storage and how the space needs to be used.
It can help answer practical questions such as:
Where should the sofa and television go?
What size dining table will fit comfortably?
Can the room accommodate living, dining and working?
Is there enough space around the furniture?
Where could additional storage be introduced?
Will the furniture I want to buy actually fit?
Could the existing layout work better without increasing the size of the property?
The aim is simple: work out how the space should function before you invest in the wider design.

Space Planning for London Properties
London properties can present very different layout challenges.
Period homes may have narrow rooms, alcoves, bay windows and later extensions.
Apartments may need several functions to work within a relatively compact footprint.
Large open-plan spaces can have the opposite problem — plenty of floor area but no obvious way to organise the furniture.
Our approach starts with the property you actually have and how you want to use it.
Rather than immediately changing walls or increasing the size of a property, we first consider whether the existing space could simply work harder.

Space Planning FAQs
What is space planning in interior design?
Space planning is the process of organising furniture, circulation and functional areas within a room or property. It helps establish how the available space should work before progressing into detailed interior design or purchasing furniture.
How much does space planning cost in London?
The cost depends on the size of the property, number of rooms, information already available and level of layout development required.
Send us your existing floor plan and requirements and we can advise on the appropriate scope and fee.
Can I book space planning for one room?
Yes. Space planning can be used for an individual room, open-plan area, complete floor or wider property.
Can you help me decide where furniture should go?
Yes. Furniture positioning, scale and circulation are central to space planning.
Can you tell me what size sofa or dining table will fit?
Space planning can help establish appropriate furniture dimensions and clearances based on accurate room measurements.
Can I send you an existing floor plan?
Yes. Architect's, developer's and suitable existing property plans can be used where they provide the information required for the project.
Do you need to visit my property?
Not always. Some space planning projects can be developed using accurate plans, measurements, photographs and video. Where additional site information is required, we can discuss the appropriate next step.
Is space planning the same as interior design?
No. Space planning focuses primarily on layout, furniture positioning, circulation and how the room functions. Interior design develops the wider appearance and character of the space.
Is space planning the same as interior architecture?
No. Space planning can focus on furniture and functional layouts. Interior architecture is more appropriate where greater reconfiguration and technical interior development are required.
Can space planning help before a renovation?
Yes. Resolving furniture layouts and how different areas will be used before renovation can help inform later decisions around lighting, electrical positions, joinery and the wider interior design.
Do you provide commercial space planning?
Yes. Space planning can also form part of office, retail and hospitality interior design projects.
