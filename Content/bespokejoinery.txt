Why Choose Bespoke?
When Standard Furniture Doesn't Solve the Problem
Off-the-shelf furniture is made to standard dimensions.
Your property isn't.
An awkward alcove, sloping ceiling, period wall, narrow bedroom or unusually shaped room can leave valuable space unused.
Bespoke joinery allows the furniture to respond to the property.
It can help you:
Use the full height of a room
Make use of awkward corners
Build around architectural features
Create storage where standard furniture won't fit
Hide cables and equipment
Combine storage and display
Coordinate furniture with the wider interior
Choose the internal layout you actually need
The result should not simply be furniture that fits the wall.
It should fit the way you use the room.

Start With What Needs to Fit Inside
Good Storage Is Designed From the Inside Out
Before deciding what wardrobe doors should look like, we need to understand what the wardrobe needs to hold.
For example:
How much long hanging do you need?
How many shirts and jackets?
Do you fold knitwear?
How many pairs of shoes?
Do you need drawers for jewellery or accessories?
Should luggage be stored above?
Do you need a dressing table?
Should anything be hidden?
Would internal lighting help?
The answers determine the internal configuration.
Only then do we develop the exterior proportions, doors, handles, materials and finishes.
This approach applies to more than wardrobes.
A media wall starts with the television and equipment.
A home office starts with the way you work.
A library starts with what you want to store and display.

Bespoke Joinery Questions
What is bespoke joinery?
Bespoke joinery is furniture or cabinetry designed and made specifically for a particular space rather than manufactured to standard dimensions.
What types of bespoke joinery can Oraanj design?
Projects can include fitted wardrobes, dressing rooms, media walls, alcove units, home offices, shelving, storage, cabinetry and selected commercial joinery.
Can you design the inside of my wardrobe as well?
Yes. Internal storage planning is an important part of wardrobe and dressing-room design.
Can lighting be integrated into bespoke joinery?
Yes, where appropriate. Lighting and the required electrical provisions should be considered during the design stage rather than after the joinery has been manufactured.
Can Oraanj design a media wall around my television?
Yes. The design can consider television dimensions, storage, display, cabling, equipment, ventilation and lighting requirements.
Can you design a fitted home office?
Yes. Bespoke home offices can incorporate desks, shelving, storage, equipment, power requirements and cable management.
Can Oraanj design the joinery if I already have my own carpenter?
Potentially, yes. The required level of drawings and responsibility should be agreed before the work begins.
Do you arrange fabrication and installation?
Oraanj can discuss fabrication and installation coordination as part of the wider bespoke joinery service, depending on the project.
When should bespoke joinery be designed during a renovation?
Ideally before related electrical, lighting and finishing decisions are finalised, particularly where the joinery includes power, lighting or built-in equipment.
How do I start?
Send us photographs, approximate dimensions or floor plans, what you want to create and any inspiration images you have.
