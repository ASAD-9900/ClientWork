Covent Garden Hospitality Design

A West End cocktail bar planned with classic club proportions

Located in Covent Garden, this destination bar and lounge draws on the proportions and material discipline of traditional London members' clubs. The challenge was to balance an intimate, atmospheric drinking environment with the high footfall and operational demands of the West End theatre district.

We organised the space into a sequence of distinct zones: a lively front bar for walk-in guests, deep buttoned leather booths for table service, and a discreet rear lounge for private bookings. Rich walnut joinery, honed Verde Guatemala marble counters, and antiqued brass detailing were selected to withstand constant commercial service while developing an authentic patina over time.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Bar Operations, Acoustic Management & Traditional Joinery

Carefully detailed for efficient bartender ergonomics, comfortable guest dwell times, and robust commercial performance.

The bar counter was engineered as the operational heart of the venue, incorporating twin barista and mixology stations, sunken ice wells, and overhead glass gantries crafted from blackened steel and brass. To tame reverberant noise from hard stone surfaces, we integrated acoustic absorption behind fabric-wrapped ceiling coffers and deep velvet wall panels. Individual brass reading lamps and discreet floor-level lighting establish clear walking pathways without disturbing the low-lit ambiance of the seating booths.
