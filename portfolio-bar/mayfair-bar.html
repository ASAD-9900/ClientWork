Covent Garden Hospitality Design

A West End cocktail bar planned with classic club proportions

Located in Covent Garden, this destination bar and lounge draws on the proportions and material discipline of traditional London members' clubs. The challenge was to balance an intimate, atmospheric drinking environment with the high footfall and operational demands of the West End theatre district.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to bar interior designer, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

We organised the space into a sequence of distinct zones: a lively front bar for walk-in guests, deep buttoned leather booths for table service, and a discreet rear lounge for private bookings. Rich walnut joinery, honed Verde Guatemala marble counters, and antiqued brass detailing were selected to withstand constant commercial service while developing an authentic patina over time.

Our team delivered tailored Kensington lounge bar project, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Bar Operations, Acoustic Management & Traditional Joinery

Carefully detailed for efficient bartender ergonomics, comfortable guest dwell times, and robust commercial performance.

The bar counter was engineered as the operational heart of the venue, incorporating twin barista and mixology stations, sunken ice wells, and overhead glass gantries crafted from blackened steel and brass. To tame reverberant noise from hard stone surfaces, we integrated acoustic absorption behind fabric-wrapped ceiling coffers and deep velvet wall panels. Individual brass reading lamps and discreet floor-level lighting establish clear walking pathways without disturbing the low-lit ambiance of the seating booths.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated bespoke joinery design London, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

Client Requirements, Design Challenges & Indicative Investment

Every project undertaken by Oraanj is defined by a tailored architectural brief, rigorous site problem-solving, and transparent, realistic financial planning.

01 Client Requirements

Theatrical Glamour & High-Capacity Hospitality Venue

A vibrant West End cocktail destination demanding theatrical lighting, rich jewel-toned velvets, and durable commercial finishes built to handle hundreds of theatre-goers nightly.

  • Dramatic curved bar with fluted timber facing, brushed bronze trims, and polished quartzite countertops.
  • Multi-level tiered seating layout providing sightlines across the lounge and central stage area.
  • Smart automated lighting scenes shifting from vibrant pre-theatre brightness to atmospheric late-night glow.
02 Challenges & Solutions

Rapid Pre-Theatre Rush Ergonomics & Heavy Floor Wear

Designing for intense 60-minute pre-theatre drink rushes where 150+ patrons arrive simultaneously, requiring rapid service flow and spill-resistant surfaces.

  • Twin independent cocktail stations with integrated speed rails, glass rinsers, and rapid ice wells.
  • R11 slip-resistant porcelain tile flooring with stain-proof epoxy grouting impervious to citrus and alcohol spills.
  • High-durability upholstery tested to 100,000 Martindale rubs with wipe-clean stain repellent protection.
