Hampstead Hospitality Design

A contemporary cocktail lounge combining natural timber and living greenery

This hospitality venue in East London was designed to offer an inviting, tactile alternative to conventional industrial bar schemes. We planned the open warehouse floor plate around a central horseshoe bar that optimises customer circulation, providing 360-degree service access while anchoring the room visually.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to bar interior designer, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

The material palette pairs natural lime-washed plaster walls with sustainably sourced reclaimed timber, woven cane details, and integrated biophilic planting. Curved banquette booths follow the perimeter walls, upholstered in hardwearing linen-blend fabrics treated with stain-resistant coatings for easy maintenance during busy weekend nights.

Our team delivered tailored lounge lighting design, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Circulation Planning, Biophilic Integration & Material Longevity

Balancing open social energy with intimate seating pockets and commercial durability.

A key consideration was managing the venue's natural light transition from daytime brunch into late evening service. We installed large woven pendants fitted with warm 2000K filament LEDs, supplemented by low-level joinery uplights that highlight the textured lime plaster. Overhead planter boxes with integrated drip-irrigation house hanging ivy and ferns, acting as natural acoustic baffles that absorb sound across the high-ceilinged space.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated Covent Garden lounge bar project, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Lighting Strategy

Custom Woven Luminaires

Large-scale woven rattan pendants and concealed joinery LED strips calibrated to provide flattering low-level lighting throughout evening service.

02 Acoustic Control

Plasterwork & Greenery

Textured acoustic plaster ceiling finishes and lush integrated planting that significantly reduce echo and sound reverberation across the bar.

03 Operational Flow

Horseshoe Island Bar

A central service island with dual pour stations, undercounter bottle coolers, and integrated waste chutes configured for rapid bartender efficiency.
