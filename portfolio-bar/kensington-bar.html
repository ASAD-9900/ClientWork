Kensington Hospitality Design

An intimate neighbourhood lounge planned for day-to-night hospitality

Designed for a prominent site in Kensington, this boutique cocktail lounge and bar was planned to accommodate both quiet daytime coffee and brisk evening cocktail service. We configured the floor plate around a central curved bar counter, ensuring swift staff circulation and clear service routes whilst framing semi-private seating alcoves along the perimeter walls.

The property was developed through our comprehensive approach to bar interior designer, aligning architectural flow with bespoke finishes.

Fluted oak wall panelling and terracotta acoustic plasterwork soften the room’s acoustics during busy service hours. Hardwearing leather banquettes, honed granite bar tops, and brushed brass footrails were chosen for their durability under heavy commercial use, patinating handsomely as the venue matures.

Our team delivered tailored interior design in Kensington and Chelsea, creating effortless circulation and balanced proportions.

Design Narrative & Detailing

Service Flow, Acoustic Control & Architectural Illumination

Engineered to deliver high operational efficiency for staff alongside comfortable, layered seating for guests.

In hospitality venues, layout dictates commercial success. We designed the under-bar stations with commercial refrigeration, dual cocktail speed rails, and integrated glasswashers to reduce server travel time during peak trading. Lighting is controlled via a four-scene architectural dimmer, transitioning smoothly from natural daylight levels at midday to warm, 2200K low-level illumination by evening, accented by wireless rechargeable table lamps at every booth.

Every bespoke element was realised through dedicated lounge lighting design, blending durable craftsmanship with refined tactile textures.

01 Lighting Design

Layered Dimming Circuits

Low-glare downlights, perimeter joinery LED ribbons, and rechargeable brass table lamps calibrated to create an intimate atmosphere after dark.

02 Joinery & Acoustics

Fluted Timber & Plasterwork

Custom fluted oak wall panelling backed with sound-absorbent acoustic insulation to manage ambient sound levels during high-capacity evenings.

03 Seating Configuration

Custom Leather Banquettes

Deep banquette booths upholstered in commercial-grade saddle leather with fluted plinths, designed for durability, comfort, and efficient cover turnover.
